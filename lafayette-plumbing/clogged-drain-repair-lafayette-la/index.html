    Kitchen sinks clog from grease that cools and hardens on the pipe wall. Food and soap stick to it.     Bathroom Drain Clog Clearing in Lafayette, LA

    Hair, soap scum and toothpaste bind together into a mat that sits on the pop-up stopper. That is where most bath sink clogs start. We pull the stopper and clean it, then clear the trap and cable the drain arm if the flow has not come back. We can suggest a hair catcher.

    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Lafayette, LA

    Vanity drains clog at the pop-up stopper first. The pivot rod catches hair. Mineral crust can narrow the tailpiece too.
