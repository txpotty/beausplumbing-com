    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Lafayette

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. That saves some labor. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Lafayette, LA

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. A swap in an existing pit is the low end. Backups add cost. Your written quote shows each option and its price.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Lafayette, LA

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. We carry common pumps on the truck. Most swaps take about two hours. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Lafayette

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Lafayette, LA

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Lafayette, LA

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Lafayette, LA

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Lafayette, LA

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.
