    Pressure Tank Replacement Choices in Lafayette, LA

    Well-Rite Pressure Tank Installation in Lafayette, LA

    Well-Rite tanks use a replaceable diaphragm design in many models. We install them in the common residential sizes. Like any tank, the key is setting pre-charge before it fills, and checking that drawdown matches the pump. A good tank installed badly fails early.

    Commercial Well Pressure Tank Services in Lafayette, LA

    Commercial wells often run several tanks in parallel. Some run four or more on a manifold. We check each tank's pre-charge separately, since one bad tank in a bank can still cause short cycling, while planning the work so the building keeps water. Larger tanks need two people.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Lafayette

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. The connection height and size usually match. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, because the old tank may have been too small all along. Check before swapping.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Lafayette, LA

    Diaphragm tanks use a flexible sheet clamped inside the shell. The diaphragm splits air from water. When it fails, symptoms match a bladder tank, with short cycling, pressure swings and water spitting from the air valve. Replacement is the usual answer.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Lafayette

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. They will not rust. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, plus they are easier to move into cramped utility rooms. Same plumbing as steel.

    Pressure Tanks for Well Houses and Barns in Lafayette, LA

    Tanks in outbuildings face heat, cold and damp. Moisture eats steel tanks from the bottom. We often recommend a composite tank in those spaces, raise it off the floor on a stand, and insulate exposed piping and the switch when the building sees freezing weather. Simple protection.

    Same-Day Tank Swaps in Lafayette

    We keep common tank sizes on our trucks. That means a call in the morning can often end with a new tank installed by afternoon, including new tank tee, gauge and relief valve, without waiting on a parts order. Bigger tanks may take a day to order.

    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Lafayette

    Leaks at the tank tee, a gauge or a fitting can be fixed. Once the shell leaks, it is done. Bladder tanks with a fixed bladder cannot be rebuilt, and a pinhole in the shell means replacement, while a leaking tee or relief valve is a simple part swap we can usually do in under an hour.
