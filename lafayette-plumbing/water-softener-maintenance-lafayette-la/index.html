    Routine Water Softener Tune-Ups in Lafayette

    Our routine tune-ups involve a thorough inspection of your entire water softener system. We check for proper salt levels, inspect the brine tank for salt bridges and confirm the resin bed is working effectively. This preventative service is vital wherever hard water minerals can quickly affect system performance if not regularly addressed.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Lafayette, LA

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their effectiveness. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for maximum softening capacity. We make sure your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Inspection in Lafayette, LA

    Over time, sludge and insoluble materials can build up in your brine tank, hindering the softening process. Our maintenance includes thoroughly cleaning the brine tank, inspecting for any cracks or leaks and ensuring the float valve operates correctly. This step is crucial for the efficient regeneration of your water softener in Lafayette, LA.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Lafayette, LA

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.

    Preventative Maintenance for All Brands in Lafayette, LA

    Whether you have a Fleck, Culligan, Kenmore, GE or EcoPure system, our technicians are trained to service all major water softener brands. Our preventive maintenance protocols are adapted to the specific requirements of each manufacturer, so your unit receives the best possible care, regardless of its make or model in Lafayette, LA.

    Maintenance Versus New Softener Costs in Lafayette, LA

    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Lafayette, LA

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without squandering salt or water.
