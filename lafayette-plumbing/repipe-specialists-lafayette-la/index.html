    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Lafayette

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Lafayette, LA

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average home often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper typically costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 including access and restoration. After two or three leaks, a repipe often pays for itself. We give you a side-by-side comparison so you can decide.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Lafayette, LA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Lafayette, LA. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Lafayette, LA

    Many older homes in Lafayette, LA still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This leads to low water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. Our team specializes in the complete removal and replacement of these outdated systems with modern PEX or copper piping. This upgrade not only resolves existing issues but also greatly improves water quality and flow, boosting your home's value and plumbing reliability for decades to come.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Lafayette

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Homes are commonly built on slabs or raised pier foundations, and raised homes keep much of the plumbing easier to reach. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Lafayette, LA

    Repiping requires small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures. Our crews cut clean openings, protect surrounding areas and patch every hole when the plumbing is done. We match common textures like knockdown and orange peel. Your walls are ready for paint when we finish.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Lafayette

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us what you have.
