    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Lafayette, LA

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some. When the auger finds nothing and the bowl still fills, the blockage is further down the drain or in the main line. We run a cable from the cleanout or put a camera in.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Repair in Lafayette

    Restaurant restrooms get the hardest use we see, and wall-hung bowls there can loosen on their carriers until the seal leaks behind the wall. We tighten or rebuild the carrier and reseal the bowl. That one needs care. A loose wall-hung bowl can crack the china if it is ignored.

    Toilet Brands We Service in Lafayette

    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Lafayette, LA

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Lafayette

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. Before replacing parts, we test the sensor and the solenoid. Batteries are the cheap fix. Ghost flushing on a sensor valve usually means the eye needs adjusting or cleaning.

    Toilet Flush Repair in Lafayette, LA

    Two or three tries to get a full flush points to a flapper closing too early, a chain hooked in the wrong hole, scale in the rim jets, or a cracked overflow tube letting water bypass the bowl. You can usually see which one with the lid off. Our plumber watches a complete flush with the tank lid off and replace the flush valve if the overflow tube is cracked, since it cannot be patched.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Lafayette

    Food coloring in the tank is the cheapest test we run. Should the bowl turn color within fifteen minutes without a flush, water is leaking past the flapper or the flush valve seat, and the toilet is quietly refilling itself all day and night. Most of the time a new flapper does it, unless the seat under it is pitted or cracked.
