    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Lafayette, LA

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Lafayette, LA homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current.     Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Lafayette, LA

    Businesses in Lafayette, LA with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank. One unit can fail and the rest still carry the load. We size the rack by peak demand. Expect a bigger gas line and a mechanical permit on commercial jobs.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Lafayette, LA

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and parts are easy to get. Their condensing and non-condensing lines both fit different homes, depending on the vent route and the budget you are working with. The non-condensing ones need stainless vent pipe. Condensing units vent in PVC and drain condensate.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Lafayette

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and several models come with a recirculation pump built in. The built-in pump can move hot water toward a distant bath on a timer, cutting the wait at the tap without a separate pump. Venting is usually two-inch or three-inch PVC. There is a condensate line to route as well.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Lafayette, LA

    Replacing an old tankless unit tends to go faster than a conversion. Since the gas line and vent are already there, most swaps mean matching connections, inspecting the old vent and mounting the new heater. Vent sizes can change by brand. We check before ordering.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Lafayette

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Lafayette

    Gas tankless units in Lafayette are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. The catch is gas supply. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or larger. We size it and pressure test it before the unit is lit.
