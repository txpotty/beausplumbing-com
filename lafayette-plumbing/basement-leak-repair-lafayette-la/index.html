    Waterproofing for Older Louisiana Homes in Lafayette, LA

    Many older homes in Lafayette and across Louisiana were not built with modern waterproofing techniques, making them more prone to basement leaks. Homes from the late seventies through the mid nineties may contain polybutylene supply pipe, which is prone to failure. Beau's Plumbing focuses on retrofitting older foundations with current solutions, addressing issues like failing mortar, porous concrete, and inadequate drainage. We preserve the integrity of historic structures while providing modern protection.

    Interior Drainage Systems in Lafayette

    When exterior excavation isn't feasible, interior drainage systems provide an effective solution for managing basement leaks. We install perimeter drains along the basement walls, collecting water before it can surface and directing it to a sump pump for removal. This system is less invasive and can be a cost-effective way to dry out a wet basement.

    Sump Pump Repair and Installation in Lafayette, LA

    Many basements rely on a sump pit to handle storm runoff and groundwater. A high water table means water has nowhere to go after big storms, which can soak yards, crawlspaces, and septic drain fields. Pumps from brands like Zoeller, Liberty and Wayne fail most often from a stuck float or a burned-out motor after sitting idle for months. We repair or replace the pump, check the check valve and route the discharge well away from the foundation. A battery backup is a smart addition because big storms often knock out power.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Lafayette, LA

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes crack and scale with age, and plastic joints can separate if a line was poorly supported. These leaks often show up only when an upstairs fixture runs. We run water through each drain while watching the basement, then verify the crack with a camera. Repairs range from a single section swap to replacing an entire stack with PVC or ABS.

    Basement Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Lafayette, LA

    A simple fitting or supply line repair typically costs $200 to $600. Drain stack repairs run $500 to $2,500 depending on access and pipe material. A new sump pump installed typically costs $600 to $1,800, while interior drain channels and full seepage systems can run several thousand dollars. We explain each option in writing so you can weigh the fix against the risk.
