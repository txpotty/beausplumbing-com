    How We Fix Basement Leaks in Lafayette, LA

    Wall Crack Sealing for Basements in Lafayette

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. We set ports along the crack, seal the face with epoxy paste, and pump in polyurethane or epoxy from the bottom port up until resin shows at the next port. Epoxy bonds the two sides. Polyurethane is better for active leaks because it foams on contact with water.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Lafayette

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. We camera the tile from the sump inlet to see where it is blocked. Jetting clears it. If the old clay tile has collapsed, it gets replaced with new perforated pipe.

    Commercial Basement Waterproofing in Lafayette

    Commercial basements hold inventory, records or mechanical rooms that cannot sit in water. Elevator pits, floor drains and sumps all get checked together because one weak link floods the floor. Duplex pumps help. A high-water alarm warns staff before the floor gets wet.

    Emergency Basement Leak Repair in Lafayette, LA

    A pipe that lets go in a basement can put an inch of water on the floor in under an hour. First, close the main water shutoff.     Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Lafayette

    Seepage at the wall or the slab joint is usually surface water pooling against the foundation. Much of Louisiana sits on soft, expansive clay that swells and shrinks with moisture, which can shift slabs and crack buried pipes. We look at gutters and grading first, since fixing them outside often stops the seepage for far less money. We always try the cheap fixes first.

    Interior Drainage Systems in Lafayette

    Where digging outside is not practical, we handle the water from inside instead. A strip of slab comes out along the walls, perforated pipe goes in on stone, and it all drains to a sump. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Water then runs behind it into the channel.
