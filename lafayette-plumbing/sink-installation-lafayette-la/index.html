    Drop-In Sink Installation in Lafayette, LA

    Drop-in or self-rimming sinks are the most forgiving style and a good choice for laminate counters. We check the cutout, seal the rim with silicone or plumber's putty as the maker specifies, and connect a new pop-up drain. This is often the quickest and most affordable sink installation. It is also a simple way to refresh an older vanity.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Lafayette

    The kitchen sink is a high-traffic area, and a proper installation is key to its longevity and function. We install all types of kitchen sinks, including undermount, drop-in, farmhouse, and bar sinks. Our team delivers correct alignment with countertops, secure mounting, and watertight connections for both the drain and supply lines. We also connect garbage disposals and dishwashers seamlessly during the installation process, adhering to all Louisiana plumbing codes.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Lafayette, LA

    Pedestal sinks are popular for their classic look and space-saving design, but their installation can be challenging due to the exposed plumbing. Our plumbers ensure the drain and supply lines are perfectly aligned within the pedestal column and securely anchored to the wall. This prevents wobbling and ensures all connections hidden and watertight. We handle the delicate nature of ceramic pedestals with care, providing a stable and attractive fixture for your home in Lafayette, LA.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Lafayette, LA

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter, which changes the drain height and faucet requirements. We install the mounting ring, set the bowl level, and use a vessel-specific drain with or without an overflow as the basin calls for. We also confirm your faucet is a tall or wall-mount model that reaches past the rim. The trap arm is adjusted so everything lines up under the counter.

    Commercial Sink Installation in Lafayette, LA

    For businesses in Lafayette, we provide specialized commercial sink installation for kitchens, restrooms, and industrial applications.     Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Lafayette, LA

    Wall-hung sinks need carriers or heavy blocking to meet load requirements. For accessible bathrooms, we set the rim height and knee clearance to ADA guidelines and insulate or shield exposed pipes under the basin. This is common in aging-in-place remodels and small commercial restrooms. We make sure the sink is strong enough to lean on safely.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Lafayette, LA
