    Yard and Area Drain Cleaning in Lafayette

    Yard drains and area inlets collect runoff from lawns, rock landscaping and patios. Over time the grates fill with leaves and the pipes fill with silt and small gravel. We pull the grates, vacuum the inlet box and jet the pipe toward its outlet. This restores the line so it can handle a full storm. We also check that grates are secure and level.

    Hydro Jetting for Storm Drains in Lafayette, LA

    Hydro jetting is a powerful solution for deeply clogged storm drains in Lafayette, LA. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away stubborn obstructions like compacted soil, heavy silt, roots, and grease buildup. It is very effective for thoroughly cleaning the inside of the pipe walls, restoring the drain to its full capacity and preventing a quick return of blockages, a common issue with older drainage systems.

    Channel and Trench Drain Cleaning in Lafayette

    Channel drains across driveways and garage entries catch water before it reaches the house. Their narrow channels fill quickly with sand, leaves and debris. We remove the grates, clean the channel and jet the outlet pipe. Cracked or loose grates can be replaced while we are there. A clean trench drain keeps water out of your garage during heavy rain.

    Root Removal from Storm Drains in Lafayette

    Tree roots are a common cause of severe storm drain blockages in Lafayette, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees.
