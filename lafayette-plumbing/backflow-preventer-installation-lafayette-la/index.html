    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Lafayette, LA

    For high-hazard applications in Lafayette, LA where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is often required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. Beau's Plumbing is experienced with RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is critical for effective operation.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Lafayette, LA

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Lafayette, LA, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.
