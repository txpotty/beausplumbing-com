    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Lafayette, LA: Style by Style

    Workstation Sink Installation in Lafayette, LA

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and most are single deep bowls with an offset drain. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Faucet reach matters too.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Lafayette, LA

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. The disposal gets mounted, the dishwasher drain goes through an air gap or high loop, and the dishwasher gets its own hot line and shutoff. Doing it all at once saves money. We test it all the way you will use it.

    Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in Lafayette, LA

    Three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks each have their own health code rules. Prep sinks usually need an indirect drain to a floor sink with an air gap, while the three-compartment sink often runs through the grease trap. We confirm the rules first. Work is scheduled before or after service so the kitchen does not close.

    Same-Day Sink Replacement in Lafayette, LA

    A sink with a crack through the bowl or a strainer that has rusted out cannot be patched for long. We stock the most common double-bowl stainless sizes, which makes same-day replacement common, and we replace the strainers and trap at the same time. Old stops come out too.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Lafayette

    Drop-in sinks are common in Louisiana homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim carries the weight. After checking the cutout, we lay silicone under the lip and draw the clips down evenly so the whole rim sits flat. Then the drain and faucet get connected and tested.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Lafayette, LA

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. It prevents a crooked reveal. Cast iron versions can top 150 pounds, so we bring two people.

    Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in Lafayette

    Budget stainless sinks ring like a drum when dishes hit them or the disposal runs. Better sinks come with rubber pads and an undercoating, and if yours does not, we can add a coat of spray undercoating before it goes in. It is a small thing. After the sink is in place the underside is hard to get at, which is why we do it while the bowl is still sitting out.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Lafayette

    A double bowl drains both sides through one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. We pick the layout around your disposal and dishwasher, since a disposal on one side changes where the tee can go and how much fall each side gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.
