    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Lafayette, LA

    If the camera shows a sound pipe with a grease or root clog, cleaning is usually all you need. Cracked, offset or collapsed pipe will keep clogging no matter how often it is cleared. Cleaning typically costs a few hundred dollars, while repairs range from a spot fix to trenchless lining or full replacement. We walk you through the footage and costs so you can choose.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Lafayette

    Beau's Plumbing uses powerful hydro-jetting technology to clear even the most stubborn sewer line blockages. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away grease, scale, mineral buildup, and tree roots, restoring your pipes to their original diameter. It's a highly effective, non-invasive way to clean your sewer lines thoroughly and prevent future clogs. Unlike snaking, hydro-jetting cleans the entire pipe wall.

    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Lafayette, LA

    Understanding the difference between a main sewer line clog and a branch line clog is key for proper diagnosis and repair in Lafayette, LA. A main line clog affects all drains in your home, often causing backups in lower-level fixtures. Branch line clogs are usually isolated to a single fixture or area. Our technicians are skilled at pinpointing the exact location of the blockage, whether it is in the main line or a specific branch, for a targeted and efficient repair.
