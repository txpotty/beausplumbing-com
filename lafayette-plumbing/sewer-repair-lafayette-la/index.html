    Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Lafayette, LA

    Pipe Burst and Collapse Repair in Lafayette, LA

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. Every drain backs up. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and we locate the break and its depth before we start digging. The broken length comes out and new pipe goes in. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Lafayette

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. The pipe was leaking first. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they keep growing until the joint splits or the pipe cracks open. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. A liner or new pipe at that joint is what actually stops regrowth.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Lafayette, LA

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, so every crack and leaking joint is covered by one jointless pipe. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. On a four-inch residential line, that rarely matters.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Lafayette, LA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip. Low-lying areas have a high water table and settling ground, which can cause sewer lines to sag, crack, or separate over time. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Lafayette, LA
