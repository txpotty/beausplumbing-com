    What Goes Into a Water Heater Swap in Lafayette, LA

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Lafayette, LA

    Rheem and AO Smith make tanks in nearly every size, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. Both are solid, and the pick usually depends on what fits the space, the warranty length you want and what the supply house has in stock. We lay out the options.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Lafayette

    Louisiana has many older homes with cast iron drains and galvanized supply pipes that corrode faster in warm, wet conditions. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. Two-person crew, always.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Lafayette

    Electric water heaters in Lafayette usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Hot water is back the same day.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Lafayette, LA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Lafayette

    Businesses in Lafayette run their water heaters harder than homes do, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons, laundromats and offices each have a different demand curve, and we size the new unit by peak gallons per hour and the temperature health code requires, which can mean a larger tank, a booster or a rack of tankless units. Downtime costs money.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Lafayette, LA

    A leaking tank can empty itself onto the floor faster than most people expect. If water is coming from the heater, shut off the cold supply valve on top, turn the gas valve to off or flip the breaker, and open a hot tap to relieve pressure, then call and we will try to have a plumber and a new tank at your home that day. Do not wait it out.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Lafayette, LA

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on most tank jobs. Bradford White is trade-only and durable, Rheem offers range and parts, and AO Smith brings plenty of sizes, so the pick comes down to fit, warranty and stock. All three hold up.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Lafayette

When we replace one, we match the fuel, size and venting, check whether the old warranty might still cover the tank, and register the new unit so the warranty is on file from day one. Registration matters.
