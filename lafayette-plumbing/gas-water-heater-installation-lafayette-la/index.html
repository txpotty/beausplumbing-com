    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Lafayette

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon gas water heater typically costs $1,800 to $4,000 installed. Power vent and tankless gas units often cost more due to venting and gas line requirements.

    Installing a gas water heater involves working with natural gas lines, water lines and exhaust venting, which can be dangerous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning or water damage. It is highly recommended to hire a licensed professional for safety and code compliance.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, or visible leaks around the base of the unit. Older units, especially those over 10 to 12 years old, are also candidates for replacement.

    Most standard gas water heater installations take between 3 and 5 hours. More complex installations, such as converting to a tankless unit or significant gas line adjustments, might take a full day. We work efficiently to minimize disruption to your home.

    It's recommended to have your gas water heater professionally serviced annually. Regular maintenance can extend its lifespan, improve efficiency and catch potential issues before they become major problems, supporting safe operation.
