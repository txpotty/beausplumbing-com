    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Lafayette, LA

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. Hardness gets tested on both sides of the unit, and the cheap fixes get tried before we talk replacement. Discontinued valves usually mean replacement.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Lafayette

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. We replace cracked tanks and bad safety floats. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Lafayette

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. Sometimes a carbon filter goes ahead of it.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Lafayette

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. Old clock-timer units come out and metered valves go in. Salt use usually drops. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Lafayette

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico runs them without electricity, using water flow to drive the valve. They cost more.

    Residential Water Softener Replacement in Lafayette

    Scale in the water heater is the expensive part of hard water. A tank water heater on hard water can lose a noticeable share of its efficiency as scale builds on the bottom, and tankless units scale up even faster. A working softener stops that.

    Swapping a salt-based softener in Louisiana homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. We pressurize and check every joint. We set an air gap on the drain line.
