    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Shreveport, LA: Style by Style

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Shreveport, LA

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. We mount the disposal, run the dishwasher drain through an air gap or high loop and install a dedicated hot supply with its own shutoff. Doing it all at once saves money. We test it all the way you will use it.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Shreveport, LA

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Shreveport

    Drop-in sinks are common in Louisiana homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim carries the weight. After checking the cutout, we lay silicone under the lip and draw the clips down evenly so the whole rim sits flat. Next we connect and test the faucet and drain.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Shreveport, LA

    A new sink is the cheapest time to add a disposal. The disposal mounts to the new flange, the trap is set to its outlet height and the dishwasher plug gets knocked out for the hose. You also need power. We check for a switched outlet in the cabinet.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Shreveport

    Both bowls of a double sink share one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. We pick the layout around your disposal and dishwasher, since a disposal on one side changes where the tee can go and how much fall each side gets. Heavy-wall fittings resist leaks.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Shreveport

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and a lower gauge means thicker steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Top-mount and undermount plumb differently. We connect the strainers, faucet and disposal either way.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Shreveport, LA

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, typically for a full day, before loading it with water. Skipping the cure time leads to sagging. Just an eighth of an inch of sag leaves a gap that catches water and food along the edge.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Shreveport, LA

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. We build a support frame inside the cabinet and set the sink level before the counter is installed. A deep bowl means a lower trap. The drain gets planned for that depth from day one.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Kitchen Sink Installation in Shreveport

    Most installs cost $200 to $600 in labor. Where you land depends on the sink type, the condition of the old stops and drain stub, and whether the cabinet needs reinforcing. The sink itself is a separate cost.

    Standard sink bases usually need help. We add a support frame under fireclay and cast iron sinks so the weight rests on the cabinet sides, not the face frame or the countertop.

    Top-mount sinks sit on the counter by their rim. Undermounts hang below the counter from clips and adhesive, which gives a clean edge but needs cure time and a solid stone or quartz top that can hold the anchors. Laminate cannot take an undermount.

    Most jobs take two to four hours. Heavy farmhouse sinks, countertop changes or new supply lines can stretch it to a full day, and undermounts need overnight cure time before the drain sees a full load.

    We see a lot of Kohler, Elkay, Moen, Blanco and Franke. Other brands are fine too, and we follow the maker's install sheet on anything unfamiliar because clip and drain specs differ.

    Every install from Beau's Plumbing comes with a labor warranty. The terms are in writing. If a joint we connected leaks within the warranty period, we come back and fix it, and the sink and faucet makers cover their own products separately.

    Persistent leaks, rust, cracks and deep chips are the signs. Frequent clogs usually point to the drain line rather than the sink, but a strainer that has corroded or separated from the bowl can be the cause too. We check before recommending a new sink.

    Simple drop-in swaps are doable on your own. Undermount, farmhouse and double-bowl installs with disposals are more complex and much more likely to leak.
