    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Services in Shreveport

    The cost of drain cleaning in Shreveport can vary a great deal based on the complexity of the clog and the method required. Simple drain snaking might range from $150 to $350, while hydro jetting or main sewer line clearing could be $400 to $800 or more. We provide a detailed estimate after diagnosing the issue.

    Many homes benefit from main line cleaning every one to two years, especially with mature trees nearby. Restaurants typically need jetting every three to six months.

    Hydro jetting is safe for pipe in sound condition, including PVC and most cast iron. We camera the line first so we do not jet a pipe that is currently cracked or collapsing.

    Most single fixture drain cleanings take under an hour. Main line cleaning and hydro jetting usually take one to three hours.

    For most homes, a professional drain cleaning every one to two years is sufficient for preventative maintenance. Businesses, especially restaurants, often require more regular service.

    While readily available, we usually advise against using chemical drain cleaners.
