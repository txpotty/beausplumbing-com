    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Shreveport

    The cost of well pump replacement in Shreveport, LA can vary widely, typically ranging from $1,500 to $5,000 or more. This price depends on factors like the type of pump, submersible or jet, well depth, horsepower and any additional components needed like pressure tanks or control boxes. We provide a detailed, upfront estimate after diagnosing your situation.

    Shallow well pumps, or jet pumps, are designed for wells where the water level is typically 25 feet or less from the pump, using suction to draw water. Deep well pumps, or submersible pumps, are placed directly in the water near the bottom of the well and push water up, suited for depths over 25 feet. Choosing the correct type is critical for efficiency and performance.

    Regular maintenance, such as checking your well's water level, keeping the pressure tank air charge correct and fixing any leaks promptly, can extend your pump's life. Avoiding heavy water use and installing a good quality sediment filter can also reduce wear and tear on the pump.

    Well pump replacement is a complex job that involves high voltage electricity, heavy equipment and possibly deep well access. It requires specialized tools and experience for proper installation, electrical safety and compliance with local plumbing codes. It is highly recommended to hire a licensed professional to avoid injury, property damage or costly mistakes.

    The lifespan of a well pump varies greatly depending on usage, water quality and pump type, but most last between 10 and 20 years. Regular maintenance and proper sizing can extend its life, but eventually wear and tear will call for replacement.

    New well pumps typically come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. Beau's Plumbing also stands behind our installation work with our own labor warranty. We will discuss warranty details for your chosen pump during the quoting process.

    Most standard well pump replacements can be completed within a single day, typically 4 to 8 hours. However, complex installations involving very deep wells, difficult access or additional system repairs may take longer. Our team works quickly to restore your water as soon as possible.

    If your pressure tank is old or waterlogged, replacing it at the same time can protect the new pump. A failed tank causes rapid short cycling, which is one of the most frequent reasons submersible motors burn out early.
