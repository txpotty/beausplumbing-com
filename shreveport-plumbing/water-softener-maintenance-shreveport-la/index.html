    Control Valve and Injector Service in Shreveport, LA

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.

    Hardness Test and Water Quality Check in Shreveport

    As part of our maintenance, we run a water hardness test to verify your water softener is effectively reducing mineral content to proper levels. This ensures you are receiving the soft water benefits you expect and allows us to fine-tune your system if necessary. We aim for consistent water quality in every Shreveport home we serve.

    Routine Water Softener Inspections in Shreveport

    Regular inspections are the foundation of effective water softener maintenance. Our technicians will examine your entire system, including the brine tank, resin tank, control valve and all connecting pipes. We check for leaks, corrosion, proper salt levels and any signs of wear that could lead to future problems. This preventive approach helps extend the life of your unit and keeps performance consistent, especially since water hardness varies from place to place.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Shreveport

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs much less than a new system.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Inspection in Shreveport, LA

    Over time, sludge and insoluble materials can accumulate in your brine tank, hindering the softening process. Our maintenance includes thoroughly cleaning the brine tank, inspecting for any cracks or leaks and confirming the float valve operates correctly. This step is crucial for the efficient regeneration of your water softener in Shreveport, LA.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Maintenance in Shreveport

    Template assisted crystallization and other salt-free conditioners do not remove hardness but change how it behaves. They still need media replacement every few years and sediment prefilter changes much more often. We inspect the media tank, replace prefilters and check bypass valves. We also explain plainly how these systems differ from a true softener.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Shreveport, LA

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their effectiveness. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for full softening capacity. We make sure your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Shreveport, LA

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes prevent leaks and backflow problems.
