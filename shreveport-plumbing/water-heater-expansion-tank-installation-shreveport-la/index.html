    Expansion Tank Replacement in Shreveport, LA

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a sure sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installation.

    Expansion Tank Sizing for Large Water Heaters in Shreveport

    A standard 2 gallon tank works for many 40 and 50 gallon heaters at moderate pressure. Larger heaters, higher incoming pressure or high-capacity systems may need a 4.5 gallon tank or larger. We use manufacturer sizing charts that factor in heater volume, supply pressure and temperature setting. Correct sizing keeps the tank from over-flexing and wearing out early.

    New Expansion Tank Installation in Shreveport

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is likely seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle. We install a new tank on the cold water supply near the heater using a tee and short nipple. The air side is precharged to match your static pressure, usually between 50 and 70 psi. We then run the heater and watch the gauge to confirm pressure stays stable.

    Preventing Water Heater Damage in Shreveport

    Thermal expansion is a common cause of premature water heater failure in Shreveport homes. Without an expansion tank, constant pressure swings stress the water heater's tank, connections and internal components, leading to leaks, reduced efficiency and a shorter lifespan. Our installation service directly addresses this issue, providing a buffer that protects your investment. This small addition can save you thousands in future water heater repair or replacement.
