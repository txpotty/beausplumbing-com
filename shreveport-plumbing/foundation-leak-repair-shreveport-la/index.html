    Foundation Leak Detection in Shreveport

    Accurate detection is the most important step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to verify a leak exists.     When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most affordable choice.
