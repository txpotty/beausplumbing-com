    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Shreveport

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Rusty water is a sign.     PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Shreveport, LA

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Each fails its own way. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, and we use parts and methods made for each pipe type. Brittle CPVC needs a gentle hand.

    After-Hours Pipe Repair in Shreveport

    Pipes like to burst at night and on holidays. Someone picks up. An after-hours plumber in Shreveport can stop the water, find the failed section and fix it the same night using parts already on the truck. Larger repairs get scheduled for morning.

    PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Shreveport

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, and we cut out the bad fitting and replace it with a crimp, clamp or expansion connection rated for the pipe. Most take under an hour.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Shreveport

    Threaded steel pipe rusts from the inside until flow drops and threaded joints start to leak. Repairs are possible. The trouble is that every fitting you disturb on old galvanized can start a new leak, so we replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric fittings and talk through a full replacement plan when the rest is close behind. You decide the timing.
