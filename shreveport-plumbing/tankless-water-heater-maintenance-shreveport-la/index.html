    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Shreveport, LA

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Shreveport

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. On a condensing unit the PVC or polypropylene vent should pitch back toward the heater. Every joint gets checked, and we test for carbon monoxide by the heater. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Emergency Tankless Repair in Shreveport, LA

    No hot water before work is a rough start. Nine times out of ten, a no-heat call is a dead igniter or a scaled exchanger tripping the high-limit. The truck carries igniters and descaling gear, so we can usually fix it that day.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Shreveport

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. The trap gets cleaned and the media checked. Spent media gets replaced. Where the line runs outside, we check that it is supported and protected.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Shreveport

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate. The condensate trap gets cleared and the neutralizer media checked. On A-series models with the built-in recirculation pump, we test the pump and the buffer tank too.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Shreveport, LA

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. We start a Noritz tune-up at the exhaust, checking for soot and any blocked termination. Next comes the combustion air intake and the fan. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Shreveport, LA

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. On a Rinnai an LC code means scale, on a Navien E003 means no ignition, and on a Noritz a 90 usually means an air or exhaust problem. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Same-Day Tankless Help in Shreveport, LA

    A blinking error code and a cold shower usually mean the heater protected itself from something. Heavy scale or a blocked exhaust is the usual cause. We bring common parts and a descaling kit on emergency calls, so most heaters are firing again before we pack up the truck.
