    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Shreveport, LA

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original work, thermal expansion or vibration. Look for white or green crust. A leaking joint usually weeps for a while before it drips, and we clean, reflux and resolder the joint or replace the fitting with new pipe on each side when the old copper is too thin. The joint gets tested.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Shreveport, LA

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Raised homes with pipes under the floor are the most vulnerable during a cold snap, since cold air moves freely underneath. Shut the main first. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many bursts are found hours after the cold. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Then we add insulation.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Shreveport

    Pinholes start inside the pipe where the copper wall pits and thins. The first sign is often a spot. They are small and easy to miss behind drywall, which is why we use thermal imaging to find them before we open anything. We replace the pitted length with Type L. We look at the copper around it.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Shreveport

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot lines get it worst. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, which we fix by replacing that length with heavier pipe. One usually means more are coming.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Shreveport, LA

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Heat speeds it up. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, which is why we often add a timer to the pump after fixing the leak. It slows the next pinhole.
