    A standard sump pump inspection and cleaning typically runs $125 to $250. Replacing a float switch or check valve usually adds $75 to $250 depending on parts.

    Some Louisiana groundwater carries iron and sulfur, which can cause sulfur smells, stains, and buildup in water heaters. Regular maintenance helps limit these effects.

    The best time is a few weeks before your wettest season begins. Homes that see snow may also want a fall check before winter storms and snowmelt.

    The check valve is essential because it prevents water that has been pumped out from flowing back into the sump pit after the pump shuts off.
