    Sump Pump Replacement in Shreveport, LA

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors. We replace old pumps with new models and inspect the pit, check valve and discharge line. Upgrading to a higher capacity pump may help if your old one could not keep up. We test the new pump by filling the pit with water. Replacement before the rainy season is a smart move.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Shreveport, LA

    Power outages during heavy storms are a serious concern, which is why a battery backup sump pump is a smart addition. This system automatically takes over if your primary pump loses power, providing continuous flood protection. We install dependable backup units that can operate for hours, giving you peace of mind even when the electricity goes out in Shreveport.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Shreveport, LA

    For complete peace of mind, consider a combination sump pump system. This setup includes a primary electric sump pump and an integrated battery backup pump in a single unit, providing full protection against both power failures and primary pump malfunctions. Beau's Plumbing can install these dual-pump systems in your Shreveport, LA home, offering stronger flood prevention. This solution is ideal for homes in flood-prone areas or those with expensive basement finishes.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Shreveport, LA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to reach for maintenance and repair. They are often a good choice for shallow sump pits or areas where space is tight. Our team is experienced in installing these pumps, ensuring proper pit depth and discharge line setup for reliable performance in Shreveport, LA.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Shreveport

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a reliable place to collect and remove water.
