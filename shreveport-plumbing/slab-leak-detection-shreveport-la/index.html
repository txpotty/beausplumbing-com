    Slab Leak Reroutes in Shreveport

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is popular when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Leak Detection for Older Louisiana Homes in Shreveport, LA

    Many older homes in Shreveport, LA have copper or galvanized steel pipes that are more susceptible to corrosion and leaks over time. Our technicians are highly experienced in detecting leaks in these older systems, understanding their common failure points. We provide expert analysis for the challenges presented by vintage plumbing under concrete slabs.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Shreveport

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs constantly and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before flagging the spot.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Shreveport

    A slab leak that is flooding floors or causing water to bubble up through tile needs fast attention. We respond quickly, help you shut off the water and isolate the leaking side if possible. That often lets you keep some water service until the repair. Then we locate and fix the leak.

    Non-Invasive Leak Detection in Shreveport, LA

    Our primary goal is to locate your slab leak with the least amount of disruption possible. We favor non-invasive techniques like acoustic and thermal imaging before considering any intrusive methods. This approach saves your home in Shreveport, LA from unnecessary damage, preserves your landscaping and lowers the overall cost and time of the repair process.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Shreveport, LA

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.
