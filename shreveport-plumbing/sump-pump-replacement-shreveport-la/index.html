    Backup Pump Options in Shreveport, LA

    Hurricanes and tropical storms are a major concern in Louisiana, bringing heavy rain, flooding, and power outages that affect plumbing.     Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Shreveport

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. That is not a sump pump job.     Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Shreveport

    Horsepower matters less than people think. A third-horse pump moves plenty of water for most homes, as long as the vertical lift to the discharge is under about 15 feet. We check the flow chart against your actual lift before we pick a size. Oversize it and it short cycles.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Shreveport, LA

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We swap the pump, replace the float rod and stops, and run new 1.5-inch PVC to the check valve. Cheaper than a submersible, too.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Shreveport

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. A sealed AGM or deep-cycle marine battery in the 75 to 100 amp-hour range will typically handle several hours of intermittent pumping during an outage. We ask how often your pump runs in a storm and size the battery to match. Plan on a new battery every three to five years.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Shreveport

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test. Some calls only need a new switch, while others need the whole pump. We tell you which before we sell you anything.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Shreveport, LA
