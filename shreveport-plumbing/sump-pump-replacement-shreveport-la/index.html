    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Shreveport

    The cost of sump pump replacement in Shreveport, LA usually ranges from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, submersible or pedestal, its horsepower and any needed pit changes. Battery backup systems will add to this cost, usually between $300 and $800. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your needs.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that activates if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many Shreveport homes benefit from a backup system due to storm risks.

    Most sump pump replacements take one to two hours. Battery backups or new discharge lines take longer.

    While it may seem like a simple task, sump pump replacement involves electrical connections, proper plumbing and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding. DIY replacement can lead to improper operation, electrical hazards or system failure. It is best to hire a professional plumber for a safe and effective installation.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 3 to 5 years. We also offer a labor warranty.

    Constant running, loud grinding, frequent cycling or failure to start are typical signs. Rust, age and a burning smell also point to trouble.

    It is recommended to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit. The pump should activate, drain the water and then shut off automatically. If it does not, it is time for a professional inspection.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods can fail sooner due to stuck switches.
