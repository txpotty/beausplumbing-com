    Drain and Sewer Jetting Services in Baton Rouge, LA

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Baton Rouge

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. We jet the full main to the street tap. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Sewer Lateral Jetting in Baton Rouge, LA

    The lateral from your house to the street handles every drain in the house, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. We jet it from the cleanout to the tap. Low spots where the pipe has settled hold water and collect sludge, so we spend extra passes there and mark the location on the camera footage in case a repair makes sense later.

    Commercial Hydrojet Plumbing in Baton Rouge, LA

    Businesses put more through their drains than any house. Multifamily and office buildings carry heavy daily loads. We jet stacks, laterals and mains for commercial buildings, often before opening hours, and we leave a written report with camera notes for the property manager's records.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Baton Rouge, LA

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Because jetting removes the buildup that causes repeat clogs, many homeowners spend less over a few years than they would on repeated snaking visits for the same line.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Baton Rouge

    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Baton Rouge

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain is a main line blockage. Stop using water first. Our technician opens the line, then jets it to remove the cause, and a camera pass after jetting shows whether the backup came from buildup alone or from a broken or root-filled section that needs repair.

    After-Hours Hydrojet Service in Baton Rouge

    Backups at night or on weekends are handled with the same gear as any weekday call. Our on-call trucks carry jetters. That means we can clear and jet a grease-packed restaurant line or a root-filled home sewer in one visit, without waiting until Monday to bring the jetter back for a second trip.

    Root Removal Jetting in Baton Rouge

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. A root nozzle cuts them out. Jetting does not stop roots from returning, because the crack is still there, but it clears the pipe completely and lets us see on camera whether a spot repair or a liner would stop the regrowth.
