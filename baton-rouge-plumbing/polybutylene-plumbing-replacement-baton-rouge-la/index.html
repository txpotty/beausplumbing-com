    Why Polybutylene Fails in Baton Rouge

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Some Louisiana groundwater carries iron and sulfur, which can cause rotten egg odors, stains, and buildup in water heaters. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Yard Service Line Replacement in Baton Rouge, LA

    Many poly-era homes also have a blue or black polybutylene service line running from the water meter to the house. Buried poly service lines split, run up bills and saturate the yard.
