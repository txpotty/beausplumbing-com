    Leak Detection and Assessment in Baton Rouge, LA

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. With all fixtures off, we watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera to trace the leak behind walls, under floors or in the slab. Catching it early keeps it small. Homes are commonly built on slabs or raised pier foundations, and raised homes keep much of the plumbing easier to reach.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Baton Rouge

    Private wells serve many rural homes, and sediment, iron, and bacterial contamination make periodic well testing a good habit. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. Then we explain your options. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.
