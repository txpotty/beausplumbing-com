    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Baton Rouge

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Baton Rouge, LA

    For badly damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time. This provides a completely new, durable pipe, often HDPE (high-density polyethylene), which resists corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for widespread issues in Baton Rouge, LA.
