    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Baton Rouge

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. We still answer.     Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Baton Rouge, LA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Homes are commonly built on slabs or raised pier foundations, and raised homes leave much of the plumbing easier to reach. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Baton Rouge

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and we always jet a line before lining it. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Baton Rouge

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. Crushed pipe cannot be lined. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. Older homes often need this on at least one section. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.

    Emergency Sewer Repair in Baton Rouge

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. We answer at night and on weekends.
