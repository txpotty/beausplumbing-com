    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Baton Rouge

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Cabling just opens a hole. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Baton Rouge

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Both have weak spots. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.
