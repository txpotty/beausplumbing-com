    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Baton Rouge

    Most well pressure tank replacements cost $900 to $2,200 installed, depending on tank size and whether the switch and tank tee are replaced. Large capacity or multi-tank setups can run $2,500 or more. We give a firm price before starting.

    Rapid pump cycling, pressure that swings up and down, and water coming out of the air valve are typical signs. A tank that sounds solid and full when tapped near the top is often waterlogged.

    We install Amtrol Well-X-Trol, Flexcon and Water Worker tanks. All three are widely available and backed by solid manufacturer warranties.

    Common signs include your well pump cycling on and off very often (short-cycling), fluctuating water pressure at your faucets, no water coming from the tap, or a waterlogged tank when you tap on it. Visible rust or leaks around the tank are also obvious indicators.

    Both bladder and diaphragm tanks use an air cushion to pressurize water, but they differ in how the water and air are separated. Bladder tanks have a replaceable rubber bladder that holds the water, fully isolating it from the air. Diaphragm tanks use a fixed rubber diaphragm. Bladder tanks are often preferred for their longevity and ease of maintenance.

    Regular maintenance for a well pressure tank involves checking the air pressure in the tank at least once a year, ensuring it's properly pre-charged. You should also listen for excessive pump cycling and visually inspect the tank for any signs of corrosion or leaks. We can include this check during regular well system servicing.
