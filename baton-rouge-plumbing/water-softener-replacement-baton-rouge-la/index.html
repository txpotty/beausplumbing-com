    Softener Loop Installation in Baton Rouge, LA

    Many homes have a softener loop near the main water line that allows easy installation. When a home lacks one, we can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. We also route a drain line to a laundry box, floor drain or approved location. A good loop keeps outdoor hose bibs on hard water to save salt. This makes future service simpler.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Baton Rouge

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water around the clock. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but prevent hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Baton Rouge

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the best salt type for your system.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Baton Rouge, LA

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more economical than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Baton Rouge

    Clack valves are a popular choice for their reliability and adjustable settings. If you have an older Clack system failing, Beau's Plumbing offers professional Clack water softener replacement in Baton Rouge. We can replace just the control valve or the entire system, ensuring compatibility with your existing setup and configuring the new unit for peak performance.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Baton Rouge

    A standard single tank softener replacement usually costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can add to the cost.

    We test your hardness in grains per gallon and estimate daily water usage. That tells us the resin capacity needed.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is not typically required for a like-for-like water softener replacement, especially if there are no significant changes to the existing plumbing lines. However, if new lines are run or the system type changes substantially, a permit might be necessary. We handle all needed checks and make sure the job complies with local codes.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had heavy mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can investigate other causes.

    Common signs include a return of hard water symptoms like scale buildup on fixtures, dry skin and hair, dingy laundry or heavy soap use. Other signs are a noticeable drop in water pressure, leaks from the unit, or a softener over 10 to 15 years old that needs frequent repairs.

    The right size depends on your household's daily water usage and the hardness of your water in grains per gallon. Our technicians will perform a water test and calculate the correct softening capacity in grains per day for your Baton Rouge home, so the system performs well.
