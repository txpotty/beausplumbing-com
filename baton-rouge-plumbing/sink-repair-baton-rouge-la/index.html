    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Baton Rouge, LA

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Baton Rouge, LA

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A burst supply line can dump gallons a minute, and we answer at night and on weekends with a stocked truck. While you wait, shut off the valves under the sink. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Baton Rouge

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount sinks hang on clips glued under the counter, and pedestal sinks hang on a bracket that can pull loose from drywall. We reset the clips and reseal the rim. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Baton Rouge

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. That points to worn O-rings on the spout or a bad base gasket, and sometimes to a cracked faucet body that cannot be rebuilt. Each faucet style rebuilds its own way. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Baton Rouge

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Next, put towels on the floor. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in Baton Rouge
