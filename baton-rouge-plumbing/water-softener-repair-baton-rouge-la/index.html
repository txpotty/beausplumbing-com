    Resin Bed Replacement & Regeneration Issues in Baton Rouge

    Over time, the resin beads inside your water softener can become fouled, damaged, or depleted, reducing their ability to remove hardness minerals. If your system is regenerating but still delivering hard water, the resin bed may be the culprit. We can test the resin, flush the system to remove iron or sediment buildup, or perform a complete resin replacement. This process revitalizes your softener's core function, ensuring effective hard water treatment for years to come.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Baton Rouge

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a frequent issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine. Beau's Plumbing provides professional resin bed replacement services, carefully removing old resin and installing new, high-quality media to restore your softener's efficiency and capacity in Baton Rouge.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Baton Rouge, LA

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank and keeps salt from dissolving. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. We also suggest salt types that bridge less in warm or humid spaces.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Baton Rouge, LA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed usually costs $1,500 to $3,500. If the tank and valve are sound, repair usually wins. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense.
