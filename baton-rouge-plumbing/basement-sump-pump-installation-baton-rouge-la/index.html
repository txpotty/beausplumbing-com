    Residential Basement Sump Pump Installation in Baton Rouge

    A typical house pit holds an 18 by 22 inch basin with a 1/3 horsepower pump, and that covers most homes. We size the pump to your inflow and lift, not to the sale sticker.     Sump Pump Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Baton Rouge, LA

    High water alarms cost little and buy you time. The basic version sounds off when water climbs above the float, while a Wi-Fi monitor sends a text if the level rises or the power drops, which homeowners who travel tend to like. You hear it go off before we leave.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Baton Rouge

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. That keeps it dry and easy to reach, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Baton Rouge homes. They are louder, though.

    Sump Pit Installation in Baton Rouge, LA

    Pit size controls how often the pump starts. A small 15 inch pit makes the pump cycle every minute in heavy flow, so we usually install an 18 by 22 inch or 18 by 30 inch basin with a solid bottom and perforated sides wrapped in filter fabric. Fewer starts mean a longer life.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Baton Rouge, LA

    Submersible units sit down in the pit, sealed, with the water around them cooling the motor. That makes them quieter than a pedestal, and in Baton Rouge, LA we set them on a couple of bricks so the intake stays above the silt that settles on the bottom of every liner. The cord seal matters.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Baton Rouge

    Older homes often have narrow pits that will not fit a submersible. That is where a pedestal pump earns its place, and we install it with rigid discharge, a quality check valve, and a float rod set to your pit depth so it starts before water reaches the top of the liner. Noisy, but they last.
