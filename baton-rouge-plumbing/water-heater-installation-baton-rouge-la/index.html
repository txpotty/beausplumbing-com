    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Baton Rouge

    The cost of water heater installation in Baton Rouge, LA usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your specific needs.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, water heater installation involves working with gas lines, high voltage electricity and water connections, all of which carry significant risks if not handled correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, gas explosions, electrical fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It can also void your warranty, and local codes often require a licensed plumber for this work.

    Beau's Plumbing installs all major reputable brands of water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz. We work with both gas and electric models, as well as many sizes and efficiencies. Our team can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your hot water demands and budget.

    Most households of two to four people do well with a 40 or 50 gallon tank. Larger families may need a 75 gallon tank or a tankless unit sized by flow rate.

    We install Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, Navien, Rinnai and other trusted brands. We recommend models based on reliability, warranty and budget.

    Most standard water heater installations, particularly for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring significant plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    An expansion tank absorbs excess water pressure created as water heats up inside the tank. Many homes in Baton Rouge, LA, particularly those with closed-loop plumbing systems, are required by code to have an expansion tank to protect the water heater and plumbing system from damage.

    A traditional tank water heater usually lasts between 8 and 12 years with proper maintenance. Tankless water heaters often have a longer lifespan, ranging from 15 to 20 years, thanks to their durable components and on-demand operation.
