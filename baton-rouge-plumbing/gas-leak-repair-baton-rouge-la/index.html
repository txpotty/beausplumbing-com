    Propane Gas Leak Repair in Baton Rouge, LA

    Propane behaves differently from natural gas because it is heavier than air, so a leak settles into basements and crawlspaces instead of rising.     Underground Gas Line Repair in Baton Rouge

    Yard lines run from the meter to the house, and often on to a detached garage, a pool heater, a grill or an outdoor kitchen. Steel pipe in the ground corrodes wherever the wrap was nicked during the original install. Roots and settling do damage too. We call 811 for locates, dig at the suspected spot and replace the bad run with yellow polyethylene pipe and tracer wire. Sand goes around the new pipe before backfill, per Baton Rouge trench rules.

    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Baton Rouge, LA

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. Bonding means a 6 AWG copper wire from the gas piping to the electrical grounding system. Plenty of older installs lack it. We repair CSST only with the matching brand's fittings, because TracPipe and Gastite parts do not interchange. A splice that skips that rule fails inspection.

    Gas Leak Detection in Baton Rouge

    Some leaks announce themselves with a hiss you can hear across the room, and others lose a few cubic feet a day and only show up on the bill. Slow leaks are what the test gauge is for.     Interior Gas Pipe Repair in Baton Rouge, LA

    Inside the house, gas leaks hide at unions in the basement and at the threaded nipple behind the furnace, places nobody looks. Testing runs from the meter inward, one section at a time.
