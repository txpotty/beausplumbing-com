    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Baton Rouge, LA

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They are heavier and pricier than two-piece models. A one-piece toilet usually takes two people to carry upstairs, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Baton Rouge, LA

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are noticeably louder than gravity toilets. When we replace one, we pick a vessel brand with parts that are easy to get later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Baton Rouge

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. Battery and hardwired models are both available. We do the work after hours if needed, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.

    Rental and Multi-Unit Toilet Replacement in Baton Rouge, LA

    Landlords with several units do better standardizing on one toilet model with common parts, because the next flapper or fill valve is then the same part in every unit. Multiple toilets can be swapped in a single trip. Tenants keep a working bathroom. Entry gets coordinated with each tenant directly, so you do not have to be there.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Baton Rouge

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. The carrier itself sometimes needs new bolts or a coupling. The new bowl gets matched to the carrier already in the wall whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Baton Rouge, LA

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank. Work gets scheduled around your business hours. Bowls are matched to the flush valve's gallons per flush, since a mismatch leaves waste behind or wastes water.

    What a Standard Swap Includes in Baton Rouge

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected. Then we level it. The base gets caulked, with a small gap left at the back so any future leak shows up instead of hiding under the floor.

    Choosing a Toilet Brand in Baton Rouge, LA

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield all make solid toilets, and each line has a few models we steer people away from because of weak flushes or hard-to-find parts. We will tell you which ones. What fits your bathroom comes down to rough-in and bowl shape, and we check both before you buy.
