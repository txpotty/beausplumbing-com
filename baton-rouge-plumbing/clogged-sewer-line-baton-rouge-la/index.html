    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Baton Rouge, LA

    Understanding the difference between a main sewer line clog and a branch line clog is key for proper diagnosis and repair in Baton Rouge, LA. A main line clog affects all drains in your home, often causing backups in lower-level fixtures. Branch line clogs are usually isolated to a single fixture or area. Our technicians are skilled at pinpointing the exact location of the blockage, whether it is in the main line or a specific branch, for a targeted and efficient repair.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Baton Rouge

    Hydro jetting uses water at thousands of psi through a specialized nozzle to cut roots, grease and scale. It cleans the full diameter of the pipe rather than just boring a hole through the clog. We check the pipe condition with a camera first so jetting is only used on lines that can handle it. Many homeowners find that jetting buys them years before another clog.

    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Baton Rouge

    Roots from thirsty trees and shrubs seek out moisture and push into sewer pipe joints. Clay and older cast iron pipe are especially prone to root intrusion because of their many joints. We cut roots with a sewer machine fitted with root saws and then camera the line to see how much damage remains. For ongoing root problems, we can recommend a root treatment schedule or lining.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Baton Rouge, LA

    If the camera shows a sound pipe with a grease or root clog, cleaning is usually all you need. Cracked, offset or collapsed pipe will keep clogging no matter how often it is cleared. Cleaning typically costs a few hundred dollars, while repairs range from a spot fix to trenchless lining or full replacement. We walk you through the footage and costs so you can decide.

    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Baton Rouge, LA

    Low-lying areas have a high water table and sinking ground, which can cause sewer lines to sag, crack, or separate over time. Soil that swells, shrinks or settles can cause sections of sewer pipe to sink and form a belly. Waste and water collect in the low spot and lead to repeat clogs. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. We can then quote a spot repair to restore correct slope.
