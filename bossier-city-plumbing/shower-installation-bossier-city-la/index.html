    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Bossier City, LA

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Both prevent scalding. We help you choose based on how many outlets you plan.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Bossier City

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Bossier City

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. A linear drain often works best. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.
