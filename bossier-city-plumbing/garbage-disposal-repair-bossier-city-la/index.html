    Leak Detection and Repair in Bossier City, LA

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.

    Leaking Disposal Fix in Bossier City, LA

    When the drip starts at the top, the putty under the sink flange is usually dried out. We drop the unit, pull the flange, clean off the old putty and reset it with fresh putty and new gaskets, then hang the disposal back up. Most top leaks stop there. A leak on the side is usually the discharge gasket or a loose hose clamp.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Bossier City, LA

    Fine grinding with slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We pull the trap, clear the tube and the arm with the right size cable, and check that the arm has at least a quarter inch of fall per foot toward the wall.

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in Bossier City

    When a disposal fails, water can back into the sink and the dishwasher. If water is rising or reaching the floor, shut off the disposal and the dishwasher and call us, since running either one just adds more water to the cabinet. We move quickly on these.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Bossier City

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Bad bearings mean replacement.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Bossier City

    Humming with no spin almost always means a jam. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Bossier City

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. Corroded wire nuts and loose cord connections are common in that damp space, and we replace them rather than just tightening them back down.

Celery, artichoke leaves and corn husks belong in the trash.
