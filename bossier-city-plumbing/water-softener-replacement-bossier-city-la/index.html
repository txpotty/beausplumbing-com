    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Bossier City

    A standard single tank softener replacement usually costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can add to the cost.

    While it may seem like a straightforward task, water softener replacement involves working with water lines, drain connections and sometimes electrical components. Improper installation can lead to leaks, inefficient operation or even water damage. Hiring a professional means the job is done correctly, safely and to code.

    Most standard residential water softener replacements can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. This includes disconnecting the old unit, installing the new one, making plumbing connections and testing the system. More complex installations might take a bit longer.

    New water softeners usually come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years on different components, such as the control valve and resin tank. Beau's Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work for your peace of mind. Exact warranty details will be provided with your quote.

    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, effectively softening the water. Salt-free conditioners do not remove minerals but change their crystal structure so they do not stick to surfaces. That prevents scale buildup without actually softening the water.

    The right size depends on your household's daily water usage and the hardness of your water in grains per gallon. Our technicians will perform a water test and calculate the appropriate softening capacity in grains per day for your Bossier City home, so the system keeps up.

    Requirements vary by city. Simple replacements may not require one, but new plumbing connections may. We check local rules on your behalf.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear faster with very hard or chlorinated water.
