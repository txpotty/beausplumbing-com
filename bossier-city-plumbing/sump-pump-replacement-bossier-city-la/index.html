    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Bossier City

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized.     Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Bossier City

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    We install Zoeller, Liberty and Wayne pumps. Cast iron models are preferred for durability.

    Sump pumps usually have a lifespan of 7 to 10 years. Even if they appear to be working, their efficiency can decrease over time. Regular inspection and testing are important, and replacement is advisable as they approach the end of their expected service life.
