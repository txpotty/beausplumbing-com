    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Bossier City

    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can keep up with more showers back to back, and in some homes a 50-gallon high-recovery gas unit does the job of a 75-gallon standard tank without taking up any more floor space. Bigger is not always needed.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Bossier City

    Businesses in Bossier City put far more hours on a heater than a house, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons, laundromats and offices each have a different demand curve, and we size the new unit by peak gallons per hour and the temperature health code requires, which can mean a larger tank, a booster or a rack of tankless units. We schedule around your hours.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Bossier City, LA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Bossier City, LA

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Bossier City, LA homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Every joint gets checked.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Bossier City

    Electric water heaters in Bossier City usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Bradford White Water Heater Replacement in Bossier City

    Bradford White sells through professional plumbers only, so you will not find one at a big-box store. Their tanks have a good reputation for durability, and parts and warranty support run through the plumber who installed it, which makes it simple for us to handle a warranty claim for a homeowner down the road. Parts are easy to get.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Bossier City, LA

    Ruud and Rheem come out of the same company and share many parts. We install both, and because parts overlap, a thermostat or element is easy to find when repairing a newer tank beats replacing it. We will say when repair works.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Bossier City

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. You see both numbers.
