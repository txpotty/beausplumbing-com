    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Bossier City

    Sediment buildup is a frequent issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.     Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Bossier City, LA

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing hard. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can destroy a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Bossier City

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is common in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Bossier City

    A standard flush and inspection typically runs $125 to $250 depending on tank size and access. Anode replacement typically adds $75 to $200 in parts and labor, and powered anodes cost more.

    While some basic steps like draining a small amount of water can be done by a homeowner, professional hot water tank maintenance involves specialized tools and experience to properly inspect electrical or gas components, anode rods and pressure relief valves. It is best left to a professional to protect safety and code compliance.

    That smell usually comes from bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. We can switch to an aluminum-zinc or powered anode and sanitize the tank to get rid of it.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which counts most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it extends the lifespan of your unit.

    Watch for slower recovery, rusty or cloudy hot water, noises, moisture around the base or a relief valve that drips. Any of these means it is time for an inspection visit.

    Neglecting maintenance can lead to inefficient operation, higher energy bills, premature tank failure from corrosion or sediment buildup, and potential safety hazards from a malfunctioning pressure relief valve. Regular service prevents these costly and dangerous issues.

    Most flushes and inspections take 45 minutes to 90 minutes. Heavily scaled tanks or anode replacements in tight closets can take a bit longer.

    Once a year is the minimum for most homes, and more often where water is hard. Homes without a softener or with heavy use may benefit from a flush every six months.
