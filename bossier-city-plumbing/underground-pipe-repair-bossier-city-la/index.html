    Pipe Bursting Services in Bossier City, LA

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless technique where a new pipe is pulled through the path of the old, broken pipe, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is very effective for complete pipe replacement, creating a new, durable pipe of the same or larger diameter.     Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Bossier City

    The cost of underground pipe repair in Bossier City, LA can vary a lot, typically ranging from $500 for minor spot repairs to $3,000 to $10,000 or more for trenchless full line replacements, or even more for extensive excavation and re-piping. Factors include the pipe material, depth, length of the damaged section, the repair method chosen and the extent of property restoration needed. We provide honest, upfront pricing after a thorough diagnosis.

    For water lines we usually use PEX or Type K copper, both approved for direct burial. For sewer lines we use Schedule 40 PVC. The right choice depends on the existing pipe and local code.

    The time of an underground pipe repair depends on the complexity. A minor leak repair might take a few hours, while a full trenchless sewer line replacement could take one to three days. We always aim for efficient service with minimal disruption.

    We use acoustic listening devices that pick up the sound of escaping water, tracer gas that rises through soil at the leak point and electronic locators that trace the pipe path. Together these narrow the leak to a small area.

    The duration of an underground pipe repair depends heavily on the severity and location of the damage, as well as the repair method chosen. Minor repairs using trenchless techniques might be completed in a single day. More extensive issues, such as full sewer line replacements requiring excavation, could take two to five days, including restoration work.

    Look for unusually wet spots in your yard, unexplained increases in your water bill, foul odors, decreased water pressure or gurgling sounds from drains. These can all point to a hidden leak or blockage in your underground plumbing system.

    Common signs include a water bill that jumps without explanation, a meter that moves with all water off, low pressure and wet or sinking spots in the yard. A hissing sound near the meter box can also point to a leak.

    Common signs of a broken underground pipe include unexplained wet spots or lush patches in your yard, a sudden drop in water pressure, unusual odors such as sulfur or sewage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets or an unexpected increase in your water bill. If you notice any of these, it's smart to call for a professional inspection promptly.
