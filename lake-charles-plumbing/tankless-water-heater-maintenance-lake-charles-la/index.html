    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Lake Charles

    Most tankless descaling visits cost $175 to $350, depending on brand and access. Adding an isolation valve kit typically runs $150 to $300 more.

    Rinnai shows LC codes, Navien may show E003 or E030 and Noritz often shows 90 or 99 when scale or airflow is a problem. These codes typically clear after proper cleaning.

    With regular descaling, many tankless heaters last 15 to 20 years. Neglected units in hard water can fail in under half that time.

    The descaling process involves shutting off the unit, isolating the water lines and connecting a circulating pump to flush a mild acidic solution through the heat exchanger. This solution dissolves mineral deposits like calcium and magnesium, which are common in hard water, improving heat transfer efficiency.

    If you have isolation valves, a pump and the right solution, a handy homeowner can do it. A pro visit also checks gas pressure, venting, sensors and condensate, which a DIY flush skips.

    Many tankless water heater manufacturers require proof of regular, professional maintenance to keep your unit's warranty valid. Skipping annual service can void the warranty, leaving you responsible for costly repairs that would otherwise be covered. Always check your own warranty terms.

    Most visits take one to two hours, including the descaling cycle. Installing isolation valves on the same trip adds roughly an hour.

    A standard tankless water heater maintenance service, including flushing and inspection, usually takes 1 to 2 hours. The exact time can vary depending on the unit's condition, the amount of scale buildup and any unforeseen issues encountered during the service.
