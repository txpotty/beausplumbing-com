    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Lake Charles, LA

    The meter box is where a lot of leaks start. Compression fittings there work loose over time. Before we touch anything, we figure out whether the leak is before or after the meter, since the utility is in charge of the meter and the pipe feeding it. If it is ours to fix, we fix it. That often means new fittings and a new customer shutoff valve on the same visit.

    Commercial Water Line Repair in Lake Charles, LA

    Commercial service lines are often 1 1/2 to 4 inch pipe feeding a meter bank, a backflow assembly and sometimes a fire line. A break shuts the business down. Beau's Plumbing can schedule repairs early in the morning or after closing, with large-diameter couplings and fittings already loaded. We also arrange the backflow test and inspection that most water providers require after work on the commercial side of the meter.

    After-Hours Water Line Breaks in Lake Charles, LA

    Water lines tend to break on a Saturday night. Ours is a 24 hour line.     Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Lake Charles, LA

    A full break can flood the yard and leave the house dry at the same time. Shutting the water off comes first. Same-day repairs are the norm. We photograph the damage for your insurance or HOA.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Lake Charles

    Underground leaks hide well. Many people find out from the bill before they see anything in the yard. Others notice a patch of grass that grows greener than the rest, or ground that stays soft long after the sprinklers shut off. We confirm the leak at the meter, trace the pipe route and listen along it. The dig follows the sound.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Lake Charles

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Rocks pressing against the pipe wear pinholes over time, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. A two-foot section is typical. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, and we check the exposed pipe on both sides for green crust or soft spots.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Lake Charles

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Newer construction mostly uses PEX and plastic drains, while older houses often mix copper, galvanized, and cast iron. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. We price each one for you.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Lake Charles, LA

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Two or more leaks on galvanized is a replacement. Age matters too, since most service lines past 40 years old are living on borrowed time. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. We show you the pipe we exposed.
