    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Sewer Line in Lake Charles

    Clearing a main sewer line through an accessible cleanout typically costs $200 to $550. Hydro jetting runs about $400 to $1,000 depending on length and buildup. Adding a camera inspection is typically $150 to $350 more.

    Homeowners are typically responsible for the lateral from the house to the property line or city tap. If the clog is in the city main, the municipality handles it. Our camera and locator help show where the blockage is.

    While minor clogs in individual drains can sometimes be resolved with DIY methods, a main sewer line clog is best left to professionals. Attempting to clear it yourself without the right tools can push the blockage further or damage your pipes, especially with older plumbing systems.

    Minor sewer line cleanings typically do not require a permit. However, if the clog calls for extensive repair work, such as replacing a section of the sewer line, a permit from your local permitting department will likely be required for code compliance. Beau's Plumbing handles all required permitting.

    Rental drum augers can be dangerous and may get stuck or damage old pipe. Main sewer clogs also involve sewage exposure. It is safer and often more effective to call a professional.

    Common signs include multiple drains backing up simultaneously, sewage odors coming from drains, gurgling sounds from toilets or drains, and water backing up in unusual places like the shower when the toilet is flushed. These indicate a blockage further down your main sewer line, not just a localized drain issue.

    Most main line clearings take one to two hours. Severe root intrusion or lines without a cleanout can take longer. Camera inspection adds about 30 to 60 minutes.
