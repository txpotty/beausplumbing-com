    What a Standard Swap Includes in Lake Charles

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected. Then the leveling. The base gets caulked, with a small gap left at the back so any future leak shows up instead of hiding under the floor.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Lake Charles

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. We match the new bowl to the existing carrier whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Lake Charles, LA

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank. We schedule around your hours. We match the bowl to the gallons per flush on the valve, since a mismatch leaves waste behind or wastes water.

    ADA and Commercial Toilet Replacement in Lake Charles, LA

    Public restrooms need bowls rated for heavy use, and many need to meet ADA height and clearance rules when a fixture is replaced. Clearances get checked before we order anything. Adding a sensor flush valve during the swap takes little extra time, and they cut down on unflushed bowls.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Lake Charles, LA

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They cost more and weigh more. A one-piece toilet usually takes two people to carry upstairs, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Lake Charles

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Replacement parts are on every hardware shelf. If the floor is uneven, we shim it level, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    Choosing a Toilet Brand in Lake Charles, LA

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield all make solid toilets, and each line has a few models we steer people away from because of weak flushes or hard-to-find parts. We will tell you which ones. What fits your bathroom comes down to rough-in and bowl shape, and we check both before you buy.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Lake Charles, LA

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Take the measurement from the wall to the closet bolts, not from the baseboard trim. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. Offset flanges can fix a small mismatch.
