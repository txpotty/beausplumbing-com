    What We Fix on Toilets in Lake Charles, LA

    Toilet Brands We Service in Lake Charles

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield make up most of the toilets we open, and each one uses its own flapper size or flush valve design. The wrong flapper will leak. We read the model number cast inside the tank wall and stock the common ones on every truck.

    Running Toilet Fixes in Lake Charles

    Phantom refills, the tank topping itself off for a few seconds in the middle of the night, almost always mean a slow leak at the flapper. The water level drops until the fill valve kicks on again. Other times the refill tube sits too deep in the overflow pipe, which siphons the tank. We clip it above the water line.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Lake Charles, LA

    Braided supply lines last years, but the plastic nut on the toilet end can crack and a bulge in the braid means the inner hose is about to burst. Every visit includes a look at the supply line. Bulges get replaced on the spot. Old vinyl or solid plastic lines get swapped for braided stainless with metal nuts.

    Toilet Flush Repair in Lake Charles, LA

    Two or three tries to get a full flush points to a flapper closing too early, a chain hooked in the wrong hole, scale in the rim jets, or a cracked overflow tube letting water bypass the bowl. Each one has a tell. We watch a full cycle with the lid off and replace the flush valve if the overflow tube is cracked, since it cannot be patched.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Lake Charles

    Food coloring in the tank is the cheapest test we run. Should the bowl turn color within fifteen minutes without a flush, water is leaking past the flapper or the flush valve seat, and the toilet is quietly refilling itself all day and night. A new flapper fixes most of these.

    Flush Mechanism Repair in Lake Charles, LA

    A weak flush usually means the tank is not filling to the fill line, the chain has too much slack, or the flapper is closing before the tank empties. We bring the water up to the line stamped on the overflow tube and adjust the chain to about half an inch of slack. Handles break too. A cracked handle arm or a stripped mounting nut takes about five minutes to replace.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Lake Charles

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports. We swap them for an adjustable fill valve from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the height so the critical level mark sits at least an inch above the overflow. It is a twenty-minute job. You will notice the difference the first night, because the tank stops hissing.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Lake Charles

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. Before replacing parts, we test the sensor and the solenoid. Batteries are the cheap fix. Ghost flushing on a sensor valve usually means the eye needs adjusting or cleaning.
