    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Lake Charles, LA

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Lake Charles

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. In elevator pits, the pump usually needs an oil-sensing control so it will not discharge hydraulic fluid. Duplex systems and high-water alarms get tested on a fixed schedule.     Annual Sump Pump Inspections in Lake Charles

    Once a year is the minimum. We check the motor, impeller, float and discharge line on every annual visit. Then we fill the pit and time how long the pump takes to empty it, because a slow drawdown is the earliest sign of a worn impeller or a partly blocked line.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Lake Charles

    Brand matters less than people think, but parts do not cross over cleanly between Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and Little Giant. Our plumber reads the model number off the label and orders the matching float or seal kit. A submersible Zoeller and a pedestal Wayne get two different checklists. We know both.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Lake Charles, LA

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. That is the moment a backup pump earns its keep. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Lake Charles

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. A failed valve sends that water back down for the pump to move again. Motors wear out faster. Our plumber tests the valve, confirms there is a weep hole below it to prevent air lock, and checks each joint to the wall.

    Home Sump Pump Maintenance in Lake Charles

    A typical house pit is about 18 inches across and two feet deep, and most of what goes wrong happens inside it.     Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Lake Charles

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. Even so, we look for cracks in the casting and make sure the float moves freely. Common Zoeller parts ride on our trucks.
