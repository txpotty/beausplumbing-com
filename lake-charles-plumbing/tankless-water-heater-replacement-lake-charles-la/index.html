    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Lake Charles

    When your existing tankless has a cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board that is not worth repairing, a direct replacement is often the quickest fix. We match the new unit to your current gas line, venting type and mounting location whenever possible. Many Rinnai and Navien models share similar footprints, which keeps wall patching to a minimum. We also replace the old service valves if they are corroded or stuck. Most like-for-like swaps are finished in four to six hours.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Lake Charles, LA

    If you are tired of running the tap waiting for hot water, a tankless with a built-in recirculation pump can bring heat to the fixture in seconds. Navien A series and Rinnai units with internal pumps can use a dedicated return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. We program schedules so the pump runs only during your morning and evening peaks. That keeps gas use reasonable while saving thousands of gallons of wasted water a year. This upgrade is popular in long single-story ranch homes.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Lake Charles, LA

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can wear out plastic covers and wiring over time. Raised homes with pipes under the floor are the most vulnerable during a cold snap, since cold air moves freely underneath. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to shield the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Lake Charles, LA

    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet. The catch is that tankless units usually need a larger gas line, often three quarter inch or bigger, plus new venting. We evaluate your meter size and pipe run before quoting so there are no surprises mid-job. We also cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer used. Plan for a conversion to take a full day.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Lake Charles

    The cost for tankless water heater replacement in Lake Charles, LA typically ranges from $3,000 to $6,000, depending on the unit's brand, capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed changes to gas lines, electrical or venting. This estimate includes both the new unit and professional installation. We will provide a detailed quote upfront after assessing your needs.

    Well-maintained tankless units can last 15 to 20 years. In hard water areas without regular descaling, heat exchangers can fail far sooner. Annual flushing makes the largest difference.

    To prepare for tankless water heater replacement, make sure the area around your existing unit is clear and accessible for our plumbers. If possible, clear a path to the installation site and keep pets or children away from the work area. We will handle draining the old unit and all other technical preparations.

    A direct swap typically takes four to six hours. A tank to tankless conversion with new gas and venting usually takes a full day.

    Frequent error codes, fluctuating temperature, water leaking from inside the cabinet and a unit older than 15 years are common signs. If the heat exchanger is leaking, replacement is usually the better value.

    A standard tankless water heater replacement, if you are upgrading from an existing tankless unit, usually takes 4 to 8 hours. If you are converting from a traditional tank water heater, the process can take longer, possibly a full day, due to extra plumbing, gas or electrical changes required for proper installation and venting.

    New tankless water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the heat exchanger, usually 10 to 15 years, and on parts, usually 3 to 5 years.
