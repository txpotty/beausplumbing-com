    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Lake Charles

    CIPP lining is a proven trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is excellent for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Lake Charles.

    Commercial Trenchless Pipe Repair in Lake Charles

    Commercial properties often have sewer and storm lines under parking lots, sidewalks and buildings. Trenchless repair avoids costly concrete demolition and disruption to tenants and customers.     Sectional Point Repair in Lake Charles

    When damage is confined to a specific section of pipe, sectional point repair is an ideal trenchless solution.     Pipe Bursting Services in Lake Charles, LA
