    Emergency Tank Troubleshooting in Lake Charles

    Standard Tank Tune-Up in Lake Charles

    A standard tune-up starts with the fuel off and the cold supply closed, then a full drain through the bottom valve until the water runs clear. Figure on half an hour or a bit more for the drain. We check the heating elements or burner, read the thermostat setting and look at every fitting on top of the tank for green crust or drips.     Relief Valve Testing in Lake Charles

    Temperature and pressure relief valves on residential tanks are typically rated to open at 150 psi or 210 degrees, and they can stick shut after years of mineral buildup. Our tech lifts the lever, lets water flow, then watches it close. If it keeps dripping, we replace it. We also confirm the discharge pipe runs full size to within six inches of the floor or to an approved drain, which is what plumbing codes ask for.

    Expansion Tank Service in Lake Charles, LA

    A thermal expansion tank is the small steel tank on the cold line above the heater, and it gives heated water somewhere to go when a check valve or pressure reducer closes the system. Our tech checks the air charge with a tire gauge. Precharge should match your house water pressure. A waterlogged expansion tank sounds solid when you knock on it, and it lets pressure spike every time the burner fires.

    Rental and Commercial Tank Logs in Lake Charles

    Landlords and property managers with a dozen units need to know which tank is next to fail. Each tank we touch goes into a log.
