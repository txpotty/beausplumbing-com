    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Lake Charles

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. This not only waterproofs the walls but also controls humidity, preventing mold growth and wood rot in the crawl space. Long, hot summers and heavy humidity are the main stress on plumbing in Louisiana, driving condensation, mold, and hard-working water heaters. An encapsulated crawl space can noticeably improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home.     Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Lake Charles

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. Low-lying areas have a high water table and sinking ground, which can cause sewer lines to sag, crack, or separate over time.
