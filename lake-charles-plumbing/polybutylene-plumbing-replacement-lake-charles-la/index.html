    PEX Pipe Installation in Lake Charles, LA

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost. We keep PEX out of direct sunlight, away from water heater flues and recessed light housings and support it at the spacing the manufacturer calls for, which protects both the pipe and the warranty.

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Lake Charles

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. Attic fittings fail hard. After an emergency repair we schedule the full replacement, since removing the poly system is the only lasting fix.

    The Cost of Polybutylene Replacement in Lake Charles, LA

    Price depends mostly on house size, the number of bathrooms, attic access and whether you pick PEX or copper. PEX costs less. One burst can cost more in drywall, flooring and cabinets than the full replacement, not counting your deductible. Our estimate lists labor, materials, permit and patching separately.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Lake Charles

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. It is rigid, handles heat well and has decades of history in homes. Plan on a higher price. Some water can pinhole copper over time, so we look at your water and any past leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Lake Charles, LA

    Some homeowners in Lake Charles, LA want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. Our copper replacements use thicker Type L pipe with lead-free soldered or pressed joints, not the thinner Type M. Copper costs more and takes longer, since each turn needs a fitting, but it tolerates heat and sunlight well and has a long track record.

    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Lake Charles

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. Watch the water meter. With every fixture off, a spinning leak indicator on the meter means water is going somewhere, and we use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to find it without opening walls at random. Once we find it, a temporary fix buys time to schedule the repipe.

    Permits for Polybutylene Replacement in Lake Charles

    A whole-house poly replacement in Lake Charles is a repipe, and it usually needs a plumbing permit. The permit is on us. The inspector looks at the new lines with the walls still open, checking supports, nail plates, shutoffs and the pressure test before anything is patched. That record helps later with insurance and resale.

    Emergency Polybutylene Burst Repair in Lake Charles

    Poly fittings tend to let go during sharp temperature swings, when the pipe expands and contracts more than usual. Hard freezes are rare in Louisiana, but when one arrives, homes built for heat often have exposed pipes that burst. Close the main if a line bursts, and call us to stop it, patch it and get water back on. A full replacement comes next. Living with poly until the next failure costs the most in the end.
