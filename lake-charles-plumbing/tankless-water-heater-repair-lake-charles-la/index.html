    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Lake Charles

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, and they hold temperature off thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. We pull the screen, read sensor values in the service menu and compare thermistor resistance to the chart. Usually under an hour.

    Igniter & Flame Rod Repair in Lake Charles, LA

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. A rod coated in oxide reads low on a microamp meter and the board shuts the gas valve after a few seconds. We clean or replace the rod, check the igniter for cracks or weak spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Lake Charles, LA

    Rinnai units are common, and most of their trouble starts with scale. The LC code is the unit's own warning that the exchanger needs a flush. We also see dirty flame rods behind a code 11 or 12 and tired combustion fans behind a code 61, and we test every one against Rinnai's published values. Parts come from the factory list.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Lake Charles, LA

    Condensing tankless heaters pull extra heat out of the exhaust and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. Clogged traps shut the unit down. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.
