    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Kenner

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Water Softener Replacement in Kenner, LA

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. We remove the old unit and install a new metered model, then check the loop and drain line while everything is apart. Salt use drops. You will notice soft water the same day.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Kenner, LA

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops.     Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Kenner, LA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. Beau's Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. They work best on very hard water.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Kenner

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Little upkeep is needed. They suit moderate hardness.
