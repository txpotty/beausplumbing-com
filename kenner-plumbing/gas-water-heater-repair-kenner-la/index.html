    Tank Leaks and Pressure Problems in Kenner

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure. Private wells serve many rural homes, and sediment, iron, and bacterial contamination make periodic well testing a good habit. While some leaks may call for a replacement, minor leaks or pressure relief valve issues can often be repaired. Our team checks the extent of the damage, inspects the anode rod and addresses pressure imbalances to prevent further problems in your Kenner home.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Kenner

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane instead of natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and improper conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for proper pressure.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Kenner

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it senses vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and trip the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as required.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Kenner, LA

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something fails. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Kenner, LA

    Atmospheric gas water heaters depend on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.
