    Tankless Descaling and Service Work in Kenner, LA

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Kenner

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. The trap gets cleaned and the media checked. We replace spent media. Where the line runs outside, we check that it is supported and protected.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Kenner

    Grit gets in. The small screen on the cold inlet catches sand and loose scale after a water main repair. Units with an air intake filter collect dust and lint, which can push the burner into a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Kenner

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. They see heavy use, and scale builds faster than at home. Each heater gets descaled, and we check that the controller spreads the load evenly. Quarterly service is common.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Kenner, LA

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. Gas pressure at the inlet gets read with a manometer at full fire and checked against the rating plate. We check the flue for blockages. You get a written report with the readings, so next year's visit has something to compare against.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Kenner, LA

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. We add a pair of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines. Most kits include a relief valve port as well. After that, every flush takes minutes to set up.

    Noritz Descaling in Kenner, LA

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. We use a descaler that the manufacturer approves and keep the cycle inside the time limit in the manual, because too strong a mix can etch the metal. While the cycle runs, we check the fan motor and exhaust path.

    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Kenner, LA

    We service Bosch, Rheem, Takagi and Navien heaters around Kenner, and each one has its own filter locations and code list in the manual. We follow the manufacturer's steps, not a generic checklist. That matters for warranty claims down the road.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Kenner, LA

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations. Then we check the fan and the combustion air path. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.
