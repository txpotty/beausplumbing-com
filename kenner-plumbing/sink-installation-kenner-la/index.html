    How We Set New Sinks in Kenner, LA

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Kenner, LA

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. The faucet holes have to match the faucet you picked, whether that is a single hole, a 4 inch centerset or an 8 inch widespread, and the overflow has to line up with the pop-up drain. We check the spec sheet first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Kenner, LA

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. The top sits on the cabinet in a bead of silicone, the faucet and pop-up go on first, and the trap arm usually needs a small adjustment to meet the new tailpiece. Backsplash comes last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Kenner

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Strainers are often fused on, the disposal has to come off, and the dishwasher hose needs a fresh high loop. Then the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Commercial Sink Installation in Kenner, LA

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. Three-compartment sinks usually drain indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water within easy reach of every prep station on the line. We read the plan review first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Kenner

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. Each bowl drains through its own trap or through a continuous waste to a shared trap, and the second faucet gets new stops. We check the vent size too. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Kenner, LA

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The supply stops and the trap sit right behind the column, so we center the drain stub, use chrome or polished brass trap parts, and set the stops tight to the wall where the pedestal can hide them. Blocking in the wall is a must. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Kenner, LA

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Setting the faucet and drain while the bowl is on sawhorses is faster and seals better than working in the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Kenner

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. An even reveal around a polished cutout takes shims and patience before the clips are tightened. A crooked reveal never goes away. We check it from both ends of the counter.
