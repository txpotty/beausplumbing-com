    Exterior Foundation Leak Solutions in Kenner

    Many foundation leaks start from exterior factors like poor drainage, overflowing gutters, or saturated soil. We inspect your home's exterior to identify these culprits. Our solutions include grading adjustments, French drain installation, and downspout extensions to move water away from your foundation. Proper exterior water management is key for preventing hydrostatic pressure buildup against your foundation walls in Kenner and keeping water out.

    Water Line Reroute in Kenner

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This skips breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for easy patching.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Kenner, LA

    Many older homes in Kenner, LA have copper water lines beneath their foundations, which can corrode over time due to soil conditions or water chemistry. Private wells serve many rural homes, and sediment, iron, and bacteria make periodic well testing a good habit. When these copper pipes develop pinhole leaks or cracks, they require specialized repair. Our technicians repair or replace sections of damaged copper piping, ensuring a durable seal that withstands pressure and ground movement and helps prevent future leaks.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Kenner

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs with steel cables under high tension. Cutting into these slabs takes care and scanning to avoid hitting a cable. We use ground-penetrating radar or other scanning methods before opening the floor. When the risk is too great, rerouting the plumbing is usually the safer option.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Kenner

    Slab leaks are common in homes built on concrete foundations, often caused by shifting soil or corroded pipes underneath. Our technicians use low-impact methods like electronic leak detection and hydrostatic pressure testing to find these hidden leaks. Once located, we can perform targeted repairs, either by slab tunneling or direct access, limiting disruption to your home. We work with many pipe materials, including copper, PEX, and PVC, ensuring a durable repair for your Kenner property.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Kenner

    Newer homes often feature PEX or PVC plumbing, which, while durable, can still develop foundation leaks due to ground movement or improper installation. Beau's Plumbing has the tools and expertise to repair leaks in these plastic piping systems. We use proper fusion and solvent welding techniques to make strong, leak-proof connections, restoring the integrity of your plumbing system beneath your Kenner foundation.
