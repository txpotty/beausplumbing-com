    Leaky Toilet Repair in Kenner

    A leaky toilet can waste hundreds of gallons of water each year, increasing your utility bills and potentially causing water damage. Our technicians in Kenner are experts at identifying the source of leaks, whether it's a cracked tank or bowl, a faulty wax ring, or a worn-out fill valve. We use specialized dyes and pressure tests to reliably locate hidden leaks and provide durable repairs, often involving the replacement of seals, gaskets, or the entire flushing mechanism. Ignoring a leak can lead to structural damage or mold growth, so quick repair is crucial.

    Leaking Toilet Repair in Kenner

    Toilet leaks can cause serious water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can start from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line. Common culprits include a worn-out wax ring, loose bolts, or cracked porcelain. We thoroughly inspect all potential leak points, replacing seals, tightening connections, or recommending bowl replacement if the porcelain is damaged, preventing further water intrusion.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Kenner, LA

    Water around the base usually means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. On slab foundations, the flange often sits on concrete and can crack when bolts are overtightened. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a new seal. We also check for soft flooring so you know if any water damage needs attention.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Kenner, LA

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem may be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you honestly if replacement is the better fix.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Kenner, LA

    The gaskets and seals within your toilet tank and at its base are essential for preventing leaks. Over time, these rubber components can degrade, crack, or become brittle due to age and exposure to water, especially where the water is hard. We specialize in replacing worn-out flapper seals, tank-to-bowl gaskets, and the wax ring at the toilet's base, which is essential for preventing leaks onto your bathroom floor and maintaining a watertight seal.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Kenner, LA

    A hairline crack in the tank or a bulging braided supply line can let go without warning and flood a bathroom. We inspect the tank, tank-to-bowl bolts and gasket, and the supply line during every visit. If the porcelain is cracked we will price a replacement tank or new toilet, and we upgrade old plastic or vinyl supply lines to braided stainless.

    Toilet Flush Mechanism Repair in Kenner, LA

    If your toilet isn't flushing properly, it could be due to issues with the flush chain, handle, flapper, or fill valve. A weak flush might mean the tank isn't filling correctly, while a loose handle might not be engaging the flapper. Our plumbers are skilled at diagnosing these internal tank problems, replacing worn or broken components like flush valves, fill valves, and toilet handles to restore full and effective flushing power.
