    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Kenner, LA

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Kenner, LA

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Kenner

    Private wells serve many rural homes, and sediment, iron, and bacterial contamination make periodic well testing a good habit. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. Signs include crusted aerators, clogged showerheads, slow toilet fill and heater sediment, and we can test hardness during the visit. You get options, not a pitch. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.

    Plumbing Inspection Costs in Kenner, LA

    A basic plumbing inspection usually costs $150 to $300, and adding a sewer camera usually brings it to $250 to $500. Bigger properties cost more. Compared to an unexpected sewer replacement, which can run $4,000 to $15,000 or more, an inspection is cheap protection, and we give you the exact price when you book.

    Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in Kenner

    Plumbing problems are some of the most expensive surprises after closing. We check supply pipe, record the sewer, date the water heater and test every fixture. Your agent gets the report. Buyers often use the findings to request repairs or credits, and the sewer video by itself can head off a very costly surprise.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Kenner

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Kenner, LA

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one. Certain types need a plan. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Kenner, LA

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Kenner, LA

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. Backflow devices, grease traps, large heaters, flushometers and the main sewer are all part of it. Records matter for inspectors. Managers use the report to fix problems before an inspector or water provider flags them.
