    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Kenner, LA

    A leaking hot line heats the concrete over it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. We use it to narrow the search before the acoustic check.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Kenner

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it.     Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Kenner, LA

    Older homes in Kenner, LA often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both corrode with age.
