    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Kenner

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, while PEX gets a fresh crimp ring or expansion fitting with the right tool for the brand.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Kenner, LA

    Besides repairs, Beau's Plumbing handles service line upgrades when a commercial tenant needs more flow, for example when a restaurant adds fixtures or a gym adds showers.     Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Kenner, LA

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. Once one spot leaks, the pipe on either side is usually just as thin.     Main Water Line Replacement in Kenner, LA

    Replacement beats repair once a line has leaked two or three times, or when it is galvanized or polybutylene. Patches on those materials rarely hold.     Trenchless Water Line Repair in Kenner

    Trenchless work makes sense when the line runs under a driveway, patio or a row of mature shrubs. We dig a pit at the meter and another at the house. A pneumatic boring tool or a cable puller drags HDPE or PEX through the soil, or through the old pipe if it is a straight run, and most jobs wrap up in a single day.

    Main Water Line Repair in Kenner

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth.     Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Kenner
