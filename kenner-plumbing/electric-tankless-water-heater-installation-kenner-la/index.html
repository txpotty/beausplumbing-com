    Electrical Panel Review and Upgrades in Kenner

    Before any electric tankless install, we look hard at the panel. We check service size, free slots and current load, since large units can need over 100 amps. Older panels often fall short. A licensed electrician handles that part.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Kenner

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Some Louisiana groundwater carries iron and sulfur, which can cause rotten egg odors, stains, and buildup in water heaters. We recommend a softener or a scale inhibitor ahead of the unit, and every install gets service valves so the heater can be flushed with a descaling solution once a year. Flushing takes an hour.

    Converting from Tank to Electric Tankless in Kenner, LA

    Converting a tank to electric tankless frees floor space and ends standby loss. We remove the old tank, mount the new unit on the wall, reroute the water lines and add isolation valves, while the electrician runs the new circuits to the unit. Most finish in a day. It looks tidier.
