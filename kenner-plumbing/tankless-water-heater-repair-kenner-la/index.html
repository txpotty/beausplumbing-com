    Tankless Water Heater Error Code Diagnostics in Kenner, LA

    Modern tankless water heaters display error codes to flag specific problems, from flow rate issues to combustion errors. Our experts in Kenner, LA are trained to interpret these codes accurately, using specialized diagnostic tools to pinpoint the exact component failure. This precision allows us to quickly order the correct parts and perform targeted repairs, saving you time and money. We address codes related to burner failures, fan issues and temperature sensor errors, and more.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Kenner

    Tankless heaters rely on flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace faulty parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are accurate.

    Ignition Failure Repair in Kenner, LA

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet. Undersized gas lines are a common hidden cause when a tankless unit replaced a tank. We correct the issue so the unit lights reliably.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Kenner, LA

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and verify proper drainage. PVC venting is also checked for slope and sagging.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Kenner

    When your tankless unit fails to produce hot water, it is often due to issues with the ignition, gas supply or a clogged heat exchanger. Our technicians in Kenner will thoroughly diagnose the root cause, whether it is a faulty igniter, a pressure sensor fault or mineral buildup. We carry common replacement parts for fast fixes, restoring your continuous hot water supply efficiently. We also check for proper venting, which is crucial for the safe operation of your unit.
