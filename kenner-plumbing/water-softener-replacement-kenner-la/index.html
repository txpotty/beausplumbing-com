    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Kenner

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water around the clock. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but avoid hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Kenner

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the best salt type for your system.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Kenner, LA

    For homeowners in Kenner, LA seeking an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach. These units, sometimes called descalers, condition water to prevent mineral scale without removing the minerals or adding salt. While they do not produce soft feeling water, they effectively protect pipes and appliances from scale. We can replace your existing salt-free system or help you switch from a salt-based unit.
