    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Kenner

    Figure on roughly $800 to $2,000 for most basement sump pump installs in Kenner. At the low end you get a pump swap in a pit that is already there, while a new pit cut through the slab, a long discharge run, or a battery backup pushes the total toward the top of that range. You get a written price after we look.

    Plan on 4 to 8 hours for a standard install. Digging a new pit and patching the slab can take the whole day, and tricky electrical access or a long discharge run through a finished wall adds time on top of that. We tell you up front.

    Labor on our sump pump installs is warrantied, typically for one year. Each pump also has a factory warranty, usually one to five years depending on the brand and model, and we write the exact coverage on your invoice so you know which part to call about if something fails. Keep the receipt.

    Changing out a pump in an existing pit takes about two to three hours. A new pit with slab cutting and a concrete patch takes most of a day, since the concrete needs a little time to firm up before the pump goes into service.

    Discharge belongs in the yard, a dry well, or an approved drainage area at least 10 feet from the foundation. Tying a sump pump into the sanitary sewer is not allowed in most places, and it can draw a fine.

    For some homeowners, a same-model pump swap is a fair weekend job. New pits and backup systems are a different matter, because those mistakes end in flooding, cracked slabs or electrical hazards, and fixing them costs more than paying for the install up front. Those belong to a pro.

    We strongly recommend professional installation. The job combines electrical, plumbing and concrete work, and a bad seal on the lid, an undersized pit or a discharge that drains toward the house can leave you worse off than having no pump at all. Mistakes show up in storms.

    Most standard installs finish within 4 to 8 hours. Long discharge runs or a new pit can add several hours, and if we need a new circuit from the panel, an electrician may need to come out the same day, which can push the job into the evening. We give a timeline at the visit.
