    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Kenner

    Water heater installation in Kenner, LA runs about $1,200 to $4,000 or more. The biggest factors are whether it is a tank or tankless unit, its size, its fuel and whether the gas line or panel needs work. Tankless sits at the high end. You get a written quote after we see the space.

    Doing it yourself means handling gas, 240-volt power and pressurized water at once. A bad vent joint or a missing relief line can lead to carbon monoxide, scalding or a tank failure, and many local codes require a licensed plumber anyway. Hire a pro.

    A like-for-like tank swap usually takes three to four hours. Converting from tank to tankless, or any job with major gas, vent or electrical changes, can take a full day or more. You will know before we start.

    Tank heaters usually last 10 to 15 years, and tankless units can go 20 or more. Hard water and skipped maintenance shorten that, while yearly flushing and anode checks stretch it a good deal past the average. Flush it once a year.

    Permits are common for water heater work. Louisiana has a statewide plumbing code built on a national model code, and local building departments handle permits and inspections. Beau's Plumbing handles the permit and the inspection in Kenner.

    Household size and peak demand decide the size. Two to four people usually do fine with a 40 or 50 gallon tank, while bigger families often need 60 to 80 gallons or a tankless unit sized by flow rate. Tankless is sized by GPM.

    Homes with a pressure reducing valve or check valve usually need one. It keeps pressure spikes off your plumbing.
