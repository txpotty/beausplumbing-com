    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Kenner

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are common problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders.     Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Kenner

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. If you cannot get enough hot water, the anti-scald limit stop may be set too low or the cartridge may be worn. We repair or replace pressure-balance and thermostatic valves and adjust limit stops. Your shower will hold a steady, consistent temperature.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Kenner, LA

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints. Then we repair the source, whether that means resealing a drain, replacing a valve or rebuilding a pan. On slab homes, these leaks can damage flooring in nearby rooms.

    Leaky Shower Faucet Repair in Kenner

    A dripping shower faucet can waste hundreds of gallons of water a year and drive up your utility bill. We will ensure the new components are correctly installed, stopping the drip and saving you water.

    Leaky Shower Head Repair in Kenner

    A dripping shower head wastes water and can be incredibly annoying. Our plumbers can quickly identify the cause, whether it is a worn-out washer, O-ring, or a faulty cartridge.     Clogged Shower Drain Solutions in Kenner, LA

    Slow or standing water in your shower is a clear sign of a clogged drain. These clogs are typically caused by a buildup of hair, soap scum, and sometimes mineral deposits. We also offer advice on preventative measures to keep your shower drains flowing freely.

    Leaking Shower Valve Repair in Kenner

We replace the cartridge with a genuine part and lubricate the valve with silicone grease. This stops the drip and makes the handle turn smoothly again.
