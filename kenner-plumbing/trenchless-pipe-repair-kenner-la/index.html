    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Kenner

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Kenner yard.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Kenner
