    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Kenner, LA

    Strong storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out.     Sump Pump Replacement in Kenner, LA

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors. We replace old pumps with new models and inspect the pit, check valve and discharge line. Upgrading to a higher capacity pump may help if your old one could not keep up. We test the new pump by filling the pit with water. Replacement before the rainy season is a smart move.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Kenner

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often runs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups usually add $500 to $1,500.

    In many cities, a plumbing permit is typically required for new sump pump installations or major replacements. This confirms the work meets local building and plumbing codes. Beau's Plumbing handles all necessary permitting in Kenner, LA to ensure your installation is fully compliant.

    Strange noises, constant running, failure to turn on and visible rust are common signs. Testing your pump before storm season helps catch problems early.

    Not every home does, but homes with basements, crawl spaces, sunken patios or lots that collect storm runoff can benefit. A sump pump protects against sudden storm flooding.

    Most standard sump pump installations can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. However, if a new sump pit needs to be excavated or if there are complex plumbing or electrical adjustments, the process might take a full day. We always aim for efficiency without sacrificing quality.

    Most manufacturers offer warranties from 3 to 5 years, and some premium models carry longer coverage. We also stand behind our installation workmanship.

    It's recommended to have your sump pump inspected and cleaned once a year, ideally before the rainy season. This maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning the pit and testing the discharge line to confirm it's free of obstructions. Regular checks prevent surprise failures.
