    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Kenner

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Kenner

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. That saves some labor. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Kenner, LA

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Batteries need replacing every few years. They matter most below finished rooms.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Kenner, LA

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Kenner

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We install exterior basins with pumps for those spots. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Kenner

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. A flooded elevator pit shuts down a building. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Kenner, LA

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. People with carpet downstairs buy these. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Kenner

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. Lids close flat over them. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Kenner

    Most Kenner, LA sump pump jobs land between $600 and $1,800. That covers the pump, the check valve and labor. New pits cost more. Every line item is on the quote.

    Grinding, rattling or a pump that runs with no water in the pit are all warning signs. Visible rust, a float that sticks or a pump that cycles every minute during dry weather usually means the end is near. A flooded basement settles it.

    Swapping a pump in an existing pit usually takes two or three hours. New pit installs can run all day.

    Discharge should end at least ten feet from the foundation, on ground that slopes away. Most cities do not allow sump water in the sanitary sewer.

    Submersibles are quieter and stronger, so they suit heavy use. A pedestal pump costs less and you can reach the motor without lifting it out, but it makes more noise and moves less water.

    For some homeowners, swapping a pump in a working pit is doable. New pits, discharge routing and electrical changes are better left to a plumber.

    We install Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and a few other dependable brands. The model depends on pit size and inflow.

    Plan on seven to ten years for a well-kept pump. Heavy use shortens that. We can check yours.
