    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Kenner

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Kenner

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. We price both options.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Kenner

    Electric water heaters in Kenner usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Hot water is back the same day.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Kenner

    Running out of hot water often means the family has outgrown it. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can keep up with more showers back to back, and in some homes a 50-gallon high-recovery gas unit does the job of a 75-gallon standard tank without taking up any more floor space. Bigger is not always needed.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Kenner

    Standard tank heaters remain the most common type, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. When a tank starts leaking at the base, making loud popping sounds or giving rusty hot water, we replace it like-for-like or resize it, and the whole job usually takes two to four hours from shutoff to first hot shower. You shower tonight.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Kenner

    Businesses in Kenner run their water heaters harder than homes do, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons, laundromats and offices each have a different demand curve, and we size the new unit by peak gallons per hour and the temperature health code requires, which can mean a larger tank, a booster or a rack of tankless units. We schedule around your hours.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Kenner, LA

    A leaking tank can empty itself onto the floor faster than most people expect. If water is coming from the heater, shut off the cold supply valve on top, turn the gas valve to off or flip the breaker, and open a hot tap to relieve pressure, then call and we will try to have a plumber and a new tank at your home that day. Do not wait it out.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Kenner

    Newer construction mostly uses PEX and plastic drains, while older homes often mix copper, galvanized, and cast iron. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Moving a 50-gallon tank through a pull-down ladder or small opening takes two people, and code wants a pan with a drain line, a platform and a light. We bring the help.
