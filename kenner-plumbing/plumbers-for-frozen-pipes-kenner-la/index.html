    When a faucet only trickles or nothing comes out after a cold night, ice is blocking the line. We open the faucet and find the frozen section. Metal pipe thaws fast with electric current, and plastic lines get slower, gentler heat. Patience pays here.

    Frozen Water Line Diagnosis in Kenner, LA

    Finding the frozen spot takes the most skill. A thermal camera speeds it up. By checking which fixtures work and which do not, then scanning walls and ceilings with the camera, we can usually narrow the ice to a few feet of pipe and thaw it without cutting into the wall.

    Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in Kenner, LA

    Insulation slows heat loss, but it does not add heat. Exposed runs get heat cable. We install self-regulating cable that adjusts its output to the pipe temperature, plugged into a GFCI outlet, and cover it with insulation so it uses less power and keeps the pipe above freezing on the coldest nights.

    Residential Frozen Pipe Thawing in Kenner

    A faucet that trickles or goes dry on a cold morning points to ice somewhere in its supply line. We trace the line back with a thermal camera to find the cold spot. Once we know where it is, we thaw it with controlled heat while the faucet stays open, so the melting water has a way out and pressure cannot build behind the ice.
