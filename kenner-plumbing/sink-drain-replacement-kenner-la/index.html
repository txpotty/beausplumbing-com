    Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in Kenner

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. If your laundry or utility sink sits below the sewer line, its drain runs to a sink pump or a sewage ejector, and we check the check valve and the discharge line when we replace the trap. Pumps fail quietly. A gurgling pit is a clue.

    P-Trap Replacement in Kenner

    Thin chrome traps are the ones we replace most. They rust inside and their nuts seize, so we cut them out and fit heavy-wall PVC or polished brass. Then it gets a full flow test. Total time is often under an hour.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Kenner

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. The putty under a 3-1/2 inch strainer dries out, the locknut rusts, and water seeps down the outside of the tailpiece where it looks like the trap is leaking even when it is not. We replace the strainer and trap together. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Push-Button Drain Installation in Kenner, LA

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. A press on the cap opens and closes them, and a slotted body only goes in sinks with an overflow. Cleaning is simpler with no linkage. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.

    Leaking Drain Assembly Replacement in Kenner

    Water on the cabinet floor needs tracking, not guessing. After drying everything we fill and drain the bowl and watch each joint with a dry towel. Supply leaks look similar. We check those too.

    Corroded Drain Pipe Replacement in Kenner, LA

    Galvanized drain arms rust from the inside. An old steel stub can crumble at the threads, so we cut it back and join new PVC with a shielded coupling. We handle cast iron the same way. Sometimes that means opening the wall.

    Kitchen Drain and Disposal Tie-In in Kenner

    Disposals change the kitchen drain. The disposal outlet must sit above the trap, the dishwasher hose needs a high loop or air gap, and nothing can pull sideways. We set them all at once. Then the disposal runs with water while we watch.

    Utility Sink Drain Replacement in Kenner

    Laundry tubs see lint and paint water. Over time that wears the thin plastic drain and trap on a utility sink until it cracks, and lint from the washer standpipe often builds up in the trap so badly that it never drains at full speed again. A 1-1/2 inch trap with a cleanout plug goes in. A lint trap on the washer hose helps.
