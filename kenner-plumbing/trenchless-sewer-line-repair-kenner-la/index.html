    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Kenner, LA

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Louisiana homeowners.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Technologies in Kenner

    Beau's Plumbing uses proven trenchless technologies from trusted brands like Perma-Liner and NuFlow. Using these materials and equipment means your trenchless sewer line repair in Kenner is not only low impact but also a high-quality, long-lasting solution, backed by manufacturer warranties.

    Pipe Bursting in Kenner, LA

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is ideal for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Kenner

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We determine the material on camera before recommending a method.
