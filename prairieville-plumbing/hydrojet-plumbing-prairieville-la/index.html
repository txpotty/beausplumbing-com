    Understanding Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Prairieville, LA

    While snaking can punch a hole through a clog, hydrojetting in Prairieville, LA provides a complete pipe cleaning. Hydrojetting, conversely, scours the entire interior of the pipe, removing debris, grease, and scale, offering a more lasting solution to your drainage issues.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Prairieville

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under floors and slabs. Snaking clears a path, but the grease quickly builds back. Jetting with a rotating nozzle peels that layer off and flushes it downstream. Homeowners who cook often and restaurants benefit the most from regular kitchen line jetting.
