    Outdoor Kitchen and BBQ Gas Lines in Prairieville

    Built-in grills, side burners and pizza ovens are a favorite part of backyard living.     Gas Water Heater and Tankless Line Upgrades in Prairieville, LA

    Tankless water heaters often need up to 199,000 BTU, far more than the 40,000 BTU a typical tank heater uses. Many existing 1/2 inch lines cannot supply that demand, which causes error codes and lukewarm water. We calculate the full house load and upsize the line to 3/4 inch or larger as needed. This upgrade is one of the most important steps in a successful tankless conversion.

    Pool and Spa Heater Gas Lines in Prairieville

    Pool heaters are among the largest gas appliances in a home, often rated at 250,000 to 400,000 BTU. They usually sit at the equipment pad far from the meter, so line size and length are critical. We may recommend a larger main line or a meter upgrade from your gas utility. A properly sized line lets the heater reach its rated output and heat the water quickly.

    Fire Pit and Fireplace Gas Line Installation in Prairieville, LA

    Gas fire pits and fireplaces add comfort on cool evenings without the smoke and ash of wood. We run a properly sized line, install a key valve or shutoff and connect the burner assembly.
