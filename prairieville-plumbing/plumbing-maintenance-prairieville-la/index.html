    Water Heater Maintenance in Prairieville

    Regular water heater maintenance is crucial to preventing sediment buildup. Some Louisiana groundwater carries iron and sulfur, which can cause sulfur smells, stains, and buildup in water heaters. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, boosts efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Prairieville

    Water softeners combat hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance prolongs its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing costly scale damage.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Prairieville, LA

    Heavy downpours can overwhelm drainage systems and cause sewage backups, so backwater valves are a smart upgrade for many homes. A working sump pump is essential for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when unexpected storms hit Prairieville.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Prairieville

    Newer construction mostly uses PEX and plastic drains, while older homes often mix copper, galvanized, and cast iron. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are typical signs. Finding a leak early prevents slab damage and mold.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Prairieville

Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts.
