    Zoeller's sewage line runs from light residential to heavy duty, in cast iron with tethered or vertical float switches. The model comes from the pump curve for your lift, and most basement baths need a four-tenths or half horsepower unit that passes two-inch solids. Bigger lifts need more.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Prairieville, LA

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. Alternating the lead pump spreads wear, and when one fails or flow spikes, the second starts on its own and the panel sounds an alarm. Redundancy matters here.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Prairieville

    Grinder pumps chop waste into a fine slurry first, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. They cost more. For a basement bath near the drain, a standard ejector usually works.
