    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Prairieville, LA

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Prairieville, LA

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Many insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to insure and sell.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Prairieville

    PEX is also a premier choice for hydronic heating systems, including radiant floor heating. Its ability to withstand high temperatures and resist corrosion makes it perfect for circulating hot water. If you are considering radiant heat for a specific area of your home or business in Prairieville, PEX is the pipe of choice.
