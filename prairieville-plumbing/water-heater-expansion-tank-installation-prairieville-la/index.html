    New Expansion Tank Installation in Prairieville

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is likely seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle. Pairing a healthy PRV with a correctly charged expansion tank gives you steady pressure throughout the house.
