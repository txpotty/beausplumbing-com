    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Prairieville

    Tankless units are particularly sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.

    Standard Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Prairieville

    Our standard tank water heater maintenance includes flushing the tank to remove sediment buildup. Some Louisiana groundwater carries iron and sulfur, which can cause sulfur smells, stains, and buildup in water heaters. We also inspect the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and check for any leaks or corrosion on the tank and connections. This service helps prevent early failure and improves heating efficiency, keeping your energy bills down.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Prairieville, LA

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as necessary. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Prairieville

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely and efficiently. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for leaks.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Prairieville

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Prairieville, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's heavily depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing costly tank leaks.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Prairieville

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than you might think. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is crucial for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    Thermostat and Heating Element Checks in Prairieville

    For electric water heaters, we test the thermostat and heating elements to make sure they work correctly and hold an accurate temperature. Faulty elements or thermostats can lead to too little hot water or excessive energy use. For gas water heaters, we inspect the burner assembly and pilot light. Around Prairieville, attic and crawlspace heat can accelerate wear on supply lines, drain pans, and any plastic piping in those spaces.
