    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Every brand has its quirks. Some skirted TOTO models use a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler tanks take a gasket that hardware stores rarely stock. We keep those parts on hand.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Prairieville, LA

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Check for the WaterSense label. A MaP rating of 800 grams or better is what we look for, since a weak low-flow toilet that needs double flushing saves no water at all. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in Prairieville

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. Water comes from a T-valve at the fill line.     Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Prairieville, LA

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. Suddenly the old flange is below the floor. Either an extender ring brings the flange up or a new flange goes in, and relocating a toilet on a slab means saw-cutting concrete to move the drain. Moving it on a wood floor is easier.

    Commercial Toilet Installation in Prairieville, LA

    Commercial restrooms take a beating, so we install vitreous china bowls with flushometer valves rather than tank toilets. A flushometer needs a 1 inch supply and steady pressure. We confirm the building line can deliver the valve's rated flow, often 20 to 25 gallons a minute, before any fixture goes up. Fixture counts follow code.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Prairieville

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. A flange belongs flush with or just above finished flooring. Set it too early and the tile setter has to cut around it, which leaves the flange low and forces a thick seal or an extender later on. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Prairieville

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. They need at least 20 psi to work right. Before suggesting one, we put a gauge on a hose bib, and we mention the noise, because the flush carries through the whole house. They rarely clog.
