    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Houma

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Resin Bed Problems in Houma

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. New resin restores full capacity.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Houma

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush.
