    Root Intrusion Repair in Houma, LA

    Tree and shrub roots go straight for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste.     Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Houma, LA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies usually form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Much of Louisiana sits on soft, expansive clay that swells and shrinks with moisture, which can move slabs and crack buried pipes.
