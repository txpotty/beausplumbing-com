    Kitchen Sink Installation in Houma

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. Each one needs its own support, and a cast iron farmhouse sink can weigh well over a hundred pounds before it holds any water, so the cabinet often needs reinforcement first. The cabinet gets checked before install day. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Houma, LA

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Mounting the faucet and the pop-up on a sink that is still on sawhorses takes a fraction of the time and gets a tighter seal than working upside down inside the cabinet. That does not work on undermounts. Those get plumbed from below.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Houma

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. The silicone bead under the rim reaches handling strength in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries the weight of a full basin of water and a pot leaning on the edge. We brace it and come back. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Houma, LA

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. An undermount on new stone costs more because of clips, cure time and a second trip to connect the plumbing, and a vessel sink adds a new drain and often a taller or wall-mounted faucet. Relocating the drain is the biggest add. We price each piece on the quote.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Houma

    A cracked bowl cannot be patched for long. When a bowl cracks or a drain rusts through, we cap the lines first so the house keeps water, then set a new sink. Stock sizes are often same-day. Odd cutouts take longer.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Houma

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. The bowl hangs on clips with a full bead of silicone, and it stays braced overnight while the adhesive cures. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Houma, LA

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Houma

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. Getting an undermount centered under a polished cutout so the reveal is even on all sides takes a helper, a few shims and patience before the clips get tightened. A crooked reveal never goes away. We check it from both ends of the counter.
