    Frequently Asked Questions About Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Houma

    Point-of-use units usually cost $500 to $1,500 installed. Whole-home electric tankless installs often run $1,500 to $4,500, and a panel upgrade can add another $2,000 to $4,000 on top.

    Four to eight hours covers most standard installs. Bigger electrical or plumbing changes take longer.
