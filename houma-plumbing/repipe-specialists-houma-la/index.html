    Efficient PEX Repiping in Houma, LA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in Houma, LA. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Houma

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Much of Louisiana sits on soft, expansive clay that swells and shrinks with moisture, which can shift slabs and crack buried pipes. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Drain Line Repiping in Houma

    While often overlooked, your home's drain lines are just as crucial as your supply lines. Old cast iron or corroded drain pipes can lead to frequent clogs, slow drainage, and even sewage backups. Beau's Plumbing provides complete drain line repiping in Houma, replacing failing waste pipes with new, smooth PVC or ABS lines.
