    From Spouts to In-Wall Valves in Houma, LA

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Houma

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. That cartridge wears out and causes drips or a handle that will not reach full hot, and swapping it with a cartridge puller takes about an hour when the valve body is in good shape. Moentrol valves use a different part. We carry both.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Houma, LA

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. We quote both options. Most people choose repair first.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Houma, LA

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. They adjust flow when a toilet flushes so the mix holds, and thermostatic valves hold an exact temperature. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Houma

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. Single-handle drips mean worn cartridge seals, and multi-handle drips mean worn washers and seats. New internals end the drip. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Tub Spout Replacement in Houma

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts slide over a 1/2 inch copper stub and lock with a set screw underneath, while threaded spouts screw onto a 1/2 inch pipe nipple, and putting the wrong type on causes leaks behind the wall. We check yours before buying. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Houma

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. Single-handle valves can often be updated from the front, while three-handle valves usually get cut out and replaced. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Houma

    Repeated repairs add up. A valve on its third cartridge in five years is probably worn inside, so one replacement beats more repairs. We track that on your invoice. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Houma, LA

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. On a single-handle setup the diverter is usually the lift gate in the spout itself, and on a three-handle faucet it is the middle stem, and both wear out until water splits between the spout and the head. A new diverter spout takes minutes. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Finding Tub Faucet Replacement Near Me in Houma, LA

    Local plumbers know the local valves. Houses built by the same builder tend to have the same tub valve brand and model, so we often know which cartridge fits before we pull the handle off. That saves a trip.

    Tile matters to us as much as to you. We know how to get to a valve from a closet or bedroom wall, and when we must cut tile, we keep the hole small enough for a trim plate or access panel to cover. Most jobs never need new tile.
