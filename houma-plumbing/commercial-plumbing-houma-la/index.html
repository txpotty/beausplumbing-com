    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Houma, LA

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. Once leaks start showing up in different spots every few months, a sectional repipe in Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX usually costs less than the next three emergency calls. We map the system first. Then we phase the work by floor or by tenant so the building stays open.

    Grease Trap and Interceptor Repair in Houma, LA

    Food service sites need a grease trap or interceptor to keep fats, oils and grease out of the public sewer, and pretreatment inspectors check them. Odor is the first warning. Our work includes baffle and outlet tee repair, lid replacement, inlet line jetting and a sizing check against your fixtures and flow. Slow kitchen sinks often mean the trap is full or the baffles are gone. We remind you to keep the pump-out log current for the inspector.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Houma, LA

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. Our crew checks the baffles and tees, replaces what is broken and jets the feed lines to strip hardened grease a cable cannot touch. A jetted line holds up much better on a busy night.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Houma

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. Our certified testers check reduced pressure zone assemblies, double check valves and pressure vacuum breakers with calibrated differential gauges, then file the report with your water provider. Failed devices usually need a rebuild kit, not a new valve. That is why the truck carries Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits.

    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in Houma, LA

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. Our techs work on AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial heaters and check flue draft, gas valves and the T and P valve each visit. For a replacement, we size from fixture count and peak demand. That is how a busy kitchen stops running out of hot water during the dinner push.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Houma

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. Our tester opens each test cock in order, records the check valve and relief valve readings on a calibrated gauge and compares them against the pass limits printed on the test form. Passing devices get tagged. The provider gets the report from us, and we keep a copy for your next test.
