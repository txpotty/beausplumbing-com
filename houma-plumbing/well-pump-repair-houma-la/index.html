    Jet Pump System Repair in Houma, LA

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. Beau's Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. Nonstop running without pressure usually means lost prime or an air leak.
