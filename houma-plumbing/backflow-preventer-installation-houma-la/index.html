    For high-hazard applications in Houma, LA where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is usually required.     Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Houma

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Houma, LA

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested easily. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.
