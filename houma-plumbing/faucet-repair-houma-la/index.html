    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Houma

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. We replace what failed and check the rest. Each valve type, from compression to ceramic disc, wears in its own way, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Houma

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Houma, LA

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Some Louisiana groundwater carries iron and sulfur, which can cause rotten egg odors, stains, and buildup in water heaters. The aerator comes off with a key and gets cleaned or replaced, then check the cartridge screens. If every fixture is weak, the problem is elsewhere.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Houma, LA

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Check the cabinet floor. We replace the O-rings and tighten the mounting hardware, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Houma, LA

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Neither one takes long.     Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Houma

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Raised homes with pipes under the floor are the most at risk during a cold snap, since cold air moves freely underneath.
