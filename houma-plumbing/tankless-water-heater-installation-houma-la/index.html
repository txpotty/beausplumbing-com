    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Houma, LA

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Houma

    Propane homes in Houma are good candidates for tankless, using a model built or converted for propane. Propane runs at a higher pressure than natural gas, so the regulator, gas line sizing and the unit's gas valve all have to match, and the propane supplier may need to change the regulator at the tank.     Residential Tankless System Upgrades in Houma

    Upgrading from a tank in Houma begins with your current setup: tank size, vent type, gas line size and where the unit sits. In some homes the old flue gets capped and the new unit vents out a side wall in PVC, which is simpler and opens the old chase. Some homes need more vent pipe. We spell that out on the quote.

    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Houma

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job because the old tank spot is not always the best place. Mounting near the gas meter on a garage or outside wall shortens the gas line and venting, though it can add distance to the bathrooms. Both distances matter. A far bath may need recirc.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Houma, LA

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Houma, LA homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current. Some need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, and a 100-amp panel often cannot carry that on top of the air conditioner and range. Panel capacity gets checked up front.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Houma, LA

    Switching from a tank is more than a swap. A 40-gallon tank burns about 40,000 BTU, but a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the meter line nearly always gets bigger. Venting changes too. We plan both on the quote.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Houma

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. It saves water too.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Houma

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and many models include a built-in recirculation pump. That pump can push hot water toward a far bathroom on a schedule, which shortens the wait at the tap without adding a separate pump under the sink. Navien vents in PVC. There is a condensate line to route as well.
