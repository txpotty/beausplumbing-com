    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Houma

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Houma for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, appropriately sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and improve efficiency. That is key for handling heavy rainfall.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Houma

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Houma, LA

Beau's Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Houma, LA so your entire system functions as it should.     Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Houma, LA

We provide expert pedestal sump pump replacement in Houma, LA, with proper alignment and secure mounting.
