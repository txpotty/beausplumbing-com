    Slab Leak Detection in Houma

    Concrete slab homes have supply lines buried in sand below the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months before anyone notices. Look for warm tile first.     Preventive Leak Inspections in Houma, LA

    Checking for leaks once a year catches the small stuff. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves.     Gas Line Leak Locating in Houma

    You cannot locate a gas leak by smell alone. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, then check each joint with a detector and soap solution. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe often marks a leak that has been there a while. We coordinate with the gas utility.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Houma

    Our trucks carry ground microphones and correlators, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Each tool has a job. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, and tracer gas finds leaks that make no sound at all on plastic pipe.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Houma

    Heat and wind can pull a lot of water out of a pool, which makes it tricky to separate evaporation from a real leak. A bucket test settles it.     Fixture and Appliance Leaks in Houma, LA

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Houma, LA

    On a ground microphone, copper leaks are easy to hear. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. We handle both every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX are the two leaks we find most.
