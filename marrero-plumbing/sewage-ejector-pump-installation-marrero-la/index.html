    Grinder Pump Installation in Marrero

    Grinder pumps chop waste into a fine slurry first, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances.     Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Marrero

    Macerating systems like Saniflo add a bath fixture or two without breaking the concrete floor. It sits behind the toilet, grinds waste and pumps it through a small line, often three-quarter or one inch, to a drain above, which suits a small half bath. Venting still applies.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Marrero

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Marrero

    Digging the pit means cutting the slab and excavating below it, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor.
