    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Marrero, LA House

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Marrero, LA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Marrero

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. The old tank gets drained and hauled off, and the new one goes on a pan. New flex connectors every time. Then we leak test with soap solution and check draft at the hood.

    No Hot Water Calls in Marrero

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. On a tank past ten years, a new heater usually beats a new valve.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Marrero, LA

    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Commercial code is tighter. Sizing comes from peak demand and recovery rate.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Marrero

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Louisiana plumbers install them for that reason. Parts are easy to get. We follow their venting and clearance specs to the letter, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Marrero

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. With a check or regulator on the main, expanding hot water has nowhere to go and the pressure spikes each heating cycle. Many codes require a thermal expansion tank there. We precharge it to your house pressure.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Marrero, LA

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. We install both. Setup follows the factory manual, including the draft test.

    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Marrero, LA

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Self-cleaning dip tubes help on hard water. Powered anodes outlast standard rods. Your plans for the house help decide it.
