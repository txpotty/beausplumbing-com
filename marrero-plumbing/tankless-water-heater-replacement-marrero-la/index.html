    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Marrero

    The cost for tankless water heater replacement in Marrero, LA typically ranges from $3,000 to $6,000, depending on the unit's brand, capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed changes to gas lines, electrical or venting.     Well-maintained tankless units can last 15 to 20 years. In hard water areas without regular descaling, heat exchangers can fail much sooner. Annual flushing makes the largest difference.

    Beau's Plumbing installs and replaces all major reputable brands of tankless water heaters. Our preferred brands include Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem, as they are known for their reliability, energy efficiency and long-term performance.     A standard tankless water heater replacement, if you are upgrading from an existing tankless unit, typically takes 4 to 8 hours. If you are converting from a traditional tank water heater, the process can take longer, possibly a full day, due to additional plumbing, gas or electrical changes required for proper installation and venting.

    DIY tankless water heater replacement is usually not recommended due to the complexity of gas lines, high-voltage electricity, water connections and venting requirements. Improper installation can lead to dangerous gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning, water damage or electrical hazards. It is best to hire a licensed and experienced plumber like Beau's Plumbing.

    Yes, you can certainly replace a traditional tank water heater with a tankless unit. This is a common upgrade for homeowners looking for better energy efficiency and endless hot water.     Common signs that your tankless water heater needs replacement include repeated error codes, inconsistent water temperature, reduced hot water flow or a complete lack of hot water. If your unit is over 15 to 20 years old and repairs are becoming common and costly, replacement is often the more economical choice.
