    The cost of toilet replacement in Marrero, LA typically ranges from $400 to $800, including the new toilet fixture and labor. This can vary based on the toilet model you choose, any necessary repairs to the flange or subfloor, and the complexity of the installation.

    A like-for-like toilet replacement in the same location generally does not require a permit. If drains are moved or a new bathroom is added, a permit is usually required.

    A standard toilet replacement usually takes our skilled plumbers about 1 to 3 hours to complete. This includes removing the old toilet, preparing the area, and installing the new unit, making sure it is fully sealed and functional.

    Most standard toilet replacements can be completed by a professional plumber in about 2 to 4 hours. This includes the removal of the old unit, preparation of the flange, installation of the new toilet, and testing for leaks. More complex installations might take a bit longer.

    Manufacturers typically warranty parts for 1 to 5 years and porcelain longer. We guarantee our workmanship and will return if a leak is caused by the installation.

    Frequent clogs, cracks in the porcelain, constant repairs, rocking that returns after resetting and very high water use are common signs. Toilets more than 25 years old are often good candidates for replacement.
