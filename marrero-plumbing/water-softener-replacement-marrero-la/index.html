    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Marrero

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. What worked for three people can run out of capacity in a few days with five. We size for the house you have today.

    Softener Loop Installation in Marrero, LA

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Marrero

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Marrero

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. Cracked tanks and stuck safety floats get replaced. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.

    Urgent Softener Problems in Marrero

    Not every softener failure is urgent. Water on the floor from the valve or an overflowing brine tank does count, and those calls get moved up. Bypass it until we arrive.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Marrero, LA

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. They do keep scale from sticking inside pipes and water heaters. They need no drain line. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Marrero

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. We replace timer units with metered valves that regenerate on gallons used. Salt use usually drops. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Marrero, LA

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. It is programmed before we pack up.
