    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each.     Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Marrero

    The cost of PEX pipe installation in Marrero, LA varies with the scope of the project, such as a full repipe or a small repair. In general, homeowners can expect to pay between $3,000 and $10,000 for a whole-home repipe, while smaller projects might range from $500 to $2,000. We provide detailed, clear estimates before starting any work.

    We mainly use high-quality, reputable PEX brands such as Uponor (PEX-A) and Viega (PEX-B), which are known for their durability and reliability. These brands meet or exceed industry standards and are approved for use in Louisiana plumbing applications, providing a long-lasting and dependable plumbing system for your home.

    PEX piping is designed to be highly durable and has an estimated lifespan of 50 years or more. Its resistance to corrosion, scaling and freeze damage contributes to its long-term reliability, making it a smart investment for your Marrero home.

    PEX must be protected from sunlight because UV light breaks down it. Outdoors it needs to be buried, sleeved or completely covered.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can degrade the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should consistently be buried or otherwise shielded.

    PEX is well suited for a wide range of climates because of its flexibility and resistance to freezing. Around Marrero, attic and crawlspace heat can accelerate wear on supply lines, drain pans, and any plastic piping in those spaces. While rigid pipes can burst when water freezes, PEX can expand and contract, greatly lowering the risk of burst pipes during cold snaps. It also performs well in high temperatures, making it a reliable choice year-round in Marrero.

    Properly installed PEX is expected to last several decades. Manufacturers often offer 25 year warranties when their pipe and fittings are used together.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Unlike copper, PEX does not develop pinhole leaks over time due to water chemistry, making it a more durable choice for many Marrero homes. It also cuts down on water hammer noise.
