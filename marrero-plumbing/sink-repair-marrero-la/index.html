    Hair Clog Clearing in Marrero

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. We pull the stopper, strip the hair mat off it, then run a small hand auger through the trap and into the wall arm instead of pouring in drain cleaner. We flush the overflow channel while we are there. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Marrero, LA

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. Either the clevis slipped on the lift rod or hair has wrapped the stopper so it will not seat. Half the time, a cleaning does it. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Marrero

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Strainers lose their putty seal, sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and disposal flanges vibrate loose over the years. The wet part is the one we fix. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Marrero

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. Failed epoxy lets the bowl sag a little, the caulk splits, and counter water runs right into the cabinet. New clips go in and the bowl gets pulled tight. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Marrero, LA

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. Replace the sink only if the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain has rusted into the bowl. We quote both ways if it is a close call. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Marrero, LA

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. In kitchens it is cold grease and food, and in bathrooms it is hair knotted on the stopper and glued with soap scum. A hand auger does most of the work. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Marrero, LA

    Most sinks do not need replacing. A new tailpiece or pop-up assembly costs a fraction of a new sink and installs in an hour, and the bowl and faucet you already like stay right where they are. We match finish where we can. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Marrero, LA

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. The drain comes out and gets resealed. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.
