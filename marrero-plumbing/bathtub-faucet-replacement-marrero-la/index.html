    A spout or trim kit usually costs $150 to $350. Cartridge work runs $175 to $400, and a full valve replacement costs $450 to $1,200 depending on access. One price, before we start.

    Trim and cartridge work usually does not need one. Replacing the valve inside the wall is new plumbing work in many cities and can require a permit and an inspection, especially when the supply pipes are changed or extended to reach the new valve. We pull it when required.

    A slip-on spout is a fair DIY job. In-wall valve work means soldering or crimping in a tight cavity and pressure-testing, and mistakes leak out of sight. Leave valves to a plumber.

    Plan on three to five hours. The wall has to be opened, the old valve cut out, the new valve soldered or crimped in and pressure-tested, and then the trim and any access panel or remodel plate installed. Any tile patch takes longer.
