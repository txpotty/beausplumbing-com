    The cost of water heater replacement in Slidell, LA usually ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation.     Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, and hard water and sediment buildup can shorten that. Some Louisiana groundwater carries iron and sulfur, which can cause sulfur smells, stains, and buildup in water heaters. Annual flushing and anode rod checks can help extend that life.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and parts, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. Beau's Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, giving you added peace of mind. Specific warranty details vary by brand and model.

    While it might seem tempting, water heater replacement involves gas lines, high voltage electricity, water pressure and heavy equipment.     Generally, if your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down or has a major issue like a leaking tank, replacement is often more cost-effective in the long run.
