    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Slidell, LA

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Slidell

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. A horizontal crack often signals soil pressure pushing the wall in, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Vertical cracks are usually a one-visit job.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Slidell

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. The bad fitting or section of pipe gets replaced. Wet insulation comes out, and fans dry the cavity before we close it up.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Slidell, LA

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Louisiana has many older homes with cast iron drains and galvanized steel supply pipes that corrode faster in warm, wet conditions. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. We repoint failed mortar joints before sealing.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Slidell

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin starting at the lowest port and working up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane stays flexible if the wall moves a little, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Slidell, LA

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Around Slidell, attic and crawlspace heat can speed up wear on supply lines, drain pans, and any plastic piping in those spaces. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Slidell

    Louisiana has many older homes with cast iron drains and galvanized steel supply pipes that corrode faster in warm, wet conditions.
