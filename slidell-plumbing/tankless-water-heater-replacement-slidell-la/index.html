    Picking a Tankless for Your Slidell, LA Home

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Slidell

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. Newer Rinnai models usually fit the old mounting pattern. That keeps wall patching small. Venting stays the same, or switches to PVC if you move to a condensing unit.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Slidell, LA

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Plenty of panels do not have room. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Slidell

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Skip that and the new unit wears out first. We also set a descaling schedule.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Slidell

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.

    Navien Venting and Install in Slidell

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. We stay inside those limits. Go past them and the warranty suffers.

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Slidell

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We still check sizing against the new model. Old service valves often get replaced too.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Slidell, LA

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. Expect a bigger gas line, a new vent run and a wall-mounted spot for the heater. The old flue gets capped.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Slidell, LA

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. We handle that conversion. Gas and water connections usually line up.
