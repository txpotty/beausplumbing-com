    PEX Pipe Replacement in Slidell, LA

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Slidell

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab, which costs far less than a repipe and avoids breaking the floor. It suits a first-time leak. After multiple leaks, we will show you why a full repipe may cost less over the next ten years.

    Emergency Pipe Failures and Repipes in Slidell

    Sometimes a house reaches the repipe point all at once, with a burst line and two more leaks right behind it. We stop the active leak first, make temporary repairs that get water back on and then plan the full repipe so you are not rushed into it. That gives you time. Your policy may pay for water damage even if not for the new pipe, so we take photos of everything we find.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Slidell, LA

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. Copper commonly costs 20 to 40 percent more than PEX, and some owners prefer it anyway for its long history. Our quotes for either include permit, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Slidell

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. Our crews use Uponor or similar PEX-A, where the pipe end gets expanded and shrinks back over the fitting for a full-diameter connection. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Most homes are repiped with PEX-A in two to three days.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Slidell

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more involved. Our office works with your HOA on approvals and shutoff times, and we take steps to protect the units next door. Building-wide jobs go in phases. For larger buildings, we repipe one stack or wing at a time so residents can stay in place.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Slidell

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Homes are commonly built on slabs or raised pier foundations, and raised homes leave much of the plumbing easier to reach. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    Drain Line Repiping in Slidell

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing. We camera the lines first. New PVC or ABS, sloped and vented properly, replaces the bad sections and drains better than the old pipe.
