    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Slidell, LA

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Plumbing code caps house pressure at 80 psi.     Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Slidell, LA

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorine in the water breaks it down from the inside.     Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Slidell

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? We will usually just fix that spot.     PEX Water Line Installation in Slidell, LA

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Hard freezes are rare in Louisiana, but when one arrives, homes built for heat often have exposed pipes that burst. PEX can swell a little in a freeze and go back to shape, where copper splits. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and keep it covered from sunlight at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Slidell, LA
