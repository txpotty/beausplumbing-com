    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Slidell

    The cost of toilet installation in Slidell, LA usually ranges from $200 to $500, not including the toilet fixture itself. This price can vary based on factors like the type of toilet, any necessary plumbing modifications, or the difficulty of removing an old unit.

    We are experienced in installing all major toilet brands, including Kohler, American Standard, Toto, Delta, Gerber, and many others. Our plumbers are familiar with the specific requirements for each manufacturer to ensure a proper and efficient installation.

    Common signs that you might need a new toilet include frequent clogs, constant running, visible cracks in the bowl or tank, excessive wobbling, or a toilet that is very old and inefficient. If you're constantly repairing it, replacement is often more cost-effective.

    Generally, replacing an existing toilet with a similar model in the same location in Slidell does not require a permit. However, if you are relocating a toilet, adding a new toilet, or making major changes to the plumbing system, a permit may be required. We can advise you on local code requirements.

    A one-piece toilet has the bowl and tank integrated into a single unit, offering a sleek look and simpler cleaning. A two-piece toilet has a separate bowl and tank, which are bolted together during installation. Both types are widely available and perform well.

    TOTO, Kohler and American Standard reliably perform well in flush tests and have good parts availability. Gerber and Mansfield are solid budget options. We help you match the brand and model to your needs.

    The rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, typically 12 inches but sometimes 10 or 14. Buying a toilet with the wrong rough-in means it will not fit or will sit too far from the wall.
