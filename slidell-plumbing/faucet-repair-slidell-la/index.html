    Common Faucet Repairs in Slidell, LA

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Slidell, LA

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. Spouts install two ways. We measure the pipe stub and bring the right style, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Slidell

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Over time the washer hardens and the seat wears rough. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Slidell, LA

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. Each brand uses its own part number. We match it. Swapping a common cartridge like the Moen 1225 takes about half an hour, cleanup included.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Slidell, LA

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Private wells serve many rural homes, and sediment, iron, and bacteria make periodic well testing a good habit. We pull the aerator with a key and clean or replace it, then check the cartridge screens. Low flow everywhere is a different problem.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Slidell

    One drip a second adds up to more than 3,000 gallons a year, roughly the water used in 180 showers. That waste lands on your water bill. The worn washer or cartridge gets tracked down and replace it, then check the cabinet below for swelling or stains from the slow leak.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Slidell

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Pull the stopper first. A new pop-up assembly goes in, matched to your faucet finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Slidell

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. If the discs are scratched, the whole cartridge gets replaced, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Slidell, LA

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Many brands sell the handle on its own. We match the handle to the stem or cartridge and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.
