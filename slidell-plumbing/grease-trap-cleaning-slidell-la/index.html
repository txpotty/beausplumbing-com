    Small hydromechanical traps under sinks fill quickly in busy kitchens and often need cleaning every few weeks. We remove the lid, pump or hand-scoop the grease and solids, and scrape the baffles.     Grease Line Jetting in Slidell

    Grease that escapes the trap often coats the line between the kitchen and the interceptor or the sewer. We use hydro jetting to scour the inside of the pipe and remove hardened grease.
