    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in Monroe

    Switching to waterless is mostly a fixture swap. We remove the flush valve, cap the supply and set the waterless bowl on the same carrier. Some codes want the supply left in place. That allows a future change back.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Monroe

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. Manual valves go on a 3/4 inch line with the control stop tuned and the vacuum breaker at the correct height. Handle side follows the layout. ADA units need the handle within reach.

    Office and Restaurant Urinal Installation in Monroe

    Restaurant restrooms see a lot of traffic in short bursts. A single urinal at a busy bar can flush a few hundred times on a Friday night, so we install fixtures and valves that recover quickly and stand up to rough handling without loosening on the wall. We can fit tamper-resistant valves. Night installs keep you open.

    Commercial Urinal Installation in Monroe

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. We set vitreous china units on floor-mounted carriers, connect them with new outlet gaskets and stainless bolts, and pair them with a Sloan or Zurn flush valve sized to the bowl. Partitions get spaced to code. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Monroe

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Monroe

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Corroded hanger bolts and a flush valve seized to the spud mean we come prepared to cut, and we protect the wall tile while we do it so the new fixture does not sit against broken edges. New gaskets always go in. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Monroe, LA

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. Tankless flushometers deliver a set volume from a 3/4 inch line and recover instantly for the next user. We set the volume to the bowl. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Urinal Upgrades and Water Savings in Monroe, LA

    Older urinals often use 1.5 gallons or more per flush. Replacing them with 0.5 or 0.125 gallon models on the existing carrier, and swapping the flush valve to match, cuts water use sharply in any restroom that sees regular traffic. Ask your water utility about rebates. We provide the model numbers you need.
