    Sump Pump Types We Replace in Monroe, LA

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Monroe, LA

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We replace like for like or upsize. Sewage ejectors and grinder pumps we quote separately.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Monroe

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. A sealed AGM or deep-cycle marine battery in the 75 to 100 amp-hour range will typically handle several hours of intermittent pumping during an outage. We ask how often your pump runs in a storm and size the battery to match. Plan on a new battery every three to five years.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Monroe, LA

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. You will hear one kick on from upstairs. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Monroe, LA

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. If you have a Zoeller now, we can usually match the discharge outlet and switch style so the swap goes quickly. Pump not keeping up? The M98 steps up to half horsepower.

    Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Monroe

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. Both pumps and the alternator get swapped together. We can do the work after hours so your tenants or staff are not walking around an open pit.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Monroe, LA

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. We replace the whole assembly or just the failed side.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Monroe

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. A check valve that leaks lets the discharge column fall back into the pit, so the pump runs again for the same water and wears itself out early. New valve every time. Vertical switches suit tight pits, and tethered floats suit wide ones.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Monroe, LA

    A pit that is filling with the pump silent means you have maybe an hour or two before water crosses the basement floor, depending on how fast it is coming in. Check the breaker and the GFCI outlet first, then lift the lid and see whether the float is stuck. A shop vac or a bucket can hold the level down. Our trucks carry common sizes, so most emergency calls end with a new pump running the same visit.
