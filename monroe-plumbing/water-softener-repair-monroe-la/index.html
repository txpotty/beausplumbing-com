    Valve, Resin and Brine Repair in Monroe, LA

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Monroe, LA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Monroe, LA

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. Mineral tank cracks cannot be fixed.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Monroe

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Monroe

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. Beau's Plumbing removes the old resin and installs fresh, high-capacity media. Capacity comes back right away.

    Emergency Water Softener Repair in Monroe

    A softener stuck in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours or flood a utility room. We handle these calls quickly. The crew in Monroe puts the unit in bypass first, then finds out whether the valve, the timer or the drain line caused it. Most of the time it is a stuck piston or a failed motor that can be replaced on the same visit.

    Control Valve Repair in Monroe

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. Beau's Plumbing technicians rebuild these valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model. Mechanical or electronic, we fix the timing. Common parts are on the truck, so most valve repairs are finished the same day without a second trip to the supply house.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Monroe, LA

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. Beau's Plumbing gives honest advice on both counts. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. We lay out both options.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Monroe, LA

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. Scale returning or a drop in water pressure usually means the conditioning media is spent or the sediment prefilter is clogged. Beau's Plumbing repairs these units in Monroe, LA. The media wears out in a few years.
