    Backflow Preventer Testing in Monroe

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where necessary. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Monroe, LA

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure. If repiping makes sense, we explain PEX and copper options and give a detailed estimate. Repiping can be done in stages to fit your budget. New pipe brings back strong pressure and confidence.

    Preventative Leak Detection in Monroe, LA

    Early leak detection is a key part of our plumbing maintenance. We use modern tools to find hidden leaks in pipes, especially those under slabs or behind walls, which are common in older homes. Newer construction mostly uses PEX and plastic drains, while older homes often mix copper, galvanized, and cast iron. Catching these leaks early prevents serious water damage, mold growth, and wasted water, saving homeowners in Monroe from extensive repairs down the line.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Monroe

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is particularly important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and potential breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Emergency Plumbing Readiness in Monroe, LA

    While maintenance aims to prevent emergencies, being prepared is still wise. Our services include checking for common causes of urgent issues, like exposed pipes that can freeze or early signs of slab leaks. Hard freezes are uncommon in Louisiana, but when one arrives, homes built for heat often have exposed pipes that burst.     Water Softener Maintenance in Monroe

    Water softeners combat hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Monroe

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get replaced. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility first.
