    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Monroe, LA

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. When a homeowner has already bought one, we handle the plumbing, the bypass and the startup flush the manual requires. Skipping the flush causes trouble. Every step gets done.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Monroe, LA

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Softener Loop Installation in Monroe

    No loop? A loop is the plumbing that routes the main line through a softener, and many older homes were built without one. We cut it in for you. Where we can, we keep hose bibs and irrigation on hard water to save salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Monroe, LA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. Beau's Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Monroe

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Every tap gets soft water. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Monroe, LA

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. We install bigger tanks or twin alternating units for businesses, which keeps soft water flowing during regeneration. Equipment lasts longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Monroe

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. We follow Culligan's requirements for the drain, bypass and electrical, and we program the system for your tested hardness so it does not over-regenerate. We handle all of it. A loop saves time.

    Water Softener Replacement in Monroe, LA

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. We remove the old unit and install a new metered model, then check the loop and drain line while everything is apart. Salt use drops. You will notice soft water the same day.
