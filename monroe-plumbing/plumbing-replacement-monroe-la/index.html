    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Replacement in Monroe

    The cost of plumbing replacement in Monroe, LA varies widely based on the type of service. A water heater replacement can range from $1,200 to $3,500, while a sewer line replacement might be $3,000 to $15,000 or more. Whole-home repiping can cost between $4,000 and $15,000. We provide detailed, clear estimates after an on-site assessment.

    Replacing old pipes with PEX or copper offers many benefits. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant and less prone to bursting in freezes, while copper is durable and long-lasting. Both improve water quality, eliminate leaks from old pipes and can noticeably boost water pressure and flow throughout your Monroe home.

    We primarily use PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) and copper piping for water supply lines. For sewer and drain lines, we use durable PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene) pipes, depending on the application and local codes.

    Beau's Plumbing stands behind our plumbing replacement services with comprehensive warranties covering both parts and labor. The warranty period varies by the type of replacement and the manufacturer's warranty on components, but we always give you clear information on your coverage for your peace of mind.

    Most tank water heater replacements take two to four hours. Code upgrades or relocations can take longer.

    Some simple fixture swaps are DIY friendly, but water heaters, well pumps and sewer lines involve gas, electrical and code requirements. A licensed plumber provides safety and compliance.

    While some minor plumbing repairs can be DIY, major replacements like water heaters, sewer lines or repiping should always be handled by licensed professionals. These jobs involve complex systems, potential safety hazards (gas, high water pressure) and strict code compliance. Improper installation can lead to leaks, damage and even severe safety risks, costing more in the long run.

    If you have isolated leaks on newer pipes, a repair might be enough. However, if you have older galvanized, polybutylene or lead pipes, or if leaks are widespread and frequent, replacement is usually recommended. Our plumbers can conduct an inspection, often with camera technology for sewer lines, to provide an accurate assessment and recommendation.
