    Cracked Toilet Bowl Replacement in Monroe

    Most bowl cracks start near the bolt holes or at the base where the porcelain meets the floor, and a crack below the waterline leaks whether you can see it or not.     Commercial Toilet Bowl Replacement in Monroe

    Chipped or Stained Bowl Replacement in Monroe, LA

    Deep chips in the glaze collect bacteria, and hard water rings that have etched the surface will not come out with any cleaner. A new bowl fixes both. We match the color to your tank, which is easy for white and harder for biscuit or bone fixtures that have faded over the years.

    Flange Repair During Bowl Replacement in Monroe, LA

    The flange is the ring that anchors the bowl to the drain, and the only time anyone sees it is when the old bowl comes off. That is the moment to check it. We fix a cracked or sunken flange before the new bowl is set, since a new bowl on a bad flange will rock and leak within months.

    Flange Repair and Toilet Reset in Monroe, LA

    A wobble at the base usually means the bowl has been rocking on a loose or broken flange, and every rock breaks the wax seal a little more. Cast iron flanges rust thin. A stainless repair ring fixes most of them, or replace it, before the new bowl goes down on a fresh seal.

    Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in Monroe

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the tank, and the bowl is matched to that vessel's flush rate, so a bowl-only swap means finding the exact paired model.
