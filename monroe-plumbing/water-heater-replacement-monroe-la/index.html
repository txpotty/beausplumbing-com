    What Goes Into a Water Heater Swap in Monroe, LA

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Monroe

    Standard tank heaters remain the most common type, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. A leak at the base, loud popping or rusty hot water means it is time, and a like-for-like or resized swap usually takes two to four hours. You shower tonight.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Monroe

    Electric water heaters in Monroe usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Hot water is back the same day.

    Bradford White Water Heater Replacement in Monroe

    Bradford White sells through professional plumbers only, so you will not find one at a big-box store. They hold up well, and warranty support runs through the installing plumber, which makes a future claim easy for us to handle. Parts are easy to get.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Monroe, LA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Monroe

    Newer construction mostly uses PEX and plastic drains, while older homes often mix copper, galvanized, and cast iron. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Moving a 50-gallon tank through a pull-down ladder or small opening takes two people, and code wants a pan with a drain line, a platform and a light. We bring the help.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Monroe

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. We price both options.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Monroe

    We use Bradford White a lot on our trucks because the tanks hold up and the parts are easy to find. When replacing one, we match fuel, size and venting, check whether the old warranty still applies, and register the new heater right away. We file the warranty.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Monroe, LA

    Rheem and AO Smith make tanks in nearly every size, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. We install both, and the right pick often comes down to what fits the space, which models carry the warranty length you want and which ones the local supply house keeps on the shelf for fast replacement. We help you compare.
