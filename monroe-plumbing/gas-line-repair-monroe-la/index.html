    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Monroe, LA

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually.     Black Iron Pipe Repair in Monroe, LA

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Monroe

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Much of Louisiana sits on soft, expansive clay that swells and shrinks with moisture, which can move slabs and crack buried pipes. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    CSST Repair in Monroe

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Common damage comes from nails, rodents or lightning, and repairs require cutting out the bad section and using fittings from the same manufacturer. Bonding gets checked on every system. A bonding clamp and conductor tie the CSST to the home's grounding system, following the maker's instructions and code.
