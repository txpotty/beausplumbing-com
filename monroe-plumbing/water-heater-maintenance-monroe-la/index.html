    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Monroe

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for leaks.

    Water Heater Leak Detection and Prevention in Monroe, LA

    Leaks are a common issue that can lead to significant water damage if not caught early. Our maintenance service includes a thorough inspection of all water heater connections, pipes, and the tank itself for any signs of leaks or corrosion. Early detection and repair of small leaks can prevent larger, more damaging problems, protecting your home's floors and interior from water damage.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Monroe

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Monroe, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's heavily depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing costly tank leaks.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Monroe

    Tankless units are particularly sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures steady.

    Maintenance Cost Versus Replacement in Monroe, LA

    A water heater tune-up costs a fraction of a new unit, which can run well over a thousand dollars installed. Regular flushing and anode replacement can help a tank reach the high end of its rated lifespan. Tankless units in particular can last many years with proper descaling. If your heater is near the end of its life or leaking from the tank, we will tell you honestly. We help you weigh repair against replacement with real numbers.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Monroe, LA

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.
