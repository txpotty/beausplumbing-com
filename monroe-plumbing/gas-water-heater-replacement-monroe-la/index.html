    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Monroe, LA House

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Monroe, LA

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. We install both. Setup follows the factory manual, including the draft test.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Monroe

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. When one fails twice on a tank that is already 8 or 9 years old, replacing the whole heater is usually cheaper over time. A dedicated outlet gets confirmed first.

    Bradford White Tank Replacement in Monroe

    Most of the tanks on our trucks are Bradford White, partly because their Defender combustion system and ICON gas valve are simple to service. Model choice follows your hot water use. Then it gets installed to code.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Monroe, LA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Service in Monroe, LA

    Higher BTU inputs on commercial units mean bigger gas lines and larger vent sizes, plus a permit and inspection in most jurisdictions. That is all part of the job. We keep your downtime short.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Monroe, LA

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We size the gas line before installing, since an undersized line cuts recovery.

    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Monroe

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. Basic tanks land near 0.60 UEF. High-efficiency condensing tanks reach 0.80 or more and can qualify for rebates.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Monroe, LA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. We run the vent within the maker's length and elbow limits.
