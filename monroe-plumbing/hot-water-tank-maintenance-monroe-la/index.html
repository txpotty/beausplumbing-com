    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Monroe

    Sediment buildup is a frequent issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, ensuring clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is key for extending the life of your unit.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Monroe, LA

    A annual flush costs a small fraction of a new tank, and a new installed tank often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more. We give you an straight read on whether maintenance is still worth it. If a tank is past 10 to 12 years old, shows rust at the base or leaks from the seams, we will tell you replacement is the smarter spend.     Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Monroe, LA

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition.     Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Monroe, LA

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial protector against corrosion. Some Louisiana groundwater carries iron and sulfur, which can cause rotten egg odors, stains, and buildup in water heaters. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod.
