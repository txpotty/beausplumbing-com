    Under-Sink Grease Trap Setup in Alexandria, LA

    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Alexandria, LA

    Larger restaurants and high-volume kitchens often need an outdoor gravity interceptor. These concrete or polyethylene tanks typically range from several hundred to over 1,500 gallons. We excavate, set the tank on a stable base, connect inlet and outlet piping and install manhole risers. Low-lying areas have a high water table and sinking ground, which can cause sewer lines to sag, crack, or separate over time. We plan excavation equipment around the ground conditions we find on site. A properly installed interceptor can go much longer between pump-outs than an indoor trap.
