    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Alexandria, LA

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It is the most thorough fix and the most disruptive one.     Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Alexandria

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. We camera the tile from the sump inlet to see where it is blocked. A jetter usually opens it. Crushed clay tile has to be dug up and replaced with perforated PVC or corrugated pipe in a sock.

    Waterproofing for Older Louisiana Homes in Alexandria, LA

    Older basements usually went in with block walls and little more than tar on the outside. Homes from the late seventies through the mid nineties may contain gray polybutylene supply pipe, which is prone to failure. Water collects inside hollow block cores, and weep holes at the base let it drain into a perimeter channel. Clay tile drains fail too.

    New Sump Pump Installation in Alexandria

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. We use a cast iron pump in most homes and a 1-1/2 inch discharge with a check valve. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It usually ends outside, well away from the foundation, at a spot your local rules allow.

    Groundwater Pressure Relief in Alexandria

    High groundwater pushes up through the slab as well as in through the walls. Water rising through floor cracks or around a floor drain during wet weather is the usual sign. Under-slab drain pipe tied to a sump lowers the water level beneath the floor. It is not a quick job.
