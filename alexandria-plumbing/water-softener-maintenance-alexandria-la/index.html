    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Alexandria, LA

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their capacity. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for full softening capacity.     Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Alexandria, LA

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use.
