    Grease Trap and Interceptor Repair in Alexandria, LA

    Food service sites need a grease trap or interceptor to keep fats, oils and grease out of the public sewer, and pretreatment inspectors check them. Odor is the first warning. Our work includes baffle and outlet tee repair, lid replacement, inlet line jetting and a sizing check against your fixtures and flow. Slow kitchen sinks often mean the trap is full or the baffles are gone. We remind you to keep the pump-out log current for the inspector.

    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Alexandria

    A broken flushometer in a busy restroom gets noticed by every customer who walks in. We repair and replace Sloan and Zurn flush valves, sensor faucets, wall-hung toilets and carriers. ADA work is part of it too. That includes setting fixtures at the right heights, adding blocking for grab bars and wrapping exposed drain lines under lavatories.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Alexandria

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. We see the most benefit in restaurants, schools and multi-family stacks.

    Multi-Family and Property Management Plumbing in Alexandria

    Apartment complexes need a plumber who can handle many units and quick turns between tenants. We repair leaks, replace water heaters, clear stacks that serve several floors and repipe whole buildings while keeping residents informed about every shutoff. Managers get steady unit pricing. Invoices list each unit number, so your accounting team can bill back to the right account.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Alexandria, LA

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. We inspect interceptors, replace failed baffles and outlet tees and jet the kitchen lines that feed them, which removes hardened grease that a cable just slides past. Regular jetting means fewer backups during the dinner rush.

    Commercial Fixture and Faucet Repair in Alexandria, LA

    Commercial faucets and toilets are built heavy, but they still wear. Cartridges leak. Sensor and metering faucets, wall-hung toilets, mop sinks and compartment sinks are routine for us, with Sloan, Chicago Faucets, Zurn and T and S Brass parts on hand. Low-flow aerators cut water use without slowing hand washing. A typical fixture repair takes an hour or two.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Alexandria, LA

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. Our techs test flue draft, check combustion, measure the temperature at the farthest fixture and flush sediment, which together tell us whether to repair the unit or plan its replacement. Tankless units get a descaling flush. Recirculation pumps on long runs get tested as part of the visit.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Alexandria

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. Most kitchens see the same drain back up every few months. We open the line with a sectional cable, then jet the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so the grease does not just close back up in a week. Trap primers get checked too.
