    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Alexandria

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run.     Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Alexandria, LA

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Sewer gas follows soon after. Bird nests, leaves and ice can block a roof vent, and older galvanized or cast iron vent stacks crack inside the walls where nobody sees them, so we check the vent any time the drains act up but the main line looks clean.     Root Intrusion Removal in Alexandria, LA

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. Cut roots regrow within a year or two. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and tell you whether sealing or replacing those joints makes more sense than cleaning every spring. Mature trees in Alexandria yards make this a common call.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Alexandria

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. Crushed pipe cannot be lined. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. Older homes often need this on at least one section. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Alexandria, LA

    Replacement makes sense when the camera shows failures every few feet. Spot repairs will not keep up.     Commercial Sewer Repair in Alexandria

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Small defects turn into big backups.     Sewer Pipe Relining in Alexandria, LA

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.
