    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Alexandria

    Electric water heaters in Alexandria usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap.     Attic Water Heater Replacement in Alexandria

    Newer construction mostly uses PEX and plastic drains, while older homes often mix copper, galvanized, and cast iron. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Moving a 50-gallon tank through a pull-down ladder or small opening takes two people, and code wants a pan with a drain line, a platform and a light. We bring the help.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Alexandria

    We use Bradford White a lot on our trucks because the tanks hold up and the parts are easy to find.     Gas Water Heater Replacement in Alexandria, LA

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Alexandria, LA homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Every joint gets checked.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Alexandria, LA
