    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Alexandria

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. That keeps it dry and easy to reach, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Alexandria homes. Expect more noise.

    Zoeller's M53 Mighty-Mate is the cast iron pump we install more than any other. It is a 3/10 horsepower unit with a vertical float switch, and it moves about 2,580 gallons an hour at 5 feet of lift, enough for a lot of Alexandria basements. Replacement switches are on every supply shelf.

    Quiet Submersible Pumps for Finished Basements in Alexandria

    Finished basements call for a quiet pump and a sealed lid.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Alexandria, LA

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Heavy downpours can overwhelm drainage systems and cause sewage backups, so backwater valves are a smart upgrade for many homes. A second pump goes into the same pit on its own float switch, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak. Your primary pump still handles the everyday load.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Alexandria

    Rigid PVC carries the water from the pump out through the rim joist and at least 10 feet from the house. We add a spring check valve so the water in the pipe cannot fall back, which cuts motor run time and the loud thunk you hear after every cycle. Hard freezes are uncommon in Louisiana, but when one arrives, homes built for heat often have exposed pipes that burst. Where lines can freeze, we fit a freeze-resistant outlet.

    Sump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Alexandria

    The basin sets the ceiling on what the pump can do. We place it at the lowest point of the floor, bed it in washed stone, and run the discharge line with a slight pitch to the exit point so nothing sits in the pipe in Alexandria. Water should never come back.
