    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Alexandria

    The cost for water heater expansion tank installation in Alexandria typically ranges from $250 to $500, including parts and labor.     Signs of a failing expansion tank include water dripping from the tank's air valve, rust or corrosion on the tank's exterior or a water heater that frequently cycles on and off. You might also notice fluctuating water pressure or a banging sound in your pipes, which points to excessive pressure buildup.

    Most installations take one to two hours. Attic installs or PRV replacements can add an hour or two.

    A waterlogged tank feels heavy and sounds solid when tapped near the top. Water coming out of the air valve when pressed is a sure sign the bladder has failed.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve, check valve or backflow preventer on the main, you probably have a closed system that needs one. Many local codes require it whenever a water heater is installed on a closed system.

    Most standard water heater expansion tank installations can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. More complex installations, or those requiring significant pipe changes, might take a bit longer. Our plumbers work efficiently to minimize disruption to your home.
