    Water Heater Maintenance in Alexandria

    Sewer and Drain Camera Inspection in Alexandria, LA

    A camera shows what the sewer looks like inside. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and grease buildup in the main and branch drains, which matters most in older homes with clay or cast iron lines that have been in the ground for decades.     Backflow Preventer Testing in Alexandria

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Yearly tests are often required, and our certified testers check each valve and file results. We repair failures. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Testing on time avoids fines.

    Repipe Assessments in Alexandria, LA

    Polybutylene and old galvanized pipe leak more. We identify the pipe type, look for signs of failure and tell you whether repiping is urgent or can wait. We quote PEX and copper. Work can be phased.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Alexandria, LA

    Hurricanes and tropical storms are a major concern in Louisiana, bringing heavy rain, floodwater, and power outages that affect plumbing. A sump pump sits idle for months and then has to work hard all at once, so we test the float switch, clean debris out of the pit and confirm the discharge line runs clear to daylight. The check valve gets a look. Battery backups get tested too.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Alexandria

    Tankless units need descaling. Hard water scales the heat exchanger until the unit runs lukewarm or shuts off mid-shower. A small pump circulates descaler. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Scheduled Service for Businesses in Alexandria
