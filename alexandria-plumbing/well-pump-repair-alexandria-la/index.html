    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in Alexandria

    The pressure tank works in conjunction with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team accurately diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We make sure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in Alexandria.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Alexandria, LA

    Many well pump issues stem from electrical problems, including tripped circuit breakers, faulty wiring, or a malfunctioning control box. These can prevent the pump from starting or cause it to run erratically. Our licensed plumbers are also skilled in electrical diagnostics, safely testing circuits, relays, and capacitors to identify and repair electrical faults. We ensure all wiring and connections meet Louisiana electrical codes, providing a safe and reliable power supply to your well pump.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Alexandria, LA

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. Beau's Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Alexandria operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.
