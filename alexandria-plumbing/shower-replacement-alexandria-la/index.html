    Replacing Showers Damaged by Hard Water in Alexandria, LA

    Municipal water in Louisiana comes from rivers and aquifers, and it ranges from soft to moderately hard depending on the source. Scale shows up first as a weak showerhead and a stiff handle. Given time, scale seizes cartridges, pits valve bodies and etches glass beyond cleaning, which is when we replace. Softened water protects the new shower. We can recommend one.

    Prefab Shower Replacement in Alexandria

    Prefab units past their useful life usually show it at the base. Fading, hairline cracks and a soft spot underfoot all mean water has been getting through, and we check the subfloor and the wall framing once the old unit is out. Then the new unit goes in. It goes on mortar with a sealed drain.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Upgrades in Alexandria

    Sometimes the shower only needs its guts replaced. If the pan and walls are sound, we can swap an old valve, diverter and trim without tearing out tile, which fixes drips and temperature swings for a fraction of a full rebuild. The difference is real. Half a day, usually.

    Walk-In Shower Conversion in Alexandria, LA

    Walk-in conversions are one of the most requested bathroom jobs we do. The drain moves to the pan's center, the valve goes up to shower height and the slope keeps water from escaping the entry. We add blocking for grab bars. Accessibility standards are followed on request.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Alexandria

    Old valves waste water and swing temperature. Corroded valves get swapped for pressure-balance or thermostatic models, often from an access panel behind the wall. A bigger trim plate hides the old hole. It can be part of a full replacement or a stand-alone upgrade.

    Shower Replacement Options and Costs in Alexandria, LA

    A valve-only swap costs far less than a full rebuild. That only works if the pan and walls are fine, since a new valve cannot fix a pan leak. Pans usually mean tile removal. We match the option to the shower's condition.

    Aging-in-Place Shower Replacement in Alexandria

    Aging in place often starts with getting rid of the tub. The tub comes out, the drain is adjusted and the valve goes in at the right height with grab bar blocking and a handheld. Thresholds stay low. It is one of the safest changes you can make.
