    Badger Disposal Install in Alexandria

    Most replacement disposals in local kitchens are a Badger. A 1/3 to 3/4 HP Badger handles normal scraps and fits almost any sink base cabinet.     New Construction Disposal Installs in Alexandria

    New builds and gut remodels get the disposal in two trips. At rough-in we set the drain height and confirm a switched outlet is in the sink base. The disposal itself waits. After the sink and faucet are set, we return to hang the unit, tie in the dishwasher and run the full water test.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Alexandria

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. Batch feed only runs with its stopper turned in place, so nobody can reach into a running unit, which some families with small kids prefer and which needs no wall switch at all. We wire each one to match.

    Garbage Disposal Replacement in Alexandria

    Hard Water and Disposals in Alexandria

    Private wells serve many rural homes, and sediment, iron, and bacteria make periodic well testing a good habit. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. Scale makes a disposal noisy. It also drains slower, because mineral crust narrows the tailpiece and the trap, so we look inside the old drain parts before we connect anything new to them. If they are crusted badly, we swap them while we are there.
