    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Alexandria, LA

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic.     Slab Leak Reroutes in Alexandria

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is a good fit when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Slab Leak Detection in Alexandria

    The cost of slab leak detection in Alexandria, LA typically ranges from $250 to $700, depending on the complexity of the leak and the time required for detection. This cost covers the specialized equipment and expertise needed to accurately locate the leak, which is crucial before any repair work can begin.

    Much of Louisiana sits on soft, expansive clay that swells and shrinks with moisture, which can shift slabs and crack buried pipes. Hard water can also corrode copper pipes from the inside. Pipes can wear against concrete or gravel as they expand with hot water, and over time these factors cause pinholes and cracks.

    Slab leaks can cause significant damage including foundation cracks, soil erosion beneath the slab, mold and mildew growth in wall cavities or under flooring, damage to flooring materials and increased humidity levels. Over time, it can lead to structural instability and extensive, costly repairs.

    A water line slab leak involves pressurized fresh water escaping from supply pipes, often resulting in higher water bills, warm spots or the sound of running water. A sewer line slab leak involves wastewater leaking from drain pipes, which typically causes foul odors, slow drains or visible sewage seepage, and is not usually pressurized.

    We stand by the accuracy of our slab leak detection services. While detection itself is a diagnostic service, we make sure our findings are accurate. Any subsequent repairs performed by Beau's Plumbing come with our standard labor and parts warranties, which will be plainly outlined for you.

    Direct repair works well for a single leak on otherwise healthy pipe in an accessible spot. Rerouting is often better under tile or hardwood and avoids future leaks on that line. We help you compare both.

    Reroutes and repipes often require a plumbing permit, while small direct repairs may not. Requirements vary by city and county. We handle permits and inspections when needed.

    Common signs of a slab leak include unusually high water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, visible cracks in the foundation, damp or wet spots on carpets or flooring and a sudden drop in water pressure. Musty odors can also indicate moisture buildup.
