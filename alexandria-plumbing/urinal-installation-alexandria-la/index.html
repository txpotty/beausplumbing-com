    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Alexandria

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Urinal Rough-In for New Restrooms in Alexandria

    Tenant improvements need rough-in before the walls close. We lay out each urinal at 30 inches center to center and 15 inches from the side wall, set the carriers, and run waste, vent and supply so the finish crew can hang fixtures as soon as tile is done. Coordination with the GC is constant. Rough inspection comes first.

    Commercial Urinal Installation in Alexandria

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. Each unit goes on a carrier with new gaskets and stainless bolts, paired with a matched Sloan or Zurn valve. We check partition spacing too. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Alexandria

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. We install Sloan Royal or Zurn AquaFlush valves on a 3/4 inch supply, adjust the control stop for proper flow, and set the vacuum breaker the right distance above the bowl so it protects the drinking water. Handles can go left or right. ADA units need the handle within reach.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Alexandria, LA

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. Reusing a carrier and lines is cheap, while new carriers and pipe runs through finished walls cost more. Touchless valves cost more than manual. We itemize each piece.

    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in Alexandria, LA

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Urine leaves a hard mineral buildup inside the trap and the drain line, especially on waterless units and low-flow fixtures that send less water through, so we slope the line properly and add a cleanout where we can. A proper vent keeps odor out. We tie in within code distance.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Alexandria, LA

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. Tankless flushometers deliver a set volume from a 3/4 inch line and recover instantly for the next user. We set the volume to the bowl. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Residential Urinal Plumbing in Alexandria, LA

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. Expect a 2 inch drain, a built-in trap, a vent and a supply sized to the flush valve. Permit rules apply too. We check that first.
