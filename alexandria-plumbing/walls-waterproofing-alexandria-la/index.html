    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Alexandria, LA

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already passed through the foundation walls. This typically involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Alexandria

    Many homes in Alexandria have crawl spaces, which are very prone to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting stops ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Alexandria, LA

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are especially prone to water intrusion. Homes from the late seventies through the mid nineties may contain gray polybutylene supply pipe, which is prone to failure. Many older foundations may lack adequate exterior drainage or proper sealing. We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions. We respect the existing structure while installing modern, effective waterproofing, with attention to the challenges of older brick and stone foundations.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Alexandria

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. This not only waterproofs the walls but also controls humidity, preventing mold growth and wood rot in the crawl space. Around Alexandria, attic and crawlspace heat can accelerate wear on supply lines, drain pans, and any plastic piping in those spaces. An encapsulated crawl space can noticeably improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home. We use durable, reinforced poly sheeting for walls and floors, sealed at all seams and penetrations.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Alexandria, LA

    Waterproofing a shower or wall costs much less than repeated drywall, cabinet and flooring repairs. Ignoring moisture also invites mold, which can be expensive to remediate. A typical shower wall waterproofing project falls in the low thousands, while exterior block sealing varies with wall size. We give you a written quote so you can compare options clearly.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Alexandria, LA

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry for good.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Alexandria

    Even small cracks in your foundation walls can allow water to seep into your basement or crawl space. Our walls waterproofing service includes foundation crack repair using proven epoxy or polyurethane injection techniques. These materials fill and seal cracks, preventing future water entry and often strengthening the foundation. Addressing cracks early in Alexandria homes is crucial to avoid larger, more costly structural issues.
