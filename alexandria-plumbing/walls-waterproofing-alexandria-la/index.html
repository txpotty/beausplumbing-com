    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Alexandria, LA

    Basement Wall Sealing in Alexandria, LA

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Long, hot summers and heavy humidity are the main stress on plumbing in Louisiana, driving condensation, mold, and overworked water heaters. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Alexandria, LA

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Alexandria

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Long, hot summers and heavy humidity are the main stress on plumbing in Louisiana, driving condensation, mold, and overworked water heaters. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Alexandria

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin from the bottom port up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane stays flexible if the wall moves a little, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Alexandria

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor.
