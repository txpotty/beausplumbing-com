    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Alexandria

    A high water alarm belongs on every ejector system, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. Audible alarms on their own power or Wi-Fi alarms that text you give warning while the pit is still filling, not after waste reaches the floor. A high water table means water has nowhere to go after big storms, which can flood yards, crawlspaces, and septic drain fields. Backup power can help.

    Emergency Ejector Pump Replacement in Alexandria, LA

    A dead ejector pump takes every below-grade fixture out of service until it is fixed. Quit using the basement fixtures and the washer if it feeds the pit, then call (504) 317-6869 and we aim to have a plumber and pump at your door the same day. Replacements are often quick.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Alexandria

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. Grinders run pricier. For a basement bath near the drain, a standard ejector usually works.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Alexandria, LA

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for decades, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. They work well for new systems and for drop-in replacements, since many Zoeller models fit existing pits and discharge piping as is. Floats and switches are stocked locally.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Alexandria, LA

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A smaller laundry tray pump or a compact ejector can handle it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer dumps at once, with a lint filter so fibers do not wrap the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Alexandria

    Macerating systems like Saniflo let you add a toilet and sink without breaking the concrete floor. The unit sits behind the toilet, grinds the waste and pumps it through a small discharge line, often three-quarter inch or one inch, up to the nearest drain, which makes it a good fit for a small basement half bath. Venting still applies.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Alexandria, LA

    Liberty builds single and dual pump packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. Matched packages speed up the install, and Liberty's float design is less likely to snag on the basin wall, a common reason older pumps fail to start. Stuck floats are common.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Alexandria

    Digging the pit means cutting the slab and excavating below it, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Low-lying areas have a high water table and sinking ground, which can cause sewer lines to sag, crack, or separate over time. The basin sits on gravel with backfill around it so rising groundwater cannot float it, and new concrete seals the floor and locks it down. A floating basin breaks pipe.
