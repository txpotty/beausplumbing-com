    Grease Line Jetting in Alexandria

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. The buildup forms rings that are thickest along the bottom. A snake cuts a narrow path through and the line plugs again within weeks, while a jetter with a rotating nozzle peels the grease off the whole pipe wall. A camera confirms it afterward.
