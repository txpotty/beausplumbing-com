    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Mandeville

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Mandeville homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Mandeville, LA

    Sewer lines in Mandeville, LA can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Low-lying areas have a high water table and settling ground, which can cause sewer lines to sag, crack, or separate over time. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also contribute to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Mandeville

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We determine the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Mandeville, LA

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Louisiana homeowners.

    Sectional Point Repair in Mandeville

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are a good fit on otherwise healthy lines.
