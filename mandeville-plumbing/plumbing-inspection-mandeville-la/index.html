    Water Heater Inspections in Mandeville

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Mandeville

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Mandeville, LA

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. The camera starts at a cleanout. Every offset, belly, root intrusion and break shows up with its depth and location, and the recording is yours to keep.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Mandeville, LA

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. Plan on an hour or so. Doing this every year or two adds years to a water heater and keeps the shutoffs working for the day you really need them.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Mandeville, LA

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. We push the camera in from a cleanout, watch for cracks, roots, offsets and low spots and record the whole run to the street or septic tank. We mark problem spots from above. Much of Louisiana sits on soft, expansive clay that swells and shrinks with moisture, which can shift slabs and crack buried pipes.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Mandeville, LA

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Mandeville

    Private wells serve many rural homes, and sediment, iron, and bacterial contamination make periodic well testing a good habit. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters.
