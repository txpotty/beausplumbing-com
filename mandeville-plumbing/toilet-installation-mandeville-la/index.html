    Toilet Brands We Install in Mandeville

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Every brand has its quirks. Some skirted TOTO models use a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler tanks take a gasket that hardware stores rarely stock. We keep those parts on hand.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Mandeville, LA

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Look for the WaterSense label on the box. We steer customers toward models with a MaP score of 800 grams or higher, because a cheap low-flow bowl that needs two flushes saves nothing. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Mandeville

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. An access plate hides the tank. Because the carrier carries all the weight, we anchor its feet to the slab or framing first and check it for plumb before the drywall or tile goes up. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Mandeville

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. That spacing leaves room for a grab bar. Before anything is ordered we measure the drain, because shifting it to meet the ADA centerline means cutting into the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Mandeville, LA

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. It adds up quickly. A family of four swapping one old 3.5 gallon toilet can save thousands of gallons a year, and some water providers offer rebates worth checking. Pick a strong MaP rating.

    Toilet Replacement and Upgrades in Mandeville, LA

    Swapping an old 3.5 gallon toilet for a modern one is the easiest water savings in the house. Pulling the old one takes around twenty minutes. Most of the remaining time goes into cleaning the flange and setting the new bowl on shims so it does not rock on a floor that has settled over the years. We haul the old one away.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Mandeville

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set. Even tightening, alternating sides, matters more than force, since uneven pressure either cracks the china or leaves one bolt weeping. It should not wobble.
