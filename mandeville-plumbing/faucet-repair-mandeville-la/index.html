    What We Fix on Faucets in Mandeville, LA

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Mandeville

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Shut the stops first. We can usually install a new faucet the same day, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Mandeville

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. Anything worn gets replaced, and the rest gets checked. Each valve type, from compression to ceramic disc, wears in its own way, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Mandeville, LA

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Handles are often sold separately. The replacement handle gets matched to both the stem and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Mandeville, LA

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. A diverter costs very little. Touch and touchless faucets add a solenoid and batteries to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Mandeville, LA

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Look at the floor of the sink cabinet. New O-rings go on and the mounting nut gets tightened, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.

    Emergency Faucet Repair in Mandeville

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Close the angle stops under the sink. Should those stops be stuck, the house main shutoff is the backup. We come fast for these.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Mandeville, LA

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. Tightening the adjusting ring under the cap usually stops a handle leak.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Mandeville

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. The seals are cheap. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.
