    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out.     Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Mandeville

    Brand matters less than people think, but parts do not cross over cleanly between Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and Little Giant. Our plumber reads the model number off the label and orders the matching float or seal kit. A submersible Zoeller and a pedestal Wayne need different checks. We know both.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Mandeville, LA

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. That intake gets cleaned, and so does the float mechanism. Any brand can rattle its discharge fittings loose over time. Easy fix.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Mandeville, LA

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. We check the battery with a load tester, not just a voltmeter. Then we unplug the main pump and let the backup empty the pit on its own. Alarms get tested too.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Service in Mandeville

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. We still check the casting for cracks and the float for free travel. We carry the common Zoeller parts on the truck.

    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Mandeville

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. That debris gets pulled into the intake and jams the impeller. Our plumber pumps the basin down, rinses the screen and inspects the liner for cracks that let dirt seep in through the walls. Takes about twenty minutes.

    Float Switch Inspection in Mandeville, LA

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. We watch the float through a full cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Mandeville, LA

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Each start heats the motor windings. Our plumbers replace worn valves with quieter spring-loaded types and check the pipe for cracks and loose couplings out to where it leaves the house. Discharge needs to land far enough from the slab or footings that it does not seep right back into the pit.
