    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Mandeville

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Clearances, maximum vent lengths and relief valve rules differ by brand, and warranty claims get denied when an installer skipped them. Our crews install to spec. That protects your coverage.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Mandeville, LA

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. No venting needed. You get more spots to put it.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Mandeville

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Water heater replacements, repipes, sewer line work, and new fixtures commonly require a permit and an inspection. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Mandeville, LA

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. Our crews set the gas line, the drip leg and the vent connector, then do a draft test at the hood to prove the exhaust is leaving the house. Leak checks follow. We add an expansion tank if code calls for it.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Mandeville

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. Then a draft test. Gas suits busy households.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Mandeville, LA

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Gas tankless models usually need a bigger gas line than the old tank had, plus a dedicated vent run through the wall or roof. Upfront cost is higher. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Mandeville

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. Sizing is based on the first-hour rating on the yellow label, which tells you how much hot water the tank can deliver during a busy hour. Gas or electric both work. We connect the fuel, set the vent and haul the old tank away.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Mandeville

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. A leak lands on drywall. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Two to four hours covers most standard tank installs. Tankless installs or jobs with major piping changes can take four to eight hours, mainly because of the venting and gas line work.
