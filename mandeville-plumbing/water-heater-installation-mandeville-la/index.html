    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Mandeville

    The cost of water heater installation in Mandeville, LA typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any necessary plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your particular needs.

    Rusty water, leaks around the base, rumbling noises and running out of hot water quickly are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement is often wise.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. Beau's Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, giving you peace of mind. We will discuss exact warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    To improve efficiency, make sure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and reduce energy consumption.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts. We also warranty our installation labor.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the right size.

    A standard tank replacement usually takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency.
