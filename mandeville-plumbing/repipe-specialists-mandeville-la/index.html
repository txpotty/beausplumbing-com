    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Mandeville, LA

    Municipal water in Louisiana comes from rivers and aquifers, and it ranges from soft to moderately hard depending on the source. Water in many areas contains minerals that contribute to scale buildup inside pipes over time.     Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Mandeville

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections.
