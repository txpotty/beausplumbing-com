    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Mandeville

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. A single-handle valve often takes new trim and a cartridge from the front, while a three-handle valve usually has to be cut out of the wall and replaced with a modern pressure-balance body. The valve gets identified before we price it. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Mandeville

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. We cut out the old valve, sweat or crimp in a single-handle pressure-balance valve, and cover the old handle holes with a remodel plate so the tile stays intact around it. We usually work from behind the wall. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Hard Water Tub Faucet Replacement in Mandeville

    Municipal water in Louisiana comes from rivers and aquifers, and it ranges from soft to moderately hard depending on the source. Scale packs into cartridges and spout diverters until handles stiffen and the diverter will not lift. Soaking and cleaning buys time on a lightly scaled valve, but once the brass is pitted or the cartridge has fused to the body, replacing the faucet is the only fix that lasts. One cartridge pull tells us.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Mandeville

    Repeated repairs add up. If a faucet has had three cartridges in five years, the valve body is likely worn where the cartridge seals, and replacing the whole valve once costs less than another round of service calls. Your service history helps us decide. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Mandeville

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Each maker uses its own cartridges and trim threads, so a same-brand trim kit usually bolts on without plumbing changes. Mixing brands rarely works. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Same-Day Tub Faucet Replacement in Mandeville, LA

    Some faucet failures cannot wait. Snapped handles and broken spouts get same-day visits when a plumber is free. Close the stops or main while you wait. Leave the tub drain open.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Mandeville

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. The supply lines come up through the floor under the filler, which means cutting the slab or working from a crawlspace or basement, and the filler needs a mounting bracket anchored to the subfloor. Deck-mount fillers sit on the rim. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Tub Faucet Style Upgrades in Mandeville

    Wall-mount tub fillers are a popular upgrade. Swapping a standard spout for a long-reach filler or a handheld shower on a slide bar changes how the tub works, and both usually need a new valve or a diverter added inside the wall. We plan the rough-in first. The trim goes on last.
