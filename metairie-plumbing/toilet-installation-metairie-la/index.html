    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilet Installation in Metairie

    Comfort-height toilets put the seat at about 17 to 19 inches, which is gentler on knees and backs. For accessible bathrooms, we follow ADA guidelines for seat height, centerline distance from the side wall and grab bar clearance. We can install elongated bowls and add blocking for grab bars while the wall is open. These upgrades are popular with homeowners planning to age in place.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Metairie, LA

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, with the tank hidden behind an access plate. They free up floor space and make cleaning easy, but they need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep or a furred-out chase. We install Geberit, Grohe and Kohler carrier systems and set the bowl height to your preference. This work is best done during a remodel when the wall is open.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Metairie, LA

    Remodels often raise the floor with new tile, which can leave the old flange too low. We use flange extenders or replace the flange so the seal compresses correctly. If you are moving the toilet, we can relocate the drain, which on a slab foundation requires cutting and patching concrete. We plan timing so the toilet goes in after tile and grout have cured.
