    Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in Metairie

    Cracked or Damaged Toilet Bowl Replacement in Metairie

    Porcelain that cracks below the waterline leaks every time the bowl refills, and the water usually runs down inside the base where you will not see it for weeks. Even a thin crack will leak. We pull the bowl, check the flange and the subfloor, then set a matching bowl on a fresh seal.

    Brand-Specific Bowl Replacement in Metairie, LA

    Model numbers are stamped inside the tank, usually on the back wall, and sometimes on the back of the bowl near the floor. Send us a photo of it. We order matched bowls from supply houses, and most arrive within a day or two unless the model has been discontinued.

    Flange Repair and Toilet Reset in Metairie, LA

    A wobble at the base usually means the bowl has been rocking on a loose or broken flange, and every rock breaks the wax seal a little more. Cast iron flanges rust thin. We repair the flange with a stainless ring, or replace it, before the new bowl goes down on a fresh seal.

    Cost-Effective Bowl Swaps in Metairie, LA

    Swapping only the bowl saves money when the tank is newer, the fill valve and flush valve are in good shape, and the bowl is still made in your color. White is the easiest to match. Discontinued colors are where bowl-only swaps fall apart, and then a full toilet costs about the same.

    Tank-to-Bowl Leak Repair and Replacement in Metairie

    Drips from the bolts between the tank and bowl usually mean a flattened gasket or rusted bolts, but sometimes the porcelain around a bolt hole has cracked.
