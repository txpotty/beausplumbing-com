    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Metairie, LA

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. Good for rentals and guest baths. Usually a one-day job.

    Shower Pan Installation in Metairie

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. Prefab pans get a level check. Either way, the slope runs to the drain.

    Digital Shower Systems in Metairie, LA

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. The digital valve mounts in an accessible spot, often in a vanity or a closet, and needs low-voltage wiring to the controller plus a dedicated outlet near the valve. An electrician handles the outlet. Our part is the plumbing.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Metairie, LA

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves step up from there, holding an exact temperature and letting you set volume separately, which suits rain heads, handhelds and body sprays. Either one protects against scalding. We help you choose based on how many outlets you plan.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Metairie, LA

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. One head works fine off a standard valve, while multiple outlets need a thermostatic valve, volume controls and larger 3/4-inch supply lines. Layout comes first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Metairie

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Steel and acrylic tubs need a mortar bed or foam under them, and cast iron needs a level ledger, so the tub sits flat and the caulk joint at the wall does not crack. The valve goes in next. Spout and head go at the heights you choose, and the overflow gasket gets a water test.
