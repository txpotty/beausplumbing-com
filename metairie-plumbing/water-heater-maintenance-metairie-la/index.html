    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Metairie, LA

    Gas water heater maintenance involves crucial safety inspections in Metairie, LA. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter. We also clean the burner assembly and inspect the thermocouple. These checks are vital to prevent carbon monoxide exposure and keep your gas-fired appliance running safely.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Metairie

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely and efficiently. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for any leaks.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Metairie

    For electric water heaters, our maintenance service focuses on checking heating elements, thermostats, and electrical connections. We verify proper wiring and test for any short circuits or power problems that could affect performance. Sediment flushing is also performed, just like with gas models, to prevent buildup and protect the lower heating element from burning out early in Metairie.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Metairie, LA

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge where installed.

    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Metairie

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs quieter.

    Maintenance Cost Versus Replacement in Metairie, LA

    A water heater tune-up costs a fraction of a new unit, which can run well over a thousand dollars installed. Regular flushing and anode replacement can help a tank reach the high end of its expected lifespan. Tankless units in particular can last many years with proper descaling. If your heater is near the end of its life or leaking from the tank, we will tell you honestly. We help you weigh repair against replacement with actual numbers.
