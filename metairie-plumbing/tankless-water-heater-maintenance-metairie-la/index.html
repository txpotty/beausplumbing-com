    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Metairie

    Most tankless descaling visits cost $175 to $350, depending on brand and access. Adding an isolation valve kit usually runs $150 to $300 more.

    Typically, a permit is not required for routine tankless water heater maintenance, such as flushing and inspections. However, if major repairs or component replacements that alter the original installation are needed, a plumbing permit from Metairie may be necessary to ensure compliance with local building codes.

    Beau's Plumbing services all major brands of tankless water heaters, including Rinnai, Noritz, Navien, Bosch, State, Rheem and more. Our technicians are trained on the specific maintenance requirements and diagnostics for a wide range of models to ensure expert care for your unit.

    Tankless water heater maintenance focuses mainly on descaling the heat exchanger and checking electronic components for efficiency. Hot water tank maintenance usually involves flushing sediment from the bottom of the tank, checking the anode rod for corrosion and inspecting the pressure relief valve. Both aim to prolong life but address different system specifics.

    Most visits take one to two hours, including the descaling cycle. Installing isolation valves on the same trip adds roughly an hour.

    Most tankless water heater manufacturers recommend annual maintenance to ensure good performance and extend the unit's lifespan. In areas with harder water, more frequent checks, perhaps every 6 to 9 months, might help to prevent excessive scale buildup.

    The descaling process involves shutting off the unit, isolating the water lines and connecting a circulating pump to flush a specialized acidic solution through the heat exchanger. This solution dissolves mineral deposits like calcium and magnesium, which are common in hard water, restoring heat transfer efficiency.

    With regular descaling, many tankless heaters last 15 to 20 years. Neglected units in hard water can fail in less than half that time.
