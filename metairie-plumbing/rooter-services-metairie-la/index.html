    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Metairie

    The cost of rooter services in Metairie, LA can vary widely, typically ranging from $150 to $500 for standard drain snaking, depending on the severity and location of the clog. More complex issues like main sewer line blockages or hydro-jetting can range from $400 to $1,000 or more. We provide upfront pricing after a thorough diagnosis, so you know the cost before any work begins.

    Most standard rooter services for localized clogs can be completed in 1 to 3 hours. More complex issues, such as main sewer line root removal or extensive hydro-jetting, may take longer, potentially 3 to 6 hours. We aim to complete the job efficiently while staying thorough.

    Tree roots are naturally drawn to water and nutrients, which they find in sewer lines through tiny cracks or loose joints. As roots grow, they can expand and completely block the pipe, a frequent issue in Metairie neighborhoods with mature trees and older pipes.

    We generally advise against chemical drain cleaners, especially for stubborn clogs. These products can damage pipes over time, especially older ones common in Metairie's older neighborhoods, and often only provide a temporary fix. Professional rooter services are safer and more effective for a lasting fix.

    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the pipe walls. Many tough lines benefit from both.

    Key signs include very slow drains, gurgling sounds coming from drains or toilets, foul odors from drains, and water backing up in sinks, tubs, or toilets. If multiple fixtures are draining slowly, it often indicates a main sewer line blockage requiring professional rooter service.

    We use top professional-grade equipment, including Spartan Tool and Ridgid rooter machines and hydro-jetters. These brands are known for their power and reliability, allowing us to tackle the toughest blockages without damaging your plumbing system.
