    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Metairie, LA

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested easily. We carry proven brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Metairie, LA

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Hard freezes are rare in Louisiana, but when one arrives, homes built for heat often have exposed pipes that burst. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and repairs.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Metairie

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Metairie

    Residential pressure vacuum breakers usually cost $250 to $700 installed. Double check and RPZ assemblies for larger homes or businesses range from $800 to $3,500 or more depending on size and enclosure needs.

    In Metairie, most backflow preventers must be tested annually by a certified backflow tester to confirm they are working correctly and providing adequate protection. This is a separate service from installation but is crucial for ongoing compliance and safety.

    You likely need a backflow preventer if you have an irrigation system, a fire sprinkler system, a swimming pool or any auxiliary water source that connects to the main water supply. Local regulations in Metairie often require these devices for certain applications to protect public health.

    We commonly install high-quality backflow preventers from trusted brands such as Watts, Wilkins by Zurn, Apollo and Conbraco. Our plumbers are experienced with a wide range of devices and can recommend the best option for your application and budget.

    Most residential installs take two to four hours including testing. Large commercial assemblies can take a full day, especially if piping must be reconfigured.

    Most water providers require testable assemblies to be tested at installation and then every year. Some high hazard facilities may be tested more often.

    Signs of a failing backflow preventer include discolored or foul-smelling water, a noticeable drop in water pressure, visible leaks around the device or water discharging from the relief valve on an RPZ. If you notice any of these, contact us for an inspection in Metairie.
