    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Metairie

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. Never mix the two.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Metairie, LA

    Liberty builds simplex and duplex packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. Matched packages speed up the install, and Liberty's float design is less likely to snag on the basin wall, a common reason older pumps fail to start. Stuck floats are common.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Metairie, LA

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Two pumps, one alarm.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Metairie, LA

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin is the least expensive option, often done in a few hours. A full new system with basin, floor excavation, roof vent, electrical and alarm costs more, but it is usually the only way to add a below-sewer bath besides a macerator. Ask for both numbers.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Metairie

    Liberty ejector pumps are a common pick on our jobs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. A full-port valve gets added.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Metairie, LA

    Upgrading an old ejector does not always mean a new basin. With a sound basin, a new pump, fresh check valve, shutoff and alarm give you most of a new system for far less. We tell you if the basin is done.
