    Shower Unit Replacement in Metairie

    Cracked fiberglass units leak at the base first.     Aging-in-Place Shower Replacement in Metairie

    Aging in place often starts with getting rid of the tub. We remove it, adjust the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height, with blocking for grab bars and a handheld shower on a slide bar so the shower works seated or standing. Thresholds stay low. It makes a real difference.

    Tile Shower Replacement in Metairie, LA

    Grout cracks and failed waterproofing are how tile showers die. Our job is the valve, drain and pan, and we coordinate with the tile installer on the waterproofing membrane. Schluter Kerdi is a common choice. It goes on over the backer board.
