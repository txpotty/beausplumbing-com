    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Metairie, LA

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Metairie, LA

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Mounting the faucet and the pop-up on a sink that is still on sawhorses takes a fraction of the time and gets a tighter seal than working upside down inside the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Metairie

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies.     Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Metairie

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. Silicone holds in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries a full bowl. The bowl stays braced until our return. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.
