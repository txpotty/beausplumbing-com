    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Metairie

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Metairie, LA
