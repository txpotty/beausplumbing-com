    PEX Water Line Replacement in Metairie

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a popular and budget-friendly choice for water line replacement in Metairie.     Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Metairie

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and narrows the pipe. This causes low pressure and rusty water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often noticeable right away.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Metairie, LA

    For homeowners seeking to minimize disruption to their landscaping, trenchless water line replacement offers an effective alternative.     Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Metairie, LA

    Polybutylene pipe was used in some homes built in the 1980s and 1990s. It can become brittle and fail with no warning. Many insurers list it as a risk. We replace polybutylene service lines with modern materials. This reduces the chance of sudden leaks.
