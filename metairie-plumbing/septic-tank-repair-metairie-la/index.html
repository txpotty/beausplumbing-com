    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Metairie

    Old concrete lids crack, and a cracked lid over a 1,000-gallon tank is a real danger to kids and pets. We replace failing lids and add risers that bring access up to the lawn. Future pumping gets cheaper. Every lid we set is screwed down or locked so a child cannot lift it.

    Septic Line Clog Clearing in Metairie

    A slow toilet and a gurgle in the tub usually point to a clog between the house and the tank. We run a camera down the line first, then clear it with a jetter set up for a 4-inch building sewer. Grease and wipes cause most of these, and the fix after clearing depends on which one we pull out. Roots mean a joint has opened.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Metairie, LA

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones corrode from the acid that sewer gas forms and eventually fall off into the tank. Once the outlet baffle is gone, solids head straight for the field. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Repair Costs Compared in Metairie, LA

    Baffle or tee replacement often runs $300 to $900. Replacing an effluent pump, floats included, typically costs $900 to $2,000, and fixing the house-to-tank line costs $800 to $3,000 based on how deep and long it is. Leach field restoration or replacement can cost $5,000 to $20,000 or more. The cheapest fix that actually works is the one we push for.

    Septic Tee Repair in Metairie

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer flows straight into the field lines and seals the trenches. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Effluent Pump Replacement in Metairie, LA

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. A pump that hums but moves no water has a stuck impeller or a bad capacitor. We pull it and test the discharge line before setting a new one. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Septic Pipe Repair in Metairie, LA

    Old homes may still have clay tile or Orangeburg pipe running to the tank, and both fail badly with age. Much of Louisiana sits on soft, expansive clay that swells and shrinks with moisture, which can shift slabs and crack buried pipes. Shifting ground and roots crack the joints and flatten the pipe. We replace the bad run with 4-inch PVC, set on sand bedding with a quarter inch per foot of fall. Short sections take an afternoon.

    Septic Code and Permit Work in Metairie, LA

    New tanks and new fields need a permit from the local health authority in most of the country, along with any change to the system's layout.
