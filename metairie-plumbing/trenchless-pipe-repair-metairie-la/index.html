    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Metairie

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Metairie, LA

    Main water lines can also be replaced with trenchless methods. We can pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through the old path using bursting or a directional boring tool. This avoids digging across lawns, driveways and walkways. The new line is pressure tested before service is restored.

    Sectional Point Repair in Metairie

    When damage is confined to a specific section of pipe, sectional point repair is an ideal trenchless solution. We target only the damaged area, installing a patch or liner to seal the leak or reinforce the pipe. This method is cost-effective and limits the scope of work, which suits isolated issues in Metairie without affecting the entire line.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Metairie

    Not every pipe needs to be lined end to end. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, we can install a short sectional liner. This costs less than a full-length liner and is completed quickly. We verify the rest of the line is sound before recommending a spot liner.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Repair Cost in Metairie, LA

    Trenchless repair can cost more per foot of pipe than open trenching. However, once you add the cost of restoring driveways, patios, landscaping and irrigation, trenchless is often the better value. It is also faster and less disruptive. We compare both methods with real numbers for your home.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Metairie

    CIPP lining is a proven trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is well suited for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Metairie.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Metairie, LA

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing badly damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is well suited for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. Beau's Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Metairie, LA.
