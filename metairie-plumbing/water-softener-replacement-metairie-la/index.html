    Picking the Right Softener for Metairie, LA Homes

    Softener Loop Installation in Metairie, LA

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Metairie

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. We can replace just the valve on a good tank. Or the whole system.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Metairie

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. Metered valves like the Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 count gallons and regenerate only when the resin is close to spent. Most people notice less salt in a few weeks.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Metairie, LA

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Residential Water Softener Replacement in Metairie

    Scale in the water heater is the expensive part of hard water. Scale settles on the bottom of a tank heater and insulates the water from the burner, and tankless heaters clog even faster. A working softener stops that.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Metairie, LA

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. Hardness gets tested on both sides of the unit, and the cheap fixes get tried before we talk replacement. Discontinued valves usually mean replacement.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Metairie, LA

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Low-sodium diets are another reason.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Metairie

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. Old clock-timer units come out and metered valves go in. You will buy less salt. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.
