    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Metairie

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. A conversion often means a larger gas line, new venting, a condensate drain and service valves. It takes most of a day. Our licensed plumbers handle all of it to code.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Metairie

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Most run without electricity. The vent goes through a wall with proper clearances.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Metairie, LA

    Gas tankless units fire at up to 199,000 BTU, far more than a 40,000 BTU tank burner. That load usually calls for a 3/4 inch gas line or larger and a dedicated vent in PVC or stainless steel, depending on whether the unit is condensing. They never run out. Some Louisiana groundwater carries iron and sulfur, which can cause rotten egg odors, stains, and buildup in water heaters. A scale filter may be worth adding.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Metairie

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. We inspect the existing vent. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Metairie, LA

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.
