    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Metairie, LA

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Metairie

    Repeated repairs add up. A valve on its third cartridge in five years is probably worn inside, so one replacement beats more repairs. Your service history helps us decide. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Metairie

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. Single-handle valves can often be updated from the front, while three-handle valves usually get cut out and replaced. The valve gets identified before we price it. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Metairie, LA

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. They adjust flow when a toilet flushes so the mix holds, and thermostatic valves hold an exact temperature. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.

    Tub Spout Replacement in Metairie

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts slide over a 1/2 inch copper stub and lock with a set screw underneath, while threaded spouts screw onto a 1/2 inch pipe nipple, and putting the wrong type on causes leaks behind the wall. The connection gets checked first. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Metairie

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. That cartridge wears out and causes drips or a handle that will not reach full hot, and swapping it with a cartridge puller takes about an hour when the valve body is in good shape. Moentrol valves use a different part. We carry both.
