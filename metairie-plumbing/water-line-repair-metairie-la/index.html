    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Metairie, LA

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a quick spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with real information.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Metairie, LA

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Some Louisiana groundwater carries iron and sulfur, which can cause rotten egg odors, stains, and buildup in water heaters. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and ends repeat leaks.

We make sure all repairs meet current plumbing standards for a reliable, leak-free connection.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Metairie

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a especially challenging issue. Newer construction mostly uses PEX and plastic drains, while older houses often mix copper, galvanized, and cast iron. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak promptly in Metairie is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Metairie

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off fast in an emergency.

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option more and more used in homes across Metairie, LA. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact.
