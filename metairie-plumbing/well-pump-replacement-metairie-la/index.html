    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in Metairie

    Pump and tank work as a pair. An undersized or waterlogged tank forces dozens of extra starts a day, and each start puts heat into the motor windings. We check it on every pump job.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Metairie, LA

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. New pump, primed and tested. Then we watch the gauge through a full cycle.

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Metairie

    Sand kills submersibles early. Grit in a well chews up impellers fast, so we look for it on the old pump and may set the new one higher or add a sand separator. Pump setting depth matters.

    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Metairie, LA

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. A pressure switch with pitted contacts or a plugged sensing tube can stop the pump even though nothing is wrong down the well. The switch itself is a cheap part. We show you the burned contacts.

    Pulling a Submersible Pump in Metairie

    Pulling a submersible means lifting the pump and every length of drop pipe out through the pitless adapter, sometimes hundreds of feet of it. On the way up, we look at the torque arrestor and the splices. Figure on one day.

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Metairie

    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist. Most fail from sand chewing up the impellers or a lightning surge that cooks the windings. We megger the motor before we pull it. That tells us whether the problem is down the hole or up top.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Metairie

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. Short cycling, where the pump starts every few seconds of water use, usually means the tank bladder has failed and the pump is paying for it. We check the air charge with a gauge. The air charge should read 2 psi under the switch cut-in.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Metairie, LA

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. Deep-well jets with a two-pipe setup can reach further, but they work harder. Lost prime is the classic complaint. Usually a leaking foot valve or a cracked suction line lets the water fall back down the well, so we check both before we blame the pump.
