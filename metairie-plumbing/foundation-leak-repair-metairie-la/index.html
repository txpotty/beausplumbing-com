    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Metairie, LA

    Direct repair means cutting into the floor right over the leak. It is often the cheapest fix.     Water Line Reroute in Metairie

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. The floor stays whole. PEX is the usual choice since it flexes around framing and doesn't pit, and drywall openings stay small, mostly at fixture walls, for quick patching.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Metairie, LA

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. Drains carry no pressure. We plug the line and fill it with water to see if the level drops, then run a sewer camera through it to spot the cracked hub, offset joint or root intrusion that is letting water out.
