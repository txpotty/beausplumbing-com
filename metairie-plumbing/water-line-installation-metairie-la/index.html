    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Installation in Metairie

    Type K copper, PEX and HDPE are all accepted choices. HDPE and PEX flex with soil movement and have fewer joints, while copper offers rigidity and puncture resistance.

    In most communities the homeowner owns the line from the meter to the home, while the utility owns the meter and the line on the street side. We confirm responsibility with your provider before work begins.

    Beau's Plumbing offers a solid warranty on our water line installations, covering both parts and labor. The specific terms will be outlined in your project agreement, giving you peace of mind about the longevity and quality of our work.
