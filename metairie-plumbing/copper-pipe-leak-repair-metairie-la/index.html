    Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Metairie

    Corrosion is a natural enemy of copper pipes and is made worse by certain water chemistries. Some Louisiana groundwater carries iron and sulfur, which can cause rotten egg odors, stains, and buildup in water heaters. This can lead to thinning pipe walls and eventual leaks. Our team assesses the extent of corrosion, which may show up as blue-green stains on pipes or fixtures. For localized corrosion, we perform spot repairs, but if corrosion is widespread, we may recommend re-piping sections to prevent recurring issues. We make sure all new connections are properly sealed and protected.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Metairie, LA

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original workmanship, thermal expansion or vibration. A leaking joint often shows a white or green crust around the fitting. We clean, reheat and resolder the joint or replace the fitting entirely if it is corroded. The result is a clean, fully sweated connection that holds pressure.
