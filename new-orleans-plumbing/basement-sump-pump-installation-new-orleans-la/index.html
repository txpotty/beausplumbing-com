    Submersible units sit down in the pit, sealed, with the water around them cooling the motor.     Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in New Orleans

    Figure on roughly $800 to $2,000 for most basement sump pump installs in New Orleans. The low end covers a pump swap in an existing pit, while a new pit cut through the slab, a long discharge run, or a battery backup pushes the total toward the top of that range. You get a written price after we look.

    Most standard installs finish within 4 to 8 hours. Long discharge runs or a new pit can add several hours, and if we need a new circuit from the panel, an electrician may need to come out the same day, which can push the job into the evening. We give a timeline at the visit.

    Once a year is enough for most pumps, ideally before the wet season. We check the float, clean the pit and test the check valve, and we confirm the discharge outlet outside is open, since a clogged outlet makes the pump run hard against a blocked line. It takes under an hour.

    Grinding or rattling during a cycle is the first warning. Other signs are rust on the housing and rapid cycling, along with a pump that does not start during a heavy rain even though the pit is full to the float. After about ten years, plan on replacing it.

    A primary pump does the everyday work. The secondary, usually battery powered, sits beside it as a backup, and it kicks on if the primary fails, loses power, or gets overwhelmed by more water than it can move during a heavy storm. Each pump gets its own check valve.

    Quality cast iron pumps usually carry three to five years of factory coverage. We back our installation labor separately, and we walk you through the exact terms for the model you pick before we install it.

    A 1/3 or 1/2 horsepower pump handles most residential basements. The real answer depends on inflow and lift, so we time the refill with a stopwatch and measure from the pit floor to the discharge exit before picking a model. Bigger is not always better.
