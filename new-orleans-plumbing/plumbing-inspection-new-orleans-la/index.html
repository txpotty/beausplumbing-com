    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in New Orleans, LA

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Some need replacing soon. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in New Orleans, LA

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. Plan on an hour or so. Doing this every year or two adds years to a water heater and keeps the shutoffs working for the day you really need them.

    Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in New Orleans

    Plumbing problems are some of the most expensive surprises after closing. We check supply pipe, record the sewer, date the water heater and test every fixture. Your agent gets the report. Buyers often use the findings to request repairs or credits, and the sewer video by itself can head off a very costly surprise.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in New Orleans, LA

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. We check for old uncoated brass connectors, missing shutoffs, missing sediment traps and corrosion, and we test joints with an electronic detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Older homes are where we find the most issues, usually at appliance connectors that have been there since the house was built.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in New Orleans

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.

    Leak Detection and Assessment in New Orleans, LA

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. We shut everything off, check the meter's leak dial and then follow the leak with acoustic equipment and thermal imaging. Early detection limits the damage. Low-lying areas have a high water table and sinking ground, which can cause sewer lines to sag, crack, or separate over time.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in New Orleans

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. Meter tests, pressure loss checks and a look for damp baseboards come first, and slab homes get acoustic and thermal follow-up. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in New Orleans, LA

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.
