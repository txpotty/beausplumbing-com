    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Replacement in New Orleans

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon gas water heater replacement typically runs $1,800 to $3,200 installed, including haul-away and basic code upgrades. Larger 75 gallon or power vent units usually cost $3,000 to $4,800. We give a firm price before starting.

    If your home has a pressure regulator or backflow device, heated water has nowhere to expand. An expansion tank absorbs that pressure and protects the heater and fixtures.

    Water pooling at the base, rusty hot water, rumbling or popping sounds and a tank over 10 years old are common signs. A leak from the tank itself cannot be repaired.

    Tank warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We add our own labor warranty on the installation.

    Most standard gas water heater replacements in New Orleans can be finished within 3 to 5 hours. More complex installations, such as converting to a different type of unit or significant plumbing changes, may take longer.

    We install all major trusted brands of gas water heaters, including Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White and Noritz. We can help you pick the best brand and model to suit your budget and hot water demands.

    Most gas tanks last 8 to 12 years. Sediment from hard water and extreme garage temperatures can cut that. Annual flushing and anode checks help them last for more years.
