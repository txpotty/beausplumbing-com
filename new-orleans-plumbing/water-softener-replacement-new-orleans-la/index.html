    Private wells serve many rural homes, and sediment, iron, and bacteria make periodic well testing a good habit. Hard water contains dissolved minerals like calcium and magnesium. A working water softener is essential for combating these issues in New Orleans homes.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is usually not required for a direct like-for-like water softener replacement if no significant changes to the existing plumbing are made.     A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had heavy mineral buildup restricting flow.
