    What a Tank Tune-Up Covers in New Orleans, LA

    Anode Rod Replacement in New Orleans, LA

    Water chemistry changes from one street to the next, and so does anode life. Three to five years is typical. A softened water supply can chew through a magnesium rod in two. A new rod costs a fraction of a tank and often adds years.

    Anode Upgrades for Odor in New Orleans, LA

    A rotten egg smell from the hot side only is almost always sulfate-reducing bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. A different rod usually solves it. We put in an aluminum-zinc or powered anode and sanitize the tank. Powered anodes never wear out and draw very little electricity.

    Rheem and Bradford White Tank Service in New Orleans

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith and State make most of the tanks in local garages, and each has quirks a tech should know before opening a drain valve. Some models hide the anode under the top cover. Bradford White often uses a combo anode in the hot outlet nipple. Our trucks carry sockets for each brand.

    Standard Tank Flushing in New Orleans

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. The harder the water, the sooner it matters. We drain, partly refill and drain again until the hose runs clear, sometimes four or five rounds. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Gas and Electric Tank Checks in New Orleans, LA

    Gas and electric tanks share the same steel shell, but the parts that wear out are different. Gas units get a burner, thermocouple and vent check. On electric units we meter both elements and check each thermostat terminal for scorching. Both get a flush.

    Electric Element Check in New Orleans

    Electric tanks have two elements, usually 4,500 watts at 240 volts, and the lower one does most of the work and takes most of the scale. Each element gets an ohm reading. A failed lower element shows up as hot water that runs out halfway through a shower. We replace it with the matching wattage and voltage.

    Commercial Tank Maintenance in New Orleans, LA

    Restaurants, salons, laundromats and small offices run tanks much harder than a household does, often 75 to 100 gallons cycling all day. Scale piles up much faster than at home. Most commercial tanks go on a quarterly or twice-yearly schedule with a dated log on the jacket. Downtime gets scheduled outside business hours.

    Electric Thermostat Service in New Orleans

    Each element on an electric tank has its own thermostat, and the upper one carries a reset button for the high-limit switch. When the reset trips, something caused it. We trace the cause, often a shorted element or a stuck thermostat, before resetting it. Power stays off at the breaker the whole time.
