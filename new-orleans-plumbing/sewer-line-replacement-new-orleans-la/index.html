    Partial Sewer Line Repair in New Orleans, LA

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may fix the problem. We locate the exact spot and depth, excavate a small area and replace that section. This costs far less than replacing the whole lateral.     Trenchless Sewer Line Repair and Replacement in New Orleans, LA

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive option to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining installs a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods significantly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in New Orleans, LA while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in New Orleans

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Replacement in New Orleans

    Sewer line replacement in New Orleans commonly ranges from about $4,000 to $15,000 for a typical residential lateral, and can run higher for long, deep or hard-to-reach lines. Trenchless work is often priced around $80 to $250 per foot based on conditions.

    Modern sewer lines made from materials like PVC or HDPE, when properly installed, can last 50 to 100 years or even longer. This is a big improvement over older materials like cast iron or clay, which typically have a shorter lifespan.

    We usually use PVC for open-trench work and HDPE for pipe bursting. Both resist corrosion and root intrusion much better than clay or cast iron.

    We provide a written workmanship warranty, and the pipe materials carry their own manufacturer coverage. We review the terms with you before work begins.

    The timeline for sewer line replacement depends on the method and scope of work. A trenchless replacement can often be completed in 1 to 3 days, while a traditional excavation might take 3 to 5 days, sometimes longer for large projects or tight access. We will provide an estimated timeline during your consultation.

    With traditional trenching, some disruption to your yard is unavoidable, but we work to minimize it and restore the area as well as possible. If trenchless methods are suitable, yard damage is significantly reduced, often limited to small access points.

    Traditional replacement involves digging a trench to access and replace the entire pipe, which can be more invasive to your yard. Trenchless methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, require minimal digging, often just two access points, preserving your landscaping while installing a new pipe.

    Trenchless methods disturb less of your yard and are great under driveways and trees. Open-trench can be better for fixing grade problems, and may cost less in an open, easy-to-reach yard.
