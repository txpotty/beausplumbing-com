    Emergency Toilet Replacement in New Orleans

    A toilet that cracked overnight and is still seeping into the floor cannot wait for next week, so we bring a standard round and elongated model on emergency calls. Shut the angle stop first.     One-Piece Toilet Replacement in New Orleans, LA

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They cost more and weigh more. A one-piece toilet usually takes two people to carry upstairs, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in New Orleans, LA

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are noticeably louder than gravity toilets. When we replace one, we pick a vessel brand with parts that are easy to get later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in New Orleans

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Replacement parts are on every hardware shelf. If the floor is uneven, we shim it level, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in New Orleans

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. First we find the model number inside the tank. If the bowl is the cracked part, the whole toilet gets replaced, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in New Orleans, LA

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Take the measurement from the wall to the closet bolts, not from the baseboard trim. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. Offset flanges can fix a small mismatch.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in New Orleans

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. We match the new bowl to the existing carrier whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Cracked Toilet Replacement in New Orleans

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. You should replace it. The cracked unit comes out, and we look at the floor under it before setting the new one, since a slow crack leak often soaks the subfloor first.
