    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in New Orleans, LA

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. We let joints set before testing.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in New Orleans

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it is how leaks start.
