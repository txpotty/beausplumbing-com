    Replacing a Leaky Faucet in New Orleans, LA

    A faucet dripping once a second wastes a few thousand gallons a year. If the valve body is pitted or no one makes the cartridge anymore, a new faucet costs less than chasing the drip again. We pull the old one. The new faucet goes on with fresh supplies and gets a full leak check, usually inside an hour.

    Bathroom Faucet Swaps in New Orleans, LA

    Swapping a bathroom faucet usually includes the pop-up drain, because the lift rod connects through the faucet body. The drain flange goes in on plumber's putty, or on silicone for cultured marble and stone that putty can stain. Handles get lined up last. We set them so both close at the same angle.

    Kitchen Faucet Installation in New Orleans

    Pull-down sprayers get yanked a thousand times a year. That is why we clip the hose weight at the right spot on the hose and check that the hose does not snag on the shutoffs or the disposal when it pulls back, which is where most early kitchen leaks start. We connect new braided lines to both stops. Before we pack up, we run both sides for several minutes and check the cabinet floor with a dry paper towel.

    Faucet Upgrades in New Orleans, LA

    Going from a two-handle faucet to a touchless pull-down changes what is under the sink. The control box needs a dry spot on the cabinet wall, and plug-in models need an outlet within reach of the cord. We check the outlet before you buy. Filtered water dispensers add a separate line, usually 1/4-inch tubing to a cartridge under the sink.

    Touchless Faucet Installation in New Orleans

    Touchless faucets run on a solenoid valve. The control box mounts under the sink, up off the cabinet floor where a small leak will not soak it, and it runs on batteries or a plug-in transformer. Scale is hard on solenoids, and we install the inline filter when the maker calls for one. Sensor range gets tested last.

    Commercial Faucet Installation in New Orleans

    Commercial restrooms need faucets that hold up to hundreds of uses a day. We install sensor faucets and wrist-blade handles for hand sinks, plus pre-rinse units in commercial kitchens, all to current accessibility rules. We can come after you close. Your contractor or property manager gets the fixture specs for inspection.

    Pot Filler Installation in New Orleans

    A pot filler needs its own cold line. The new cold line comes up inside the wall behind the cooktop, with an accessible shutoff valve so you can service the filler later. The arm folds flat when not in use. The line is tested under pressure before anyone tiles over it.

    Outdoor Faucet and Hose Bib Installation in New Orleans

    Hose bibs get more abuse than any indoor faucet. Hard freezes are uncommon in Louisiana, but when one arrives, homes built for heat often have exposed pipes that burst. We match the hose bib to that, using a frost-free sillcock with its seat set back inside the heated wall wherever freezing is a real risk. Code calls for a vacuum breaker on every hose bib. We use models with the breaker built into the body.
