    What Goes Into a Tankless Install in New Orleans, LA

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in New Orleans

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and many models include a built-in recirculation pump. That pump can push hot water toward a far bathroom on a schedule, which shortens the wait at the tap without adding a separate pump under the sink. Venting is usually two-inch or three-inch PVC. There is a condensate line to route as well.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in New Orleans, LA

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in New Orleans, LA homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. A whole-home electric unit draws heavy amperage. Some models need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, more than a 100-amp panel can usually spare alongside the range and AC. Panel capacity gets checked up front.

    Residential Tankless System Upgrades in New Orleans

    Upgrading from a tank in New Orleans begins with your current setup: tank size, vent type, gas line size and where the unit sits. In some homes the old flue gets capped and the new unit vents out a side wall in PVC, which is simpler and opens the old chase. Some homes need more vent pipe. The quote says which.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in New Orleans

    Propane homes in New Orleans can run tankless units too, using a model built or converted for propane. Since propane runs at different pressure from natural gas, the regulator, line sizing and gas valve must all match, and the supplier may need to swap the tank regulator. Tank size matters too.

    Rinnai, Navien and Noritz Installation in New Orleans, LA

    We install Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem in New Orleans, LA homes all year long. Rinnai brings range and parts support, Navien offers built-in recirc, Noritz makes compact condensing units for tight spots, and Rheem rounds out the list. Warranty terms vary. We walk you through them.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Installation in New Orleans

    Commercial hot water loads in New Orleans run longer and harder than a house. A busy restaurant may run a dish machine, three hand sinks and a mop sink at once, and the tankless rack has to hold temperature through the whole lunch rush without dropping a degree. We rack units with a common manifold. One can be serviced while the rest run.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in New Orleans

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.
