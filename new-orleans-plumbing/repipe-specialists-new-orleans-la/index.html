    Efficient PEX Repiping in New Orleans, LA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in New Orleans, LA. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in New Orleans

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Much of Louisiana sits on soft, expansive clay that swells and shrinks with moisture, which can shift slabs and crack buried pipes. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in New Orleans

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to four days.
