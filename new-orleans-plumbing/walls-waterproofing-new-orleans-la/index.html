    Shower Wall Waterproofing in New Orleans

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall.     Crawl Space Encapsulation in New Orleans

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. Around New Orleans, attic and crawlspace heat can accelerate wear on supply lines, drain pans, and any plastic piping in those spaces. An encapsulated crawl space can noticeably improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home.
