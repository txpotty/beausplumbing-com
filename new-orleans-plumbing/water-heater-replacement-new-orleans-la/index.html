    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in New Orleans

    The cost of water heater replacement in New Orleans, LA usually ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation. Tankless units can range from $3,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on the model and necessary plumbing modifications. Factors like unit type, capacity, brand and labor complexity influence the final price.

    Leaks at the base, rusty water, rumbling noises, inconsistent hot water and an age over 10 years are typical signs. A leaking tank should be replaced right away.

    Upgrading provides more reliable hot water, improved energy efficiency and lower utility bills, better performance and a reduced risk of leaks or breakdowns. Newer models often have advanced features and longer warranties, improving comfort and safety in your home.

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith water heaters. Each offers reliable performance and a range of warranties.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but often have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. Beau's Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, so your investment is protected.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater annually is recommended in most homes. Municipal water in Louisiana comes from rivers and aquifers, and it ranges from soft to moderately hard depending on the source. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from damaging heating elements.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take more time.
