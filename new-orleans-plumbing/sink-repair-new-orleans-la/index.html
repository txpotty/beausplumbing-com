    Everyday Sink Repairs in New Orleans, LA

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in New Orleans

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Hard freezes are rare in Louisiana, but when one arrives, homes built for heat often have exposed pipes that burst. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. We fix leaks at the hose bib too. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in New Orleans, LA

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. The clevis strap slips on the lift rod, the pivot ball loses its seal, or hair wraps the stopper so tightly it cannot seat on the flange. Half the time, a cleaning does it. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in New Orleans

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Basket strainers loosen and leak around the putty, pull-down sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and the disposal flange works loose from years of vibration. The wet part is the one we fix. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in New Orleans

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount bowls hang from clips epoxied to the stone, and pedestal sinks rely on a wall bracket that may have pulled out of old drywall over the years. The clips get reset and the rim resealed. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Hair Clog Clearing in New Orleans

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. We pull the stopper, strip the hair mat off it, then run a small hand auger through the trap and into the wall arm instead of pouring in drain cleaner. We flush the overflow channel while we are there. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Sink Parts Replacement in New Orleans, LA

    Most sinks do not need replacing. Swapping a tailpiece or pop-up costs far less than a new sink, and you keep the bowl and faucet you already have. Finishes get matched when the part exists. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in New Orleans, LA

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. In kitchens it is cold grease and food, and in bathrooms it is hair knotted on the stopper and glued with soap scum. A hand auger does most of the work. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in New Orleans

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. That points to worn O-rings on the spout or a bad base gasket, and sometimes to a cracked faucet body that cannot be rebuilt. Each faucet style rebuilds its own way. We identify yours before ordering parts.
