    Submersible Sump Pump Service in New Orleans

    Our team is skilled in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in New Orleans due to their quiet operation and compact design. Maintenance involves pulling the pump to inspect the watertight seals, clearing the intake screen and checking the internal components for corrosion or wear. We also verify the electrical connections are secure and free from moisture, preventing electrical hazards and supporting longevity.

    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in New Orleans

    Regular annual maintenance is key for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage. We also test the system's operation by filling the pit with water to ensure it activates correctly and pumps water away efficiently, protecting your New Orleans home from unexpected flooding.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in New Orleans

    Heavy downpours can overwhelm drainage systems and cause sewer backups, so backwater valves are a smart upgrade for many homes. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in New Orleans, LA

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, Beau's Plumbing provides specialized maintenance services in New Orleans, LA. We focus on inspecting the external motor, confirming proper lubrication and checking the impeller for any obstructions. This type of pump, with its motor above the water, requires specific attention to its shaft and bearings to prevent premature wear and support reliable performance during heavy rains.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in New Orleans, LA

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in New Orleans, LA.
