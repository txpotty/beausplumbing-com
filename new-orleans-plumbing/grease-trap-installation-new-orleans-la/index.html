    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in New Orleans

    Inside a hydromechanical interceptor, baffles do the separating while a flow control fitting with an air intake holds incoming water to the rated flow. The air intake has to be vented to code, not left open in the kitchen. Get that wrong and the trap siphons. We also set the unit level within a quarter inch, since a tilted trap short-circuits the baffles and loses capacity.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Setup in New Orleans, LA

    Small cafes and coffee bars often get by with a 10 to 25 GPM hydromechanical trap tucked under the prep sink or beside it. The unit goes on the floor or in a shallow recess, and the flow control fitting goes upstream. Clearance matters most. Your staff needs to lift the lid and clean it weekly, so we leave space to do that without unbolting anything.

    Commercial Kitchen Grease Trap Installs in New Orleans

    Every commercial install starts with a fixture list. We count each sink, floor drain and dish machine that will tie in, then add the gallons per minute each one can dump. That number decides between an indoor unit and an outdoor tank, and where it can go with clean pipe runs to the sewer. Fewer bends, fewer clogs.

    Grease Trap Brands We Install in New Orleans

    Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura cover most of what we set in commercial kitchens, plus precast concrete tanks for the big outdoor interceptors. The lid gaskets and baffles differ from brand to brand. Parts availability matters. We prefer models whose lids and gaskets are stocked nearby, because waiting a week on a part means waiting a week to reopen.

    Restaurant Grease Trap Compliance in New Orleans, LA

    Most sewer authorities run a FOG program with an inspector who opens your trap and checks your pump-out manifests against the schedule they set. Fail that and you can face fines or a surcharge on your sewer bill. We install to pass. That means a sample port where the program wants one and a unit sized to the number their formula gives, not the number the last contractor guessed.

    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in New Orleans

    A gravity interceptor works on time, not machinery. The tank holds water for roughly half an hour, which gives grease time to rise and food solids time to drop. Sizing comes from your fixture units and the retention time your sewer authority wants, and we check that number with them before ordering a tank. No moving parts to fail.

    Food Truck Commissary and Ghost Kitchen Traps in New Orleans, LA

    Commissary kitchens see a dozen trucks dumping wash water in the same two hours, which is a different load from a restaurant spreading it over a long shift. We size for that spike. Shared delivery-only kitchens can have more sinks per square foot than any dining room, and the trap must handle all of them. Each brand's prep sink counts.

    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in New Orleans, LA

    Sizing starts with fixture drain rates and ends with where the trap can physically go. Long runs to the trap let grease cool and coat the line, so we keep the trap as close to the fixtures as we can. Closer is better. For outdoor tanks we measure the hose run from where a pump truck can park.
