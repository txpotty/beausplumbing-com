    Kitchen Sink Installs We Handle in New Orleans, LA

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in New Orleans

    Undermount sinks rely on clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in New Orleans

    Composite sinks outweigh stainless bowls and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. Strainer fit gets checked first. If the included strainer is too short for the sink wall, we swap in one that fits rather than overtightening and risking a chip around the opening.

    Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in New Orleans, LA

    Three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks carry separate health code requirements. Prep sinks usually need an indirect drain to a floor sink with an air gap, while the three-compartment sink often runs through the grease trap. We confirm the rules first. Work is scheduled before or after service so the kitchen does not close.

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in New Orleans, LA

    The rough-in decides where the drain and valves end up long before the cabinets arrive. We usually set the kitchen drain stub around 16 to 20 inches off the floor, then adjust for deep bowls or a disposal so the trap does not end up below the wall outlet. Vents go in now. We work from your sink and faucet specs to avoid moving pipe later.

    Apron-Front Sinks in New Orleans, LA

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. That avoids an uneven reveal. Cast iron versions can top 150 pounds, so we bring two people.

    Undermount Sink Installation in New Orleans, LA

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, usually overnight, before loading it with water. Skipping the cure time leads to sagging. Just an eighth of an inch of sag leaves a gap that catches water and food along the edge.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in New Orleans

    Both bowls of a double sink share one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. We pick the layout around your disposal and dishwasher, since a disposal on one side changes where the tee can go and how much fall each side gets. Heavy-wall fittings resist leaks.

    Workstation Sink Installation in New Orleans, LA

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Faucet reach matters too.
