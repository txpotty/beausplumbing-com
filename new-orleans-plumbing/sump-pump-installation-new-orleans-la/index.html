    Primary Sump Pump Installation in New Orleans

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in New Orleans

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. That saves some labor. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in New Orleans

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. Downtime costs money. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in New Orleans, LA

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    New Sump Pit Installation in New Orleans

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. We backfill around the basin with washed gravel so groundwater has a path in. Then we patch the slab. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    Sump Pump Replacement in New Orleans, LA

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in New Orleans, LA

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. Backup and combo units are available too. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in New Orleans

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Landlords like them. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in New Orleans

    Sump pump installation in New Orleans, LA runs about $800 to $2,500 for most homes. The pump type and whether we dig a new pit move the price most. Backups add to it. We quote it in writing after a site visit.

    Seven to ten years is typical. Cast iron pumps with mechanical switches tend to outlast plastic ones, and a pump that rests longer between cycles lasts longer. Yearly cleaning stretches it.

    Odd noises, rust, constant cycling or a pump that fails to start are the common signs. Any pump past seven years is worth a closer look, even if it seems fine, because the switch is usually the first part to go. An inspection settles it.

    Submersibles are quieter and stronger, so they suit heavy use. A pedestal pump costs less and you can reach the motor without lifting it out, but it makes more noise and moves less water.

    Swapping a pump in an existing pit takes about two to three hours. A new pit can take a full day.

    Have it cleaned and inspected once a year, ideally before the rainy season. The service covers the float switch, the check valve, the pit and a full test of the discharge line to the outside. It catches problems early.

    Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty make up most of what we install. All four have good parts support. The model depends on your pit and lift.

    Discharge should end at least ten feet from the foundation, on ground that slopes away. Most cities do not allow sump water in the sanitary sewer.
