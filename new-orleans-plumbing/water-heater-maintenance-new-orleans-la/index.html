    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Maintenance in New Orleans

    A standard tank water heater flush and inspection in New Orleans usually costs $100 to $250. Tankless descaling typically runs $150 to $300, and anode rod replacement adds about $75 to $200 including the part.

    Common signs include strange noises like rumbling or popping, rusty or discolored hot water, a rotten egg smell, reduced hot water supply, or a clear drop in water pressure. If you observe any of these, it's time for a professional check.

    The anode rod is a metal rod inside your water heater that sacrifices itself to keep the steel tank from corroding. It attracts corrosive elements in the water. Regular inspection and replacement of a depleted anode rod are key for extending the lifespan of your water heater, especially where water quality varies.

    While some basic tasks like flushing the tank might seem DIY-friendly, professional maintenance makes sure all critical components, such as the anode rod, pressure relief valve, and heating elements, are properly inspected. Incorrect DIY methods can damage your unit or void warranties.

    Beau's Plumbing stands behind all our services with a solid workmanship warranty. We make sure our maintenance is performed correctly and effectively. Specific warranty details will be provided when service is complete.

    Many manufacturers require regular maintenance to keep warranties valid, especially for tankless units. We provide service records for your files.

    Tank water heaters commonly last about 8 to 12 years, and tankless units often last longer with regular descaling. Hard water can shorten life without maintenance.

    Most manufacturers and plumbing experts recommend annual maintenance for your water heater. In homes with harder water, flushing every 6 to 12 months can be helpful to prevent sediment buildup.
