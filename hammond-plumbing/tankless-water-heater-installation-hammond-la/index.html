    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Hammond, LA

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Some Louisiana groundwater carries iron and sulfur, which can cause sulfur smells, stains, and buildup in water heaters. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
