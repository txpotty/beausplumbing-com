    Jet Pump Repair in Hammond, LA

    Jet pumps, usually installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Common issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear.     Control Box and Electrical Repair in Hammond, LA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. A high water table means water has nowhere to go after big storms, which can soak yards, crawlspaces, and septic drain fields. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help reduce repeat failures.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Hammond, LA

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps prematurely. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives steadier pressure at the tap.

    Well pump failures can be caused by several factors such as electrical problems (power surges, faulty wiring), mechanical wear and tear on pump components, sediment buildup in the well, or issues with the pressure tank or switch. Age and consistent use also contribute.

    Submersible pumps typically last 8 to 15 years, depending on run time, sand and water quality. Short cycling from a bad pressure tank can shorten that considerably.

    Common signs include no water, inconsistent water pressure, air sputtering from faucets, strange noises coming from the well or pressure tank, or the pump running constantly. If you notice any of these, it's smart to call for a professional inspection.

    Regular maintenance is key. This includes annual inspections, checking the pressure tank's air charge, ensuring proper well cap sealing, and being mindful of your water usage. Avoiding continuous short cycling of the pump can also lengthen its life.
