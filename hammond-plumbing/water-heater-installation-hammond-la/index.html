    Tank and Tankless Water Heaters in Hammond, LA

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Hammond

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. A leak lands on drywall. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Hammond, LA

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Rebates may apply. We help you decide if one fits.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Hammond

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. Then a draft test. Big families do well with gas.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Hammond, LA

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. No venting needed. You get more spots to put it.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Hammond, LA

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most. Each has its own install manual, and following it to the letter is what keeps the tank warranty valid if something fails in year six. We know those manuals well. You can compare warranty length and recovery rate with us before you buy.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Hammond, LA

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Gas tankless models usually need a bigger gas line than the old tank had, plus a dedicated vent run through the wall or roof. Upfront cost is higher. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Hammond, LA

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. Gas tankless installs often run higher because of the bigger gas line and new venting, and attic jobs, relocations or code upgrades add labor on top of that. Permits are included. You also get an estimate of yearly energy cost for each option.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Hammond, LA

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. Heated water then has no way back to the street, so pressure rises and works on the relief valve, supply lines and toilet fill valves. The tank soaks up that extra volume. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.
