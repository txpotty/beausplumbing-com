    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Hammond

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet typically costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout generally runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots will eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a permanent fix.

    Rental machines are available, but they can kink, grab and injure users who are not trained. They can also get stuck or damage older pipe. Professional rooter service is safer and generally more effective.

    Snaking uses an auger to break through and pull out blockages, while hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire inside of the pipe, removing buildup like grease and mineral deposits. Hydro-jetting provides a much more thorough cleaning, often recommended for recurring clogs or main sewer lines.
