    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Hammond

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each.     Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Hammond, LA

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, similar to a breaker panel for water.
