    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Hammond, LA

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Hammond

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Louisiana houses. Common finds include old pipe, stiff shutoffs, tired heaters, slow drains and rooty or sagging sewers. We test each one directly. A corroded fitting or partial blockage found now will not become a ceiling stain or a holiday backup later.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Hammond

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. We test the meter, watch for pressure loss and look for damp baseboards and mildew, and on slab homes we follow up with acoustic and thermal equipment to pinpoint the leak. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.

    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Hammond

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. We document each unit. Water heaters, fixtures and sewer lines get recorded unit by unit, and commercial inspections can add backflow assemblies and grease trap lines, which helps owners budget for maintenance and larger repairs.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Hammond

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Hammond, LA

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. We look at backflow assemblies, grease interceptors, commercial water heaters, flush valves and the building sewer. Records matter for inspectors. Managers use the report to fix problems before an inspector or water provider flags them.

    Plumbing Inspection Costs in Hammond, LA

    A basic plumbing inspection usually costs $150 to $300, and adding a sewer camera usually brings it to $250 to $500. Larger homes are priced by scope.     Preventive Maintenance Checks in Hammond, LA

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. We flush the water heater, exercise the main shutoff and angle stops, check pressure and look for slow drains and weeping fittings. It takes about an hour. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.

    Water Heater Inspections in Hammond

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.
