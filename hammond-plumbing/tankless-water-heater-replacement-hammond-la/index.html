    Picking a Tankless for Your Hammond, LA Home

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Hammond

    Sizing a tankless starts with how many fixtures run at once and how cold the incoming water is, not the square footage of the house. Two showers and a dishwasher at the same time can need 7 or 8 gallons per minute. That number picks the unit. Any gas, water or vent changes the new unit needs get done to current code.

    Emergency Tankless Replacement in Hammond, LA

    A tankless that will not ignite or leaks from the heat exchanger leaves you with no hot water at all. Call (504) 317-6869 any time it quits. Common models are on the truck. If your exact unit is back-ordered, a temporary repair can sometimes hold until it ships.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Hammond, LA

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. We replace failed units without shutting down the rest. Hand sinks and dish stations have to hit the temperatures health code requires.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Hammond

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. They run on a single 20 or 30 amp circuit and work well for a far bathroom or an office sink. Most take under two hours.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Hammond, LA

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Hard freezes are uncommon in Louisiana, but when one arrives, homes built for heat often have exposed pipes that burst. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Hammond, LA

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Across 20 years the gap narrows, because one tankless may outlast two tanks, but your usage decides the payback. You see both quotes.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Hammond, LA

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Hammond

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. Newer Rinnai models usually fit the old mounting pattern. That keeps wall patching small. We match the vent type or convert to PVC on a condensing model.
