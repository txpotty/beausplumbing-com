    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Hammond, LA

    Many older homes in Hammond, LA still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often recommend upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene).     Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Hammond

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe.     Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Hammond
