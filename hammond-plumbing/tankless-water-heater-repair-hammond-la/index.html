    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, and the same number can mean different things on two units.     Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Hammond

    Scale is calcium and magnesium carbonate that drops out of hard water when it heats, and a tankless exchanger heats water fast through narrow passages.     Tankless Flush and Scale Removal in Hammond

    Lukewarm water at high flow and a unit that runs loud are two early signs of scale.
