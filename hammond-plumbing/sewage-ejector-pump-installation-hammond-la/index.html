    Grinder Pump Installation in Hammond

    Grinder pumps chop waste into a fine slurry first, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. They cost more. A standard ejector often covers a basement bath.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Hammond

    Liberty ejector pumps show up on a lot of our installs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. We still add a shutoff.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Hammond, LA

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. We install Zoeller for both new basins and drop-in replacements, and its standard models often fit existing pits and discharge piping without changes. Parts are easy to find.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Hammond

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. Never mix the two.

    Emergency Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Hammond

    An ejector failure often starts with the high water alarm, followed by slow drains and a smell near the pit. Shut off water to the basement fixtures, keep everyone from flushing below grade, and call us, since most emergency pump replacements are done in a few hours once the plumber is on site. We carry common pumps.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in Hammond, LA

    The basin holds everything, and a 30-gallon size is common for a single basement bathroom. A larger basin means longer, less frequent cycles that spare the motor and switch, so we go bigger when the pit takes a laundry or several fixtures. Lids must be gas-tight.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Hammond

    Macerating systems like Saniflo let you add a toilet and sink without breaking the concrete floor. It sits behind the toilet, grinds waste and pumps it through a small line, often three-quarter or one inch, to a drain above, which suits a small half bath. Venting still applies.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Hammond, LA

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Two pumps, one alarm.
