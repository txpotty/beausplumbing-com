    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Hammond

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Hammond

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Hammond

    The cost for basement sump pump installation in Hammond, LA usually ranges from $800 to $2,500, depending on the type of pump, pedestal or submersible, whether a new pit needs to be excavated and whether a battery backup is included. We provide itemized estimates after an on-site assessment.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 3 to 5 years on quality cast iron pumps. We also back our installation labor, and we will walk you through the exact coverage for the model you choose.

    Most residential basements need a 1/3 or 1/2 horsepower pump, but the right answer depends on how fast water enters the pit and how high it must be lifted. We measure both before recommending a model.

    A standard basement sump pump installation typically takes 4 to 8 hours. If a new sump pit needs to be excavated, or there are unexpected challenges with plumbing or electrical access, the job might stretch to a full day. Our team works efficiently to limit disruption to your home.

    We regularly install Zoeller, Liberty Pumps and Wayne because they offer cast iron housings, reliable switches and good parts support. We will match the brand and model to your pit size and budget.

    Plumbers in Louisiana are licensed by a state board, so confirming a license before work begins is a simple way to protect your home.     Beau's Plumbing provides a warranty on our labor for basement sump pump installations, typically for one year.
